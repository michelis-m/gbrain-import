Ben Lovejoy confirmed via email that he successfully connected the Qalzy scale via Bluetooth to the iOS app, and confirmed that weight and nutrition data stream directly to his mobile device on the Basic plan.

Ben previously raised concerns that an obligatory subscription would make the device a "non-starter" for 9to5Mac readers. Michael resolved his account setup by ensuring he is on the free Basic tier, preserving full hardware intelligence and food recognition without a subscription.
