## Summary & Findings
- **Investigation Outcome:**
  - [[companies/portless]] Support concluded their internal investigation into why customer order #2736 was shipped despite cancellation.
  - The customer's cancellation arrived in Portless's system approximately 1 minute after the order had already been pushed to the warehouse floor for fulfillment.
  - Portless rules stipulate that cancellations must be registered at least 5 minutes before fulfillment dispatch to prevent warehouse picking.
- **Portless Admission of Error:**
  - Support agent [[people/nela-portless]] acknowledged that Portless support initially gave premature confirmation of cancellation to Michael based on order status alone, without cross-checking the actual fulfillment/warehouse dispatch state.

  - Parcel was fulfilled and dispatched under tracking number `9212290411389202582167`.
  - Because Qalzy already processed the customer refund, customer can refuse delivery at the door to trigger Return to Sender (RTS).
  - Portless noted RTS takes 7 to 60 days to return to warehouse, and returned packages are not automatically restocked or guaranteed for resale.
- **Systemic Prevention:**
  - Portless offered to configure a custom, extended automated order hold window for Qalzy. This hold would buffer newly received Shopify orders before releasing them to warehouse staff, allowing fast customer cancellations or edits to be captured reliably.
