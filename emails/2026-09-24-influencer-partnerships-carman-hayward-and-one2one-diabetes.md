## Overview
Michael Michelis launched a targeted paid micro-influencer outreach campaign across health, fitness, diabetes management, and macro-tracking creators, yielding immediate positive responses and initial partnership agreements.

## Creator Engagements

### 1. Carmen Hayward (`info@carmenhayward.com`)
- **Profile:** Health and fitness creator focusing on women over 40 and data-driven metabolic tracking.
- **Status:** **Agreed to terms.** Requested formal partnership agreement for review.
- **Offer Details:** Complimentary Qalzy scale ($199 value) + $120 flat fee for a dedicated Reel/TikTok featuring honest product feedback + 60-day Meta ad usage rights.
- **Creator Question:** Inquired about the premium app subscription cost and free scan limits (referencing Google Play reviews mentioning 1 free AI scan/day), requesting sufficient app access to test thoroughly.

### 2. Leah — One2One Diabetes (`hello@one2onediabetes.com`)
- **Profile:** Founder of One2One Diabetes and Type 1 Diabetes advocate; training for the NYC Marathon (Nov 1) with the Beyond Type Run team.
- **Status:** **Agreed with counter-offer ($250).**
- **Creator Feedback:** Expressed enthusiastic excitement for Qalzy. Highlighted major chronic pain points with existing AI food apps (inaccurate weight and carb estimations leading to incorrect insulin dosing calculations) versus tedious manual weighing/entry.
- **Counter-Offer:** Accepted collaboration but proposed increasing the flat fee from $150 to **$250** for a Reel demo while extending partnership ad usage rights to **90 days**.

### 3. Fiercely Fueled Nutrition (`admin@fiercelyfueled.com`)
- **Profile:** Pia Jeline Zapata, admin team for Fiercely Fueled Nutrition (strength athlete nutrition).
- **Status:** Open to collaboration, but noted she is based in Cape Town, South Africa (while majority of client base is US/North America).
- **Logistics Constraint:** Michael noted Portless logistics currently cannot ship physical scale units to South Africa, keeping dialogue on hold pending international logistics expansion.
