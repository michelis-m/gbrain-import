# Redo Returns: Inspection Bug Misgrading Factory-Sealed Scales

**Date:** 2026-10-22  
**From:** [[people/sofie-redo]] (`support@redo.com`)  
**To:** [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] (`kostas@qalzy.com`)  
**Company:** Redo, [[companies/qalzy]]  

---

## Overview
Sofie Zane from Redo SLC Warehouse Support confirmed an engineering bug within Redo's warehouse inspection workflow that caused factory-sealed and new condition scales to be erroneously categorized as damaged.

## Details
- **Inspection Bug Identified:** Sofie determined that a software/process flaw in the SLC warehouse inspection flow caused multiple brand-new, factory-sealed Qalzy scales—including return #2231—to be flagged as "Damaged" rather than restocked to inventory.
- **Resolution:** Redo apologized for the error, confirmed the flaw is being corrected, and verified that affected units will be properly reassigned.
