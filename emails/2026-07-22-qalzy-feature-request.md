Thank you for reaching out.
Qalzy database has both verified and unverified foods. Verified foods are marked with a checkmark ✅ and come from a trusted source like USDA or CoFID, so they can't be edited.

You can easily edit unverified by clicking the "Edit" button next to its macros.

You can also create your own foods and submit it to our database from Recipes > My food > Add Food

If you are looking for a faster way to add food, you can also use "Quick Add" when you click on the "+" button to add a food.

Hope this helps. If you can think of anything else that will improve your experience in the app, let us know.
