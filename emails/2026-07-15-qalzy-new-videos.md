Regarding the small screen effect appearing in the videos, this was an
editing approach I came up with during post-production, and I thought it
would be a smart way to highlight the screen content. Unfortunately,
because we're only using a very small portion of the original camera shot,
enlarging it inevitably introduces pixelation. I also tried making the
highlighted rectangle smaller, but due to the limited resolution of that
part of the footage, there's only so much improvement that can be achieved. Had
we planned this approach during filming, the ideal solution would have been
to record a separate close-up of the screen and then sync that footage in
post-production. That would have resulted in a much sharper image. At this
stage, I think we have two realistic options: either keep it as it is or
remove that effect entirely.

Thanks,
Ioanna
