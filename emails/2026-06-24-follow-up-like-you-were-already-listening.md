Follow up while the lead is still warm.

Getting a new lead is good.

Knowing who reached out, what they asked about, and how to follow up is better.

With about.me CRM and Virtual Twin, your profile can notify you when someone shares their contact information through a chat.

Instead of finding out later that someone was interested, you can see the lead while the conversation is still fresh.

Your Virtual Twin starts the conversation. Your CRM captures the lead. You get the context to follow up.

That means you are not starting cold with, "How can I help?"

You can follow up with something specific, like:

"Hi Daniel, I saw you were asking about brand photography for your consulting website. Happy to help."

That kind of follow-up feels human because it is based on what the person actually asked.
