## Key Points & Context
- [[people/leo-schottenstein]] (Partnerships Lead at [[companies/truemed]]) followed up with Kostas after consulting with Truemed's Solutions and Underwriting team regarding Qalzy's legal entity setup.
- Truemed confirmed that Qalzy's current legal structure—having a standalone US Employer Identification Number (EIN) issued to a UK registered company—**cannot be onboarded by Stripe Connect**.
- Stripe's compliance policy strictly requires:
  1. A registered US legal entity (LLC or Corporation).
  2. A physical US business address.
  3. US identity verification for a beneficial owner or manager (US Passport or Social Security Number).
- Leo concluded: *"Without a US entity / business address / identity verification (passport or SSN) in place it's not going to be a possibility at this time. Appreciate your patience and time invested here, but I don't think there's a path here as currently constructed."*

## Strategic Impact for Qalzy
- **Black Friday HSA/FSA Launch Blocked via Truemed:** Qalzy cannot offer native HSA/FSA payments through Truemed for BFCM unless a US entity is formed immediately or an alternative solution is leveraged.
- **US Entity Formation Priority:** Accelerates the strategic necessity of forming a US subsidiary (Delaware C-Corp or Wyoming LLC) via Commenda or doola, resolving both freight customs entry bonds (Trans-Am Singer Nip refusal) and US payment rails.
- **Alternative Pathway:** Michael has scheduled a discovery demo with Redo HSA/FSA for the week of November 9, 2026, which may have different underwriting requirements.
