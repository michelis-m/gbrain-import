**App:** Qalzy: Macro & Calorie Tracker  
**Rating:** ★★★★★ (5 / 5)

## Review Content
> "I wasn't really in the market for a calorie counting app for myself, more for my partner, but I started using it and found it quite addictive, I like knowing and having a record of my consumption. I have had one occasion where I needed customer service and found it to be excellent, my calorie deficit was huge and found that an exercise I had performed that day was incorrect, apparently the exercise (calorie) information is downloaded from elsewhere and requires editing there. I am very impressed"

## Takeaways & Operational Notes
- Highlights high user stickiness and daily habitual engagement ("quite addictive, I like knowing and having a record of my consumption").
- Strong praise for Qalzy customer support responsiveness and resolution quality.
- Note on external exercise sync: The user experienced an issue where third-party synced exercise data created an inaccurate calorie deficit, which was clarified as synced from an external health source requiring edit at the source.
