# Portless Logistics Escalation: Norway Order Tax Debt Collection & Resolution Proposal

**Date:** 2026-10-14  
**From:** [[people/chloe-hu]] (Portless), [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] (Qalzy)  
**Subject:** Re: PBID007771348 - Customer asked to pay tax / URGENT TAX ISSUE  
**Entities:** [[companies/portless]], [[companies/qalzy]]  

---

## Context & Summary
A severe customer logistics issue dating back 2.5 months (to late July 2026) escalated today when a customer in Norway received a debt collection agency notice for unpaid import taxes on order `#PBID007771348`. The issue arose because Portless erroneously dispatched the initial parcel DDU (Delivered Duty Unpaid) instead of DDP (Delivered Duty Paid), and failed to include the customer's phone number, leading to delivery failure and subsequent reshipment.

## Resolution Proposal (Chloe Hu - Portless)
1. **Immediate Debt Halt:** Requested that the customer pay the tax notice (1,098.94 NOK) immediately to prevent further collection agency penalties or legal action.
2. **Full Reimbursement & Goodwill:** Portless committed to crediting the entire tax amount back to Qalzy, as well as covering the cost of sending the customer a complimentary gift or reshipped unit.
3. **Internal Warehouse Safeguards:** Reinforcing strict SOPs at their fulfillment center to block any DDU dispatch to Qalzy customers.

## Qalzy Counter-Response (Kostas Koukoravas)
- Confirmed the tax bill total of **1,098.94 NOK**.
- Demanded that Portless also refund the COGS and delivery fees for the duplicate second shipment (`PBID007771348-1`), as the duplicate dispatch was caused entirely by Portless's DDU error and phone number omission.
- Proposed offering the second parcel to the customer as a gift.
