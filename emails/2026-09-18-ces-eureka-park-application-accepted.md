## Summary
The Consumer Technology Association (CTA) officially accepted Qalzy's qualification to exhibit in Eureka Park at CES 2027 in Las Vegas (January 6–9, 2027).

### Booth Selection & Current Status
- **Package Selected:** [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] confirmed selection of the **Upgraded Package** ($3,300 USD, including electrical connection and high-speed internet).
- **Inventory Allocation:** [[people/sydney-harper]] noted that qualification does not automatically guarantee booth space due to high demand and limited inventory. The Eureka Park selection team is reviewing accepted startups to assign booths.
- **Follow-up:** On 2026-09-18, Kostas followed up with Sydney to pitch Qalzy as an entirely new product category ("world's first AI kitchen scale that identifies, weighs, and tracks food nutrition with one button") and secure confirmed booth placement.
