# Portless Logistics: Orders #2770 and #2771 Released for Separate Parcel Fulfillment

**Date:** 2026-11-13  
**Parties:** Jay from Portless Support <support@portless.com>, [[people/michael-michelis]] <michael@qalzy.com>  
**Subject:** Re: [URGENT] Orders #2770 and #2771  

## Context & Details
- **Order Scenario:** A customer placed two sequential orders within 15 minutes: Order #2770 (for a Qalzy AI food scale) and Order #2771 (for a protective travel case accessory).
- **Merge Proposal & Risks:**
  - Portless proposed merging the orders into a single shipment by cancelling one order in Shopify and appending its line item to the other order.
  - Jay noted that tracking would only attach to the surviving order, leaving the cancelled order marked unfulfilled/cancelled in Shopify, which would trigger automated cancellation notices and customer confusion.
- **Decision & Instructions:**
  - Michael directed Portless not to cancel or merge the orders, opting to ship both packages separately so the customer receives independent tracking numbers for both the scale and the case.
  - Jay confirmed both orders were released from hold and will ship as separate parcels, each with dedicated tracking.
