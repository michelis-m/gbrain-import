## Summary
Portless support provided clarification regarding the operational handling of order PBID007771348 (where the customer was requested to pay import tax and the parcel was returned/held):

1. **Return-to-Origin (RTS):**
   - The parcel is retained at destination carrier facilities for **one month**.
   - If it exceeds one month without instruction, it is destroyed.
   - During the one-month window, the unit can be redispatched to a new address or back to origin.
2. **Destruction:**
   - If marked for destruction, it will be destroyed directly at customs.

Portless is awaiting instructions from Qalzy on how to proceed with the held unit.
