# 9to5Mac Review: Ben Lovejoy Subscription Model Clarification

**Date:** 2026-10-15  
**From:** [[people/ben-lovejoy]] (9to5Mac)  
**To:** [[people/michael-michelis]]  
**Subject:** Re: Building the "OURA of nutrition tracking"  
**Entities:** 9to5Mac, [[companies/qalzy]]  

---

## Summary
Ben Lovejoy, European Editor at 9to5Mac, initiated setup of his Qalzy review hardware and raised a critical editorial question regarding whether a paid software subscription is mandatory to use the physical scale. Michael Michelis clarified that the scale operates completely free of recurring charges, and that subscriptions are purely optional add-ons.

## Key Correspondence
- **Reviewer Question:** Ben noted that the onboarding flow prompted a subscription trial, asking whether customers who let the trial expire retain full intelligent features of the hardware. He flagged that mandatory subscription models on hardware are viewed very negatively by 9to5Mac readers and would make a recommendation impossible.
- **Qalzy Clarification:** [[people/michael-michelis]] clarified that users can bypass the subscription screen with one tap ("Connect your scale") and enjoy 100% of the scale's features permanently without paying:
  - Physical weighing, camera auto-recognition, and automated macro/calorie logging are completely free forever.
  - The optional subscription unlocks advanced coaching, the AI Nutrition Advisor, and phone-only camera logging (when away from the scale).
  - Offered to switch Ben's app account to the basic tier so he can experience the baseline non-subscription user experience directly.
- **Review Impact:** Ben acknowledged the clarification (*"people can just let that expire and continue to have full use of the intelligent features of the scale"), resolving the editorial blocker for the upcoming 9to5Mac review.
