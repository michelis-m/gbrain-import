## 2026-06-11 15:54 — implementations@portless.com to kostas@qalzy.com, michael@qalzy.com, shane@portless.com

Hey Kostas,

 Regarding the Factory MID number, no registration is required on the factory's side, and it is not something the factory needs to apply for or create.

 The MID is generated by the U.S. importer or our customs broker using the factory's official legal name and address exactly as shown on the commercial invoice.

 For reference, a MID is generally constructed using:

 • Country code (e.g., China = CN)
 • The first three letters of the first two words of the manufacturer's legal name
 • The largest number in the street address
 • The first three letters of the city name

 Example:

 Manufacturer: ABC Company
 Address: No. 135 Zhongchun Road, Shanghai, China
 MID: CNABCCOM135SHA

 If your supplier does not have a MID, that's completely normal. We simply need their official legal company name and full address, and the MID can be generated from that information.

 Thanks,
