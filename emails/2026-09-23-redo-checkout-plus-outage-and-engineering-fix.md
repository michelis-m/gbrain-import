## Overview
Kostas Koukoravas escalated a critical outage on the Qalzy Shopify store where the Redo **Checkout+** widget failed to function. When customers clicked the Checkout+ toggle to purchase return protection (€4.50 / €6), the cost was not added to the cart or checkout total, and no orders were registered as "covered" in the Redo merchant admin portal.

## Incident Details
- **Customer Impact:** Customers attempting to select free return coverage could not do so, leading to lost coverage fees and customer confusion during checkout.
- **Escalation:** Kostas sent an urgent escalation to Redo Support and Frank Marino at 07:59 UTC / 13:32 UTC detailing that the button was unresponsive in adding the amount to checkout.
- **Vendor Response:** Jake Smedley from Redo Support confirmed the issue and reported at 17:38 UTC that Redo's engineering team is actively working on a code fix to restore Checkout+ functionality in Shopify checkout.
