## Overview
Discussion regarding how Qalzy can repurpose returned/inspected scale inventory from Redo's warehouse to fulfill influencer gifting orders.

## Key Details
- **Redo Capability Constraint:** Frank Marino previously clarified that Redo's built-in customer outlet solution requires high volume (approx. 1,000 damaged returns/month) to maintain unit economics and cannot directly manage ad-hoc influencer shipments.
- **Qalzy Workaround:** Kostas proposed building an independent secondary Shopify outlet website that Qalzy controls.
- **Integration Request:** Kostas requested technical guidance from Frank on how to connect Redo's warehouse inventory feed to the secondary Shopify store so that stock coming into Redo automatically feeds available inventory for influencer order placement.
