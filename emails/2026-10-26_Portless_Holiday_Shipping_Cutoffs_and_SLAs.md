## Overview
Portless released official fulfillment cut-off dates and warehouse service level agreements (SLAs) for the 2026 Christmas and New Year peak shipping period across the US, UK, EU, Canada, and Australia.

---

## Key Deadlines & Fulfillment Guidance

### 1. Operational Peak Season Buffer
- **Extended Handling Time:** High shipping volumes mean orders will require an additional 1–2 business days to process and dispatch from the Shenzhen facility.
- **Recommended Order-By Dates:** Merchant order-by cutoff banners on storefronts and marketing emails should be set 1–2 days ahead of the Portless carrier dispatch deadlines.

### 2. Christmas 2026 Final Ship-Out Dates
- **United States:**
  - Standard Non-Bonded: **December 10, 2026**
  - Standard Bonded: **December 10, 2026**
  - Priority Non-Bonded: **December 14, 2026**
  - Express (DHL / UPS): **December 18, 2026**
- **United Kingdom:**
  - Standard Non-Bonded: **December 15, 2026**
  - Standard Bonded: **December 15, 2026**
  - Priority Non-Bonded: **December 18, 2026**
  - Express (DHL / UPS): **December 18, 2026**
- **European Union, Australia & Canada:**
  - Standard Bonded: **December 8, 2026**
  - Standard Non-Bonded: **December 10, 2026**
  - Priority Non-Bonded: **December 12, 2026**
  - Express (DHL / UPS): **December 18, 2026**

### 3. New Year 2027 Delivery Deadlines
- **United States:** Standard Non-Bonded Dec 20, Standard Bonded Dec 18, Priority Dec 22, Express Dec 24.
- **United Kingdom:** Standard Non-Bonded Dec 15, Standard Bonded Dec 21, Priority Dec 24, Express Dec 24.
- **EU / AU / CA:** Standard Bonded Dec 15, Standard Non-Bonded Dec 16, Priority Dec 18, Express Dec 24.
