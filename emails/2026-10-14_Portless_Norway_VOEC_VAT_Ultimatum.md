## Context & Summary
Michael Michelis issued an urgent compliance ultimatum to Portless Billings regarding five orders shipped to Norway. With Norway VOEC (VAT on E-Commerce) statutory tax returns due today (14 October 2026), Qalzy required immediate confirmation of declared customs values and tax treatment.

## Correspondence Details
- **Ruby (Portless Billings):** Stated that the declared value for all five orders was **$176 USD**, but Portless was uncertain whether this figure was inclusive or exclusive of VAT, noting declared value typically mirrors the retail price received from Qalzy.
- **Michael Michelis:** Escalated to Chloe Hu and Ryan Torreta, noting that this query had been pending since October 1. Demanded definitive clarification whether $176 applies per order and whether it includes VAT.
- **Liability Notice:** Formally placed Portless on notice that any penalties, fines, or excess tax liabilities resulting from missed VOEC reporting deadlines due to Portless's two-week delay would be held as Portless's financial liability.
