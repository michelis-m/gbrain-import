## Overview
Frank Marino and Kostas Koukoravas finalized the reverse-logistics rules and automated SKU transformation for customer returns processed at Redo's Salt Lake City (SLC) warehouse.

## Operational Agreement
- **Grading & SKU Transformation:**
  - *Factory Sealed:* Inspected, approved, and returned directly to active DTC inventory for resale.
  - *Like New:* Transformed to creator/refurbished SKUs (`QZ-01-BLK-REDO`, `QZ-01-WHT-REDO`).
  - *Cosmetic Damage:* Transformed to creator/refurbished SKUs (`QZ-01-BLK-REDO`, `QZ-01-WHT-REDO`).
- **Inventory Access:** The Redo SKUs are live on Shopify but excluded from public sales channels, allowing Qalzy to manually dispatch free sample units to influencers and partner creators.
