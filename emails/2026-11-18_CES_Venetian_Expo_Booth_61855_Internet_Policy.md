# CES 2027: Venetian Expo Booth 61855 Exhibitor Internet Policy

**Date:** 2026-11-18  
**Participants:** [[people/kostas-koukoravas]], [[people/saul-falconi]]  
**Subject:** RE: CES Booth 61855 - internet  

---

## Context
[[people/kostas-koukoravas]] contacted exhibitor services at The Venetian Expo to clarify networking and internet connectivity options for Qalzy's CES 2027 turnkey booth (Booth 61855).

## Key Information & Policies
- **Exhibitor Internet PM:** [[people/saul-falconi]] (Internet Project Manager, Exhibitor Services at The Venetian Resort / Expo).
- **Router Restriction on Standard Lines:**
  - Venetian Expo does not permit exhibitors to connect their own routers to standard shared internet lines due to RF interference and bandwidth management issues in crowded convention environments.
- **Recommended Options:**
  1. Order a designated **wireless internet package** directly through the Venetian Expo exhibitor portal.
  2. Order a **dedicated line** (dedicated Ethernet service) and bring an approved wireless router to connect directly to the dedicated line.
- Saul Falconi attached the official Venetian Expo Wireless Router Policy and provided the ordering portal link for service booking.
