## Overview
A dispute over delivery liability erupted between [[companies/qalzy]] and [[companies/portless]] after USPS incorrectly marked Order PBID007833566-1 as "recipient moved, left no address" and initiated return to sender.

- **Portless Position:** Support representative Maya stated that because Portless printed and shipped to the address provided on file, they will not cover reshipment costs or issue refunds, advising Qalzy to instruct the customer to resolve the matter directly with USPS.

- **Demands:** Michael demanded confirmation that Portless will absorb reshipment expenses, provide real-time tracking on the RTS package, and expedite dispatch of a replacement scale.
