## Information Requested from Portless
1. **Handling & Preparation Fees:** Costs for picking to pallet, palletising, stretch-wrapping, staging, loading, and generating export paperwork.
2. **Palletization Specifications:** Master carton dimensions, units per carton, carton stacking limits, total pallet count, and gross freight weight (baseline assumption: 240 scales per 125 × 106 × 150.2 cm pallet).
3. **Turnaround & Lead Times:** Timeline from formal notice to goods made available for carrier pickup.
4. **Bonded Warehouse Customs Clearance:** Documentation requirements (commercial invoices, packing lists) and whether Portless or the manufacturing factory manages China export customs clearance.
5. **Repacking Requirements:** Confirmation of whether current master cartons can withstand ocean/air bulk freight without additional repacking.
