###🗂️ Company-segmented CRM

Today we're announcing a massively long-overdue addition to the Press Ranger CRM: **you can now segment your CRM by company.**


If you manage more than one company (some of you manage hundreds), your CRM has been one giant board where every client's contacts partied together. Not any more:


- Add journalists, publishers, podcasts, etc. to a company-segmented section of the CRM 
- Change the company on the new dropdown to see only that client's CRM 
- Every card now has an edit button, so you can move individual entries where they belong 
- Works with Claude/ChatGPT/MCP out of the box, so contacts your chatbot adds land in the right client's CRM automatically 
- Existing entries get sorted for you wherever we can tell who they belong to; stragglers wait for you in Uncategorized
