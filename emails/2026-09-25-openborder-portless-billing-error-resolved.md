## Overview
Following extensive investigation by [[people/chelsea-hartanto]] into double-charged duties and tariffs on August 2026 invoices, OpenBorder confirmed that [[companies/portless]] made a significant error in their billing calculations against Qalzy.

## Key Findings & Explanations
- **Portless Billing Error:** Portless mistakenly applied Qalzy's Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) for US orders to the declared value field. As a result, Portless incorrectly classified numerous orders as falling under the *de minimis* threshold when they were actually over *de minimis*.
- **Unauthorized Small Parcel Fee:** Due to this calculation error, Portless erroneously charged Qalzy an extra £3 small parcel fee across multiple orders.
- **Portless Internal Confirmation:** [[people/craig-shaver]] from Portless acknowledged the calculation error to OpenBorder leadership.
- **Invoice Reconciliation:** Chelsea confirmed that OpenBorder's August invoice (Invoice 5091) is correct and requested that Qalzy proceed with payment.
- **Credits & Follow-Up:** Portless is expected to issue a credit note or refund to Qalzy for the wrongly billed parcel fees and tariffs. Chelsea offered to nudge Portless leadership directly to ensure the credit is processed promptly.
