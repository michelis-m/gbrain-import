# Redo Returns: Used Scales Freeze & -REDO SKU Mapping Confirmation

**Date:** 2026-11-19  
**Participants:** [[people/michael-michelis]], [[people/frank-marino]]  
**Thread Subject:** Re: URGENT: Used returns being graded "Factory Sealed" & shipped to new Qalzy customers (#2742, #2744, #2790)  

---

## Context
Following Frank Marino's response detailing root causes for three DTC orders (#2742, #2744, #2790) being fulfilled with returned units from Redo's SLC warehouse, Michael provided final operational direction on SKU remapping and grading protocols.

## Key Updates & Details
- **Final SKU Remapping Instruction:**
  - Michael instructed Redo to ensure that **all returned units are mapped exclusively to `-REDO` SKUs** (`QZ-01-WHT-REDO` and `QZ-01-BLK-REDO`).
  - This ensures that under no circumstances will returned hardware be restocked into primary DTC sellable bins (`QZ-01-WHT` / `QZ-01-BLK`).
- **Factory Sealed Internal Grading:**
  - Michael confirmed that warehouse technicians may continue tagging true unopened units as "Factory Sealed" in Redo's internal system if desired, but they must still route to holding / `-REDO` inventory rather than immediate automatic DTC restock.
  - Michael noted that based on historical return behavior, genuinely unopened factory-sealed returns are rare.
