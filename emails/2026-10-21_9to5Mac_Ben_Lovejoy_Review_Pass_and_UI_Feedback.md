# 9to5Mac Review: Ben Lovejoy Review Pass & Product Feedback

**Date:** 2026-10-21  
**From:** [[people/ben-lovejoy]] (`ben@9to5mac.com`), [[people/michael-michelis]] (`michael@qalzy.com`)  
**To:** [[people/michael-michelis]], [[people/kostas-koukoravas]]  
**Company:** 9to5Mac, [[companies/qalzy]]  

---

## Overview
Ben Lovejoy, European Editor at 9to5Mac, notified Michael Michelis that he has decided to pass on publishing a product review of the Qalzy AI Nutrition Scale.

## Details
- **Review Decision:** Ben declined to review, explaining: *"It's a very cool idea, but there are just too many problems in the implementation at the moment. The user interface is frankly a mess and the scans can be wildly inaccurate. For example, I scanned a Waldorf salad which was identified as celery."*
- **Policy Context:** Under 9to5Mac's editorial policy, they only publish reviews of products they can on balance recommend, avoiding negative takedowns that damage emerging businesses.
- **Internal Reaction:** Michael forwarded Ben's feedback to Kostas Koukoravas, noting frustration regarding the assessment and referencing the raw image scan URL for the salad.
