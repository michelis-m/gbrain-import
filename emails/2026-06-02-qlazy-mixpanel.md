*These are the main differences between the plans, you can find out more
about them here <https://mixpanel.com/pricing/>:*

   - *Event Calculation:* In the Enterprise plan, events are calculated
   annually, while in the Growth plan they are calculated monthly. (This means
   that if you exceed your event limit in a given month on the Growth plan,
   you will be charged extra. This doesn't happen on the Enterprise plan
   because the calculation is annual.)
   - *Data Retention Period:* The Growth plan only stores data for one
   year, while the Enterprise plan stores it for 2 years or more.
   - *Data Storage Location:* All data is stored on Google Cloud. However,
   with the Enterprise plan, it's stored in a dedicated and isolated location
   on Google Cloud, while the Growth plan stores it in a shared location with
   separate partitions for each company.
   - *Report and Data Sharing for B2B Clients (providing access to reports
   for the client's customers):* This is only available on the Enterprise
   plan.
   - *Account Management and Engineering Support:* This includes assistance
   with employee training, platform management, implementation, and more. This
   is only available on the Enterprise plan.
