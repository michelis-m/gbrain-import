## Overview
Trans-Am Logistics provided a formal freight estimate for moving Qalzy's inventory from Shenzhen (Portless) to ShipBots in the United States.

## Details
- **Full Container (FCL):** 18 pallets require a 40ft container. Estimated EXW door-to-door cost to ShipBots is **$9,650** plus 12.5% customs duty.
- **Foreign Entity Importer of Record:** Singer Nip noted that US customs clearance requires a US legal entity or established customs broker arrangement.
- **Foreign EIN Alternative:** Kostas clarified that Qalzy operates with a US EIN registered to its UK entity and inquired whether Trans-Am can clear customs under this structure. Kostas also requested an alternative LCL quote for shipping 13 pallets.
