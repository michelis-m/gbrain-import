# OpenBorder: Peak Season Carrier Surcharge Starting November 13

**Date:** 2026-11-06  
**From:** Operations Signature <operations@openborder.com>  
**To:** [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] <kostas@qalzy.com>, [[people/michael-michelis]] <michael@qalzy.com>  
**Subject:** Peak surcharge starting November 13  

## Context & Key Points
- [[companies/openborder]] announced a seasonal peak surcharge of **$0.70 per parcel** on all cross-border shipments billed through OpenBorder.
- **Origins Affected:** Shipments originating from US, UK, China (CN), and Hong Kong (HK) logistics hubs.
- **Effective Dates:** Active starting Friday, November 13, 2026 through January 12, 2027 (standard baseline rates resume January 13, 2027).
- **Billing Details:** Surcharge will appear as an explicit line item ("Peak Surcharge") on regular invoices.
- **Operational Impact:** Directly adds $0.70 to fulfillment cost per international order during Black Friday/Cyber Monday and Christmas peak shipping periods.
