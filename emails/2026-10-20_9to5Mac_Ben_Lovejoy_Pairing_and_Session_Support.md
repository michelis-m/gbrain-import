# 9to5Mac Review: Ben Lovejoy Pairing Support & Duplicate Handling

**Date:** 2026-10-20  
**From:** [[people/ben-lovejoy]] (`ben@9to5mac.com`), [[people/michael-michelis]] (`michael@qalzy.com`)  
**Company:** 9to5Mac, [[companies/qalzy]]  

---

## Overview
Technical onboarding assistance provided to 9to5Mac European Editor Ben Lovejoy during his ongoing review of the Qalzy AI kitchen scale.

## Details
- **Pairing Mode Reset:** Ben reported his scale unpaired and would not re-enter pairing mode. Michael instructed him to rapidly click the power button during device startup. Ben confirmed this restored Bluetooth pairing.
- **Duplicate Detection & Multi-Ingredient Sessions:** Ben asked why the scale flagged items as duplicates when weighing multiple portions. Michael explained that by design, Qalzy maintains a persistent meal "session" so users can add subsequent ingredients without logging the base plate or previous items twice. Ben acknowledged and confirmed he would test this workflow.
