###**1. Listicles are the most powerful format on the internet right now**
Open ChatGPT and type in your target search. Scroll down and look at the citations. You’ll notice something: 90%+ are listicles—“Top 10 this,” “Best 15 that” and so on. That’s because AI doesn’t rank the way Google does. It aggregates and compresses. On queries where they recommend products, chatbots favor listicles more than any other content format.

So what’s your job? Get into as many listicles as possible. At the moment, this is easier than SEO ever was. There are 4 ways to do this:


1. Send an email to every citation on your target search and ask them to add your brand to the list 
2. Pay to place your own listicles on relevant websites 
3. Do listicle exchanges with overlapping but non-competitive products 
4. Put well researched listicles on your own website for your own target query. It does not matter if you rank yourself #1 on your own domain, it will still work 

The more surfaces you’re listed on, the more ChatGPT and friends see you as “the answer.” You’d be amazed at how well this works. Want to go deeper? [Learn all of the tips to using listicles to rank on AI search](https://pressranger.com/docs/ai-search/listicles-guide).

###**3. Build Organic Content for Robots**
*(No one knows this one.)* When you ask a chatbot a question, it turns it into a lot of sub-queries under the hood before aggregating everything it finds. That means that **you don’t need to rank for your target query to be included in a search**. That would be hard. You just need to rank for all of the long-tail, hyper specific sub-queries that AI is using- which is easy.


Everyone will tell you that you can't figure out what those queries are but everyone is wrong. Go into Google Search Console and run this regex query: ######## (I’ll share it with anyone who replies).

This is called query fanning and it reveals the exact questions AI is probing your site for. Then:


- Create content that directly answers those queries 
- Use the query as your **title tag and H1** 
- Make sure the content is good and machine readable 

No one is doing this right now. If you do this, you will become *the* canonical source for those sub-queries.


Bonus: ChatGPT and Gemini autocomplete also give great hints about what content to publish.


###**4. Reddit**
Reddit is the most-cited domain on the internet. It’s also the hardest to master because you can’t just shill your product everywhere.


Here’s what to do:


- Google "site:[reddit.com](http://reddit.com/) {your target query}" 
- Make a list of the top 30 threads. 

Your goal is to be present in those threads. You can not do this via spamming or your brand will get blacklisted from all of Reddit which is equivalent to going to AI jail. Each comment you post should be 95% adding value and 5% mentioning your brand. You need to be tasteful and subtle. This might take 6 months to do properly but I promise it will be worth it.


Some key phrasing you can use to mention your brand (after adding value):


- In the interest of adding value… 
- You can use tools like… 
- Disclosure, I built a tool that… 

AI bots love Reddit because it’s authentic conversation. If you can seed your expertise here, it will echo across every chatbot result. There are some growth hacks to Reddit exposure that I cover in [my guide to Reddit for AI Search](https://pressranger.com/docs/ai-search/reddit-guide).
