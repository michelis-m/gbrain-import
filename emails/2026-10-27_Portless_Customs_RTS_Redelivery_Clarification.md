## Operational Mechanics & RTS Constraints
- **Transit & Customs Delay:** The affected package was dispatched on August 29 and was repeatedly flagged during customs inspections.
- **RTS Status:** DHL officially classified the shipment as Return to Sender on October 21.
- **Warehouse Return Timeline:** Portless clarified that parcels marked RTS do not return immediately; regional rerouting takes between **7 and 60 days** to reach an overseas return hub.
- **Redelivery Policy:** Portless confirmed that redelivery procedures can only be initiated once the physical unit has been scanned in at the overseas returns warehouse. Until receipt is verified, customer redelivery cannot be scheduled.
