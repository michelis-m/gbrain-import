In short, I’m writing because we help companies manage China sourcing projects. I wondered whether this might be relevant to Qalzy at some point.

If you’re already thinking “this has nothing to do with me”, no problem at all. Just reply “no” and I’ll leave you in peace.

If you’re still reading, I’ll share a little more about how I see this.

These days, finding a Chinese factory is not that difficult. There are platforms, AI tools, agents and supplier lists everywhere. A few years ago, I also thought this was something most companies could probably handle themselves, and that there might not be much need for a service like ours.

But after helping clients source from China, I realised the hard part is rarely just finding a factory contact.

Every sourcing request is a small or medium-sized project. Things can go wrong in many different places: the requirement may not be clear enough, the target price may not be realistic, quality control may be difficult, IP protection may be overlooked, timelines may slip, or in the worst case, the wrong supplier may simply take the money and not deliver.

That is where I think we can be useful.

Commercial Cross has been working across UK-China trade for 11 years. We understand both sides, and our team is good at project management, supplier communication and solving the strange little problems that often appear between “we need this made” and “the goods are ready to ship”.

If you have a product, packaging idea or sourcing problem in mind, feel free to send it over. Or if you would rather just have a quick chat first, I’d be happy to do that too.
