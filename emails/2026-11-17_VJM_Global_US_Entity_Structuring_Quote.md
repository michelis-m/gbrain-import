## Summary
[[people/reema-mishra]] of VJM Global submitted a formal engagement proposal for Qalzy’s US expansion following a consultation call on 10 November 2026. 

## Proposal Feedback & Assessment
- VJM Global quoted $8,000 for standard entity formation and tax registration.
- Critical omission: The quote failed to include intercompany transfer pricing agreements and intellectual property (IP) ownership/licensing structuring between Qalzy’s UK parent company and the proposed US subsidiary.
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] dismissed the proposal as overpriced and insufficient (*"they want to charge $8k and not even provide the intercompany transfer pricing and IP ownership agreement... that was the whole point"*).

## Strategic Direction
- Founders decided not to proceed with VJM Global and will instead utilize their existing corporate structure to position stock in the US without incurring excessive advisory overhead.
