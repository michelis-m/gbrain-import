## Overview
Portless notified all merchants shipping to the UK, EU, Norway, or Switzerland that they must have a validated tax compliance solution and tax ID on file by **Monday, November 30, 2026**.

## Context & Rationale
When an international parcel is delivered without a valid tax identification number on the shipping label:
1. The carrier charges VAT at the destination border, which Portless pays on the merchant's behalf.
2. If the merchant also collects and remits VAT directly, VAT gets charged and paid twice.
3. Attaching a verified tax ID to every shipping label prevents double taxation and customs holds.

## Penalty for Non-Compliance (Effective December 1, 2026)
For any order dispatched to the UK, EU, Norway, or Switzerland without an active tax ID on file after November 30, Portless will automatically invoice the merchant:
- Full destination VAT calculated on the order's **retail value** at destination rate.
- Plus an administrative surcharge of **10% of that VAT amount**.
- Plus a penalty fee of **$2.00 USD per order**.

## Approved Tax Partners & Supported IDs
- **Supported Integration Partners:** Openborder (preferred pricing/intros offered via Portless), Global-e, Zonos, Numeral, and Reach.
- **Required IDs for Existing Setups:** EU IOSS number, UK VAT number, Norway VOEC number, or Swiss VAT number.
- **Action Required:** Coordinate with [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] to submit Qalzy's EU IOSS and UK VAT registrations to the Portless CSM before the November 30 deadline to prevent order surcharges.
