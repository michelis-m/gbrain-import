## Context
Regulatory announcement regarding the introduction of a new European Union customs processing surcharge affecting non-EU direct-to-consumer imports.

## Key Regulatory Details
- **Fee Structure:** €2 per distinct customs item, defined by unique Harmonized System (HS) code and Country of Origin (COO). Identical units sharing the same HS code and COO are billed once per parcel.
- **Cumulative Cost:** This €2 fee is levied **in addition** to the €3 EU handling fee implemented on July 1, 2026 (totaling €5 in handling fees per unique line item).
- **Effective Timeline:**
  - Applies to all B2C shipments clearing EU customs on or after **November 1, 2026**.
  - [[companies/openborder]] tax engine will automatically begin collecting this surcharge at Shopify checkout on **Monday, October 26, 2026** to capture orders in transit arriving on/after November 1.
- **Exemptions:** Standard B2B inventory shipments (bulk stock transferred into an EU warehouse) are exempt.
- **Action Required:** Audit Shopify product catalog HS codes and Country of Origin metadata to ensure items are accurately classified and not inadvertently triggering multi-item surcharges.
