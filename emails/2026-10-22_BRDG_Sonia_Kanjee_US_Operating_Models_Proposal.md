## Details
- **US Customs Rule Changes (November 30, 2026):**
  - An Executive Order signed on June 3, 2026 directs CBP to tighten importer-of-record rules by Nov 30, 2026.
  - Foreign importers will no longer be permitted informal entries or continuous customs bonds (must use single-entry bonds or CTPAT brokers).
  - Explicitly targets newly formed US "shell" entities established solely as paper importers of record without commercial substance; recommended speaking with a second customs broker immediately.
- **Three Operating Models Outlined:**
  1. *Direct Sales via UK Ltd:* UK entity bills US customers directly and files annual Form 1120-F protective return + Form 8833 under the UK-US tax treaty.
  2. *UK Ltd with US LLC (Disregarded Entity):* Federal pass-through; reported on Form 1120-F with pro forma Form 1120 and Form 5472. Ideal light-touch vehicle for US banking, EIN, and address.
  3. *UK Ltd with US C-Corporation:* Invoices US customers directly and files federal and California corporate tax returns. Requires transfer pricing study (UK parent sells hardware at cost + 20–25% markup, recharges UK overhead). Preferred by US venture capital investors.
- **State Nexus Warning:**
  - Physical inventory held at a California 3PL (ShipBots) establishes immediate physical presence, which takes precedence over economic nexus thresholds ($100k sales tax / $500k income tax) and mandates California corporate tax and sales tax filings.
- **Recommended Ecosystem Partners:**
  - *US Address:* Opus Virtual Offices (dedicated suite/lease needed for banks/processors).
  - *Entity Formation:* Stripe Atlas.
  - *Bookkeeping:* Abacai or E-commerce Accountants.
  - *Sales Tax Automation:* ~$250/month automated compliance software.
