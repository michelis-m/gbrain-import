## Summary
Operational communication between Qalzy and Redo concerning returns fulfillment and creator seeding. On September 28, Frank Marino (Sales Engineer at Redo) acknowledged that while returned units were processed in 3 days, Redo's warehouse software and offline operations are strictly designed for standard D2C fulfillment and cannot support custom influencer shipments. Frank suggested that Redo may not be an appropriate operational fit if influencer ordering is required and proposed scheduling an offboarding review. In response today, [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] proposed a pragmatic workaround: leveraging Redo's existing integration with secondary "outlet" storefronts to list graded return units, allowing Qalzy to purchase and route them directly to creators as standard orders.

---

## Key Details
- **Redo Capability Limitations:** Redo stated they lack native software features or offline warehouse capacity to pick, pack, and ship graded returned inventory to ad-hoc influencer addresses.
- **Offboarding Risk:** Frank Marino suggested an offboarding discussion if influencer dispatch from returned inventory is non-negotiable.
- **Proposed Outlet Workaround:** Kostas suggested setting up an "outlet" sales channel through Redo's standard workflow so Qalzy can place orders internally to fulfill influencer shipments without disrupting Redo's automated D2C operations.
- **Status:** Awaiting Frank Marino's response regarding outlet feasibility.
