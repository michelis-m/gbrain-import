# StoryMD

**Type:** Health Technology & Visual Storytelling Platform  
**Founder & CEO:** [[people/alexander-tsiaras]]  
**Website:** storymd.com  

## Overview
StoryMD is a health education and visual storytelling platform created by Alexander Tsiaras (former NASA/DARPA programmer and Time Inc journalist). It combines medical imaging and physiological visualization with patient-centric narratives.
