# Fin (Intercom)

Intercom is Qalzy's customer support platform, and Fin is the AI agent layered on it that answers customer conversations. Michael owns the account and the relationship; Kostas uses it operationally.

## Current state

- Qalzy is billed by Intercom R&D Unlimited Company at $65.00 a month via Stripe. Receipts land 19 May, 19 June and 20 July 2026, and Michael forwards each one to Xero (`Your receipt from Intercom R&D Unlimited Company #2707-7702`, `#2733-3139`, `#2773-0850`).
- The 19 June receipt itemises the plan: "Fin AI Copilot - Early Stage Discount Year 1", "Advanced Plan - Early Stage Discount Year 1" and "Additional Usage", each at $0.00, with a $65.00 subscription line making up the total. Qalzy is on Intercom's early-stage discount.
- Fin is live on Qalzy's support inbox. Kostas checks whether Fin can handle specific cases before routing customers to it ("I can't remember if Fin can deal with this if I send her to info@qalzy" — Michael: "Fin can handle it yes", `Fwd: New message about Qalzy...`), and asks which parts of human-handled conversations can be automated, specifically discount policy and third-party review/testing questions; Michael's answer is to add them to the FAQs (`Re: Qalzy: Michael Michelis closed a conversation with Andrew Rome`).
- Michael's assessment of Fin's live quality, stated to Intercom directly on 7 August: "At the moment we see lots of errors, inconcistencies and general mistakes from Fin. We want to finetune it to make sure it can handle support queries properly" (`Fin API`).
- He wants API access to run evaluation with LLM agents. Intercom's built-in test functionality and simulations do not meet that need. Brian McKiernan, Staff Product Manager on the Fin API team, took the question and pulled in Kim Olive (kim.olive@intercom.io), saying "I know your team is trying to solve this".
