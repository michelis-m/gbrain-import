## Overview
Jenson Ventures is a London-based early-stage venture capital firm specializing in SEIS (Seed Enterprise Investment Scheme) and EIS (Enterprise Investment Scheme) investments into UK technology startups.

## Track Record & Sector Focus
- Active investor across B2B SaaS, digital health, and consumer hardware startups (notably backing hardware innovations such as electric oral care and water filtration systems).
- Early investor in [[companies/qalzy]].
