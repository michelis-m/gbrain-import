# Truemed

**Domain:** Healthcare Payments / FinTech / HSA & FSA Checkout Infrastructure  
**Website:** https://www.truemed.com  
**Key Contacts:** [[people/leo-schottenstein]] (Partnerships Lead)  
**Headquarters:** San Francisco, California, United States  

## Overview
Truemed enables direct-to-consumer health and wellness merchants to accept pre-tax Health Savings Account (HSA) and Flexible Spending Account (FSA) funds at checkout. It automates Letters of Medical Necessity (LMNs) through affiliated licensed healthcare practitioners, verifying medical necessity so customers can legally allocate pre-tax health dollars for fitness and wellness products.

## Key Facts & Relationships
- **Integration:** Embeds seamlessly into Shopify checkout, functioning similarly to payment options like Affirm or PayPal.
- **Enterprise Brand Partners:** Peloton, Garmin, Sleep Number, AG1, Life Time Fitness, Nike Strength.
- **Reported Commercial Impact:**
  - Average Order Value (AOV) increase of ~20%.
  - Conversion rate lift of 20–40 basis points.
  - Approximately 15% of total merchant GMV processed through Truemed HSA/FSA rails.
- **Synergies with Qalzy:** Evaluating Shopify integration for [[companies/qalzy]] to allow US customers to purchase the AI kitchen scale using pre-tax healthcare dollars.
