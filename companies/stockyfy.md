# Stockyfy (Leverify LLC)

**Industry:** Consumer Electronics Retail & Authorized Distribution  
**Location:** Redmond, Washington, USA  
**Established:** 2016  
**Annual Revenue:** ~$50 Million  
**Key Contact:** [[people/ahmed-hassan]] (Brand Manager, `manager.sourcing@stockyfy.com`)  

## Overview
Leverify LLC (trading as Stockyfy) is a US consumer electronics retailer and distributor operating nationwide via an Amazon storefront, direct ecommerce, and a 1,700 sq ft brick-and-mortar retail store in Redmond, WA.

## Business Model & Partnerships
- Holds written authorizations from 20+ consumer electronics brands.
- Purchases inventory upfront using internal working capital.
- Strictly adheres to Minimum Advertised Price (MAP) enforcement to prevent price erosion across Amazon and retail channels.
- Provides partners with dedicated Amazon PPC management, marketing promotion across their 100,000+ LinkedIn follower network, and presence on their proprietary Supplier Hub portal.
- On 2026-11-13, reached out to [[companies/qalzy]] to explore becoming an authorized distributor for the Qalzy AI food scale on Amazon US, initiating discussions ahead of CES 2027 in Las Vegas.
