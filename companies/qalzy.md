# Qalzy

The world's first nutrition tracking platform built around an AI-powered kitchen scale with a camera that identifies, weighs and logs food in a single tap, with patent-pending technology enabling real-time dietary tracking without manual entry. That description is Michael's own, sent as Qalzy's official bio to The Longevity Show (`Congratulations – You've Been Selected as a Top 30 Pitch Competition Finalist`). It reports calories, macros and micros, and targets weight management, diabetes, metabolic disorders, fitness and healthy aging (per Michael, interview 2026-08-14). Qalzy no longer runs a free trial (same interview).

## Current state

- Registered as QALZY LTD, UK company number 14914986, at 128 City Road, London, EC1V 2NX (`File your confirmation statement now for QALZY LTD 14914986`, `Qalzy Landing Page`).
- Raised a $330k pre-seed, covered by Athletech News in May 2026 under the headline "Qalzy banks $330k pre-seed for AI kitchen scale, joining rise of ambient wellness devices" (`Re: AI nutrition startup raises $330k pre-seed`). Michael confirmed to a returns vendor in May that "we've raised Venture Capital and we are launching in June" (`Qalzy & ReturnBear`). An unsolicited cold email attributes the round to Jenson Ventures and puts Kickstarter pre-orders at nearly €204,000 (`Qalzy + Jenson Ventures + Runway Visibility`); neither founder confirms either figure in the corpus.
- Funded pre-orders through Kickstarter, with Pledgebox used to collect backer shipping fees. 1,300 pre-orders as of 19 May 2026 (`Qalzy & ReturnBear`); 577 backer orders with completed shipping details synced into Shopify by 10 July 2026 (`Expecting to send Qalzy White tomorrow`).
- Product line and SKUs: scale in white (QZ-01-WHT) and black (QZ-01-BLK), carrying case (QZ-CC-01), plates (QZ-PL-01) and chargers (QZ-CHG-EU, QZ-CHG-US), each with its own barcode (`Portless & Qalzy Onboarding Recap and Next Steps`). Standard retail price locked at $199 following the September 2026 pricing test.
- Unit Economics: Breakeven CPA on the $199 price point is $150 ($149.62 factoring in $15 customer-paid shipping subsidizing the ~$32 courier cost, superseding the earlier $140 estimate; $157 at the $239 price point). Target CPA for profitable scale remains ~$100–$120.
  - US Duties & Tariffs: $3.42 for the AI Scale standalone; $6.16 blended per order ($3.42 × 1.8 units/order average attach rate). Billed and collected directly via [[companies/portless]]. (Corrects earlier assumptions that US orders are under de minimis threshold and tax/duty free).
- Sells through Shopify at qalzy.com. Key markets are US, CA, EU, UK, AU and NZ (`Portless & Qalzy Onboarding Recap and Next Steps`).
- Manufacturing is in China across at least three suppliers, one of them Dongguan Brilliant Packaging Co., Ltd and one North Star Sales for carry cases. Assembly line was being finalised in May 2026 (`Re: AI nutrition startup raises $330k pre-seed`, `Portless & Qalzy Onboarding Recap and Next Steps`).
- Fulfilment runs through [[companies/portless]], shipping direct from Chinese non-bonded and bonded warehouses, integrated to Shopify via Pipe17. OpenBorder handles international duties and taxes (`Qalzy + OpenBorder Onboarding`). Returns partners were being evaluated with Redo/ReturnBear and Loop Returns.
- Ships an iOS and Android app. Android package com.qalzy.app, built in Flutter, monitored via Firebase Crashlytics under project nutrioscale-d5150. Build 77 (2.2.6) was live and Build 80 (2.2.7) pending as of 2 August 2026 (`Fwd: Trending stability issues for 2026-08-01 – Android com.qalzy.app`). The app has a premium subscription tier (`Fwd: Re:The spoon was lying to him`) and a food database split into verified entries from sources like USDA and CoFID and user-editable unverified entries (`Qalzy - Feature Request`).
- Accepted into the App Store Small Business Program on 12 June 2026, dropping commission to 15% (`Fwd: Welcome to the App Store Small Business Program.`).
- Stack in use: Intercom with Fin for support (see [[companies/fin-intercom]]), Klaviyo for lifecycle email, Brevo for campaigns (account permanently suspended 30 July 2026), Mixpanel for analytics, Xero for bookkeeping, Slack internally, Shopify for storefront.
- Named a Top 30 finalist in The Longevity Show's inaugural pitch competition, May 2026.
- Hired a part-time Social Media Manager starting 1 July 2026 at €750 for 20 hours a week, engaged as a freelancer in Denmark because Qalzy has no Danish entity (`Social Media Manager - Qalzy`).
- Most profitable customer segments, per Kostas to press: women 35+ losing weight (postpartum/perimenopause), men 35+ building muscle or losing weight, diabetics managing carbs and insulin, and wellness-focused individuals 35+ (`Re: AI nutrition startup raises $330k pre-seed`).
- nutrioscale.com is a live parallel identity, not merely historic: both founders still receive mail there, the Firebase project is named nutrioscale-d5150, and the Facebook-group newsletter goes out from hello@nutrioscale.com with "Nutrioscale Ltd" in the footer (`Featured: Top 5 recipes of the week`).

## Open threads

- Post-launch fulfilment is the dominant open problem. Portless compiled a tracker covering 13 problem orders on 14 August, with more being added; failure modes include phone numbers not imported, false "delivered" scans, wrong-recipient delivery, missing items and customs holds (`Problems with multiple orders in Greece`, `Order PBID007833592`, `Customs Issues | Qalzy`).
- `Uproar PR for Qalzy` — a PR retainer from Moburst was declined as over budget; a $2,500/month holiday gift guide alternative and a possible CES package are still undecided.
- `Fin API` — Qalzy wants programmatic access to fine-tune Fin because of error rates in live support. No resolution.
- `Exclusive Distribution Inquiry – Kuwait Market` — an exclusive Kuwait distributor approach is at the "book a call" stage.
- `Response re: your HARO inquiry` — a review unit was requested by FemFounder for a gift guide; not confirmed sent.
