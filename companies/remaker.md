# REMAKER

**Domain:** Health Tech / Connected Fitness Hardware  
**Website:** https://remaker.co.uk  
**Founder:** [[people/humphrey-maddan]]  
**Headquarters:** Victoria, London, United Kingdom  

## Overview
REMAKER is a connected fitness technology company developing "REMAKER Move" — an attachable sensor that clips onto gym equipment (cables, barbells, tension rigs) to monitor movement velocity and quality, autoregulate loads, and prevent injury via AI coaching.

## Key Facts & Relationships
- **Stage:** Seed / Angel stage; completed 3 angel rounds invested in deep-tech R&D; actively fundraising for consumer expansion (SEIS/EIS eligible).
- **Manufacturing:** Electronics sourced from Taiwan; assembly and final manufacturing located in the UK.
- **Traction:** Started in elite pro-sports (teams in UK and Turkey) and expanding into prosumer strength training.
- **Synergies with Qalzy:** Strategic peer exchange on D2C scaling, international air freight, Meta paid media strategies, and creator ambassador seeding.
