## Overview
ShipBots is a US third-party logistics (3PL) provider operating fulfillment facilities in Gardena, California, strategically located 11 miles from the Port of Long Beach. It provides end-to-end warehousing, container drayage/receiving, pick-and-pack fulfillment, returns management, and automated video recording of all pack-outs for chargeback protection.

## Key Facts & Relationships
- **Port Proximity:** Proximity to Long Beach Port saves ~$5,000 per container compared to inland US 3PL facilities.
- **Service Features:** 5-point returns inspection, video recording of pack-outs stored for 1 year, and international shipping via FedEx International Connect Plus.
- **Contract Terms with Qalzy:** 0.5% inventory shrinkage allowance, receiving turnaround within 3 business days of container delivery, and $2.00–$3.00 fulfillment margins.
- **Transition Schedule:** Qalzy is routing ocean freight of 4,200 smart scales from Shenzhen/Hong Kong to ShipBots in December 2026, targeting a fully operational go-live date of **January 1, 2027** for the New Year resolution peak.
