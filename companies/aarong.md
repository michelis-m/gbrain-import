# Aarong (BRAC)

**Industry:** Fashion, Lifestyle, Home Decor & Social Enterprise Retail  
**Headquarters:** Dhaka, Bangladesh  
**Website:** https://www.aarong.com  
**Key Contacts:** [[people/atanu-bhakta]] (Technology Head of E-commerce), Tamjid Biranis (Head of E-commerce), Jamil (Tech Team Lead)  

## Context
Aarong is Bangladesh's largest retail brand and social enterprise operating under BRAC. It operates dozens of flagship lifestyle department stores alongside a major international e-commerce operation serving the global South Asian diaspora.

## Tech Architecture & Engagements
- **Tech Stack:** Headless frontend on Next.js, backend on Magento (Adobe Commerce Cloud) hosted on Adobe Cloud, APIs powered by GraphQL.
- On 2026-11-05, engaged Intelistyle to explore integrating AI styling, Virtual Try-On, and personalized recommendation widgets to enhance cross-sell and basket values.
