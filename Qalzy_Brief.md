# Qalzy Marketing Brief

Draft v. 1.0
Date: 28.03.25

## General Information

### Product Description
Qalzy is the world’s first smart kitchen scale with an integrated AI camera that automatically identifies your food, weighs it, and logs calories and macros — instantly and accurately.

It recognizes raw, cooked, and packaged foods, even when multiple ingredients are on the same plate, and syncs all your data in real time to the Qalzy app. No more typing, guessing, or jumping between apps — just place your food, press a button, and Qalzy does the rest.
Designed for people who want to track their nutrition without the hassle, Qalzy combines precise weight data with powerful food recognition to deliver the most accurate calorie tracking available. It also learns your personal recipes, supports hands-free tracking via Alexa, and even works when you’re on the go.

### Features
* **Integrated AI Camera for Instant Food Recognition:** The only kitchen scale with a built-in camera that visually identifies what you’re eating. Recognizes raw, cooked, or packaged foods, even in mixed meals — no typing, searching, or guessing.
* **Custom Food Learning AI:** Learns user-specific meals and recipes. Log each ingredient while cooking by pressing the quick scan button or the “Scan & Portion” button. Then log on to the app, select the ingredients and add them to a recipe. Next time you cook the same recipe, you just need to “Quick scan” the completed meal. The AI is smart enough to match it to your previously created recipe.
* **Weight-Based Calorie Accuracy:** Combines visual recognition with precise weight measurement to offer significantly more accurate calorie tracking than volume-based apps (meal scanner apps - AI calorie trackers).
* **Calorie and Macro Auto-Logging:** Automatically tracks calories, protein, carbs, fats, and micronutrients in real time — no manual entry required.
* **Multi-Ingredient Identification:** Accurately identifies and separates multiple different foods on the same plate during one scan.
* **Packaged Food Recognition:** Recognizes branded packaged foods and determines their specific nutritional profiles, including fat percentages and macros.
* **Nutrition Label Scanning:** Scans and extracts nutritional information from product labels to support precise logging.
* **Portion Size Detection:** Identifies and logs the appropriate portion size of food from a scanned package (e.g., one slice from a full pizza).
* **Voice and Alexa Integration:** Allows users to track foods hands-free through Alexa Skill and built-in voice AI functionality.
* **Ecosystem Integration with no subscription fee app:** Syncs seamlessly with the Qalzy app for full dashboard visibility.
* **Wi-Fi Connectivity for Instant Syncing:** Connects to Wi-Fi to automatically sync food data with the Qalzy app in real-time.
* **OpenAI Integration:** Industry Leading AI trained on millions of images.
* **The most accurate food database:** Over 2m verified foods from reputable sources such as USDA, CoFID.

### How It Works
You place the food on the scale. Press the button to identify and log. That’s it! Go into your smartphone app to confirm or adjust the food the scale has identified. Check the dashboard for your overall calorie consumption and other insights.
