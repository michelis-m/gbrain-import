## How to Look Up Contacts
- **Via GBrain CLI:** Run `gbrain search "<name or phone>"` to quickly retrieve any contact or phone number.
- **Via GBrain Think:** Run `gbrain think "What is <name>'s phone number?"` to perform semantic lookup across contacts.
- **Via Obsidian:** Open this folder (`contacts/`) or use Quick Switcher (`Cmd+O`) to find contacts by name, alias, or phone number.
