Qalzy – Product Benefits, Positioning & Competitor Overview
WHAT IS QALZY?
Qalzy is the world’s first AI-powered nutrition tracking ecosystem, combining a smart kitchen scale with a camera and an intelligent calorie tracking app.
Using patent-pending technology, Qalzy can identify, weigh, and log foods with a single tap — automatically tracking calories, macronutrients, and micronutrients in real time without manual entry.
Designed for people who want to lose weight, improve metabolic health, manage diabetes, build muscle, or support healthy aging, Qalzy removes the friction from calorie tracking and makes nutrition tracking effortless enough to become a lasting habit.

3. Accuracy That Builds Trust
Most AI calorie apps estimate calories using photos alone.

That level of inaccuracy can completely break a calorie deficit, especially for:
* Women with lower calorie requirements
* People with diabetes
* Athletes tracking macros precisely
* Users trying to lose weight consistently
Qalzy combines:
* AI food recognition

This delivers significantly higher accuracy than photo-only apps.
