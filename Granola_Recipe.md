# Granola Recipe

Working recipe for homemade granola, measured in grams. Iteration log and adjustments maintained below.

## Ingredients (Current Batch)

### Dry Mix
- **Old-fashioned rolled oats**: 360 g (~4 cups)
- **Raw pecans**: 100 g (~1 cup)
- **Pepitas (pumpkin seeds)**: 70 g (~½ cup)
- **Flax seeds**: 40 g
- **Fine-grain sea salt**: 6 g (1 tsp) *(or 4 g / ¾ tsp standard table salt)*
- **Ground cinnamon**: 1.5 g (½ tsp)

### Wet / Binder Mix
- **Olive oil**: 50 g *(reduced from standard 110 g)*
- **Honey**: 100 g *(reduced from standard 170 g)*
- **Vanilla extract**: 4 g (1 tsp)

### Mix-ins (Post-Bake)
- **Dried cranberries (or other dried fruit)**: 85 g (~⅔ cup, chopped if large)

## Preparation Method

1. **Preheat**: Preheat oven to 325°F (165°C) — slightly lower heat helps avoid burning honey while baking evenly. Line a large rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. **Combine Dry**: In a large mixing bowl, combine rolled oats, pecans, pepitas, flax seeds, sea salt, and cinnamon.
3. **Whisk Wet**: Whisk together the olive oil, honey, and vanilla extract until emulsified.
4. **Mix Thoroughly**: Pour the wet mixture over the dry ingredients. Mix very well with a spatula until every oat and seed is evenly coated.
5. **Pack Down**: Spread the mixture onto the baking sheet. Press down firmly with the back of a spatula (compacting helps form clusters).
6. **Bake**: Bake for 20–25 minutes until golden brown, rotating the pan halfway through if your oven has hot spots.
7. **Cool Completely**: Remove from oven and let it sit undisturbed until 100% cooled (granola crisps up as it cools).
8. **Add Dried Fruit**: Break into clusters, toss with the dried fruit, and store in an airtight container.
