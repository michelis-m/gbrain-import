### CONTENT CREATOR MASTER AGREEMENT

This Influencer & Content Creator Master Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on [DATE] between:

Qalzy Ltd, a company incorporated in the England and Wales, company number 14914986, with registered office at 128 City Road, London, EC1V 2NX UK (the "Company"); and

[FULL NAME], of [ADDRESS], email [EMAIL] (the "Creator").

The Company and the Creator are together the "Parties". This Agreement governs all campaign schedules agreed between the Parties. Each signed or otherwise agreed Campaign Schedule forms part of this Agreement.

1. **Campaigns and Deliverables:** The Creator will provide the content and services described in each Campaign Schedule (the "Deliverables") and will use reasonable efforts to comply with the agreed creative brief, deadlines, technical specifications and platform requirements. The Creator retains reasonable creative freedom and their authentic style and voice, subject to the factual, legal, brand-safety and deliverable requirements in this Agreement and the relevant Campaign Schedule. Any material change to the Deliverables, publication date, fee, usage rights, exclusivity or advertising rights must be agreed in writing, including by email.
2. **Product:** Where the Company provides a product for testing or content creation, the Campaign Schedule will state whether it is gifted, loaned or otherwise supplied. The Creator must promptly notify the Company of any material defect or technical issue that could affect the content. A loaned product remains the Company's property and must be returned as reasonably instructed, with agreed return costs borne by the Company unless otherwise stated in the Campaign Schedule.
3. **Fees, Product Compensation and Commission:** The Company will pay the fees and/or provide the product compensation specified in the Campaign Schedule. Unless stated otherwise, fees include the Creator's ordinary production costs. Where cash compensation is payable, payment will be made within 7 days after completion of the relevant Deliverables and receipt of a valid invoice, unless the Campaign Schedule states otherwise. Where commission applies, qualifying sales, attribution rules, rate, payment timing, returns/refunds and any discount code or affiliate link will be specified in the Campaign Schedule. The Company may exclude cancelled, fraudulent, refunded or returned orders from commission.
4. **Review and Approval:** Where pre-approval is specified in the Campaign Schedule, the Creator will submit content before publication. The Company may request reasonable revisions to correct factual inaccuracies, failure to follow the agreed brief, unapproved product or health claims, legal or regulatory concerns, or material errors concerning the Company or Product. Unless otherwise specified, two reasonable revision round(s) are included.
5. **Advertising Disclosures and Compliance:** The Creator must clearly disclose the commercial relationship in accordance with applicable advertising laws, regulator guidance and platform rules. The Creator must not make false, misleading or unsubstantiated claims about the Company or Product.
6. **Intellectual Property and Organic Usage:** The Creator retains ownership of original content created under this Agreement. For the period stated in the Campaign Schedule, the Creator grants the Company a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to reproduce, repost, display and distribute the approved content on the Company's owned organic channels, including its website, social media accounts and email marketing. Unless otherwise agreed, the Company may crop, resize, subtitle, add branding, create shorter edits and make other reasonable format adaptations, provided that these do not materially misrepresent the Creator or alter the substance of their endorsement. No paid advertising rights are granted under this clause. Paid advertising is governed separately by clauses 7 and 8.
7. **Paid Media Usage:** Only where expressly included in the Campaign Schedule, the Creator grants the Company a non-exclusive licence to use the approved content in paid digital advertising during the stated Paid Media Usage Period and in the stated territories/platforms. This may include advertisements run from the Company's own advertising accounts. The Company may create reasonable advertising variations, including crops, subtitles, hooks, shortened edits, calls to action and format adaptations, provided they do not materially misrepresent the Creator. When the Paid Media Usage Period expires, the Company will stop launching or actively running paid advertisements using the licensed content within a reasonable operational period. Historical campaign records and platform archives may remain.
8. **Partnership Ads, Whitelisting and Creator Handle Usage:** Where expressly agreed in the Campaign Schedule, the Creator authorises the Company to advertise approved content using the Creator's identity or social account through platform functionality such as Meta Partnership Ads, TikTok Spark Ads or equivalent tools ("Creator Advertising"). The Creator will provide the permissions or authorisation codes reasonably required. The Company will fund media spend unless otherwise agreed and may control campaign budget, targeting, bidding and configuration. The Company will not require the Creator's account password or access to private messages. Creator Advertising may only run for the agreed authorisation period. Any separate weekly/monthly licensing fee will be stated in the Campaign Schedule. The Creator will not revoke agreed permissions during the authorisation period without reasonable cause, including material breach, unlawful use or a reasonable risk of serious reputational harm.
9. **Publication and Content Availability:** The Creator will publish the Deliverables during the agreed publication window and, where a minimum live period is specified, keep them publicly available for that period. This obligation does not apply where content is removed by a platform, continued publication would breach law or platform rules, or the Parties agree otherwise in writing.
10. **Exclusivity:** Any exclusivity applies only if expressly stated in the Campaign Schedule. The Campaign Schedule should define the restricted competitor category and the period before and/or after publication. Unless expressly stated, exclusivity does not prevent the Creator from discussing general nutrition, fitness, health, food tracking, calorie tracking or unrelated products.
11. **Analytics and Performance Data:** Where reasonably available, the Creator will provide campaign performance information requested by the Company, such as reach, impressions, views, watch time, likes, comments, shares, saves, link clicks and audience demographics. Screenshots or platform exports may be requested after publication.
12. **Creator Warranties:** The Creator represents that: (a) they have the rights needed to create and license the Deliverables; (b) their content will be original or appropriately licensed; (c) genuine experiences and opinions will be presented honestly; (d) they will comply with applicable law and platform policies; (e) they will not knowingly infringe third-party rights; and (f) they will not knowingly use purchased/fraudulent followers, views or engagement to misrepresent campaign performance.
13. **Cancellation, Non-Delivery and Rescheduling:** If the Creator cannot complete a Deliverable, they must notify the Company promptly. If Deliverables are not completed without reasonable cause, the Company may require completion, repayment of fees attributable to undelivered work and, where appropriate, return of a product supplied specifically as consideration. If the Company cancels after the Creator has completed substantial agreed work, the Parties will agree reasonable compensation for work properly completed, taking into account any non-refundable costs. Any specific cancellation or kill fee in the Campaign Schedule will prevail.
14. **Conduct and Brand Safety:** Neither Party will knowingly use the collaboration to publish unlawful, defamatory, discriminatory or fraudulent material or material reasonably likely to cause serious reputational harm to the other Party.
15. **Confidentiality:** The Creator must keep confidential non-public information identified as confidential or which a reasonable person would understand to be commercially sensitive, including unreleased products/features, campaign plans and non-public commercial information. This does not apply to information lawfully in the public domain or independently obtained without a confidentiality obligation.
16. **Independent Contractor and Taxes:** The Creator acts as an independent contractor. Nothing creates an employment, agency, partnership or joint venture relationship. The Creator is responsible for their own taxes, insurance and statutory obligations arising from compensation received.
17. **Liability:** Neither Party excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded. Subject to that, neither Party will be liable to the other for indirect or consequential loss arising from this Agreement. Each Party's aggregate liability under a Campaign Schedule will not exceed the total cash fees payable under that Campaign Schedule, except for fraud, wilful misconduct, confidentiality breaches or intellectual-property infringement.
18. **Term and Termination:** This Agreement begins on the date above and continues until terminated by either Party on 30 days' written notice. Termination of the Master Agreement does not automatically cancel a Campaign Schedule already in progress unless agreed or otherwise permitted under this Agreement. Either Party may terminate a Campaign Schedule for material breach where the breach is not remedied within a reasonable period after written notice, where capable of remedy. Accrued payment obligations and licences already validly exercised survive to the extent stated in the relevant clauses.
19. **General:** Neither Party may transfer this Agreement without the other's written consent, except that the Company may transfer it as part of a merger, reorganisation or sale of substantially all relevant business/assets. If any provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain effective. A failure or delay to enforce a right is not a waiver. Notices may be given by email to the addresses normally used by the Parties. This Agreement, together with each Campaign Schedule, constitutes the entire agreement for the relevant collaboration and supersedes prior discussions concerning that campaign. If a Campaign Schedule conflicts with this Agreement, the Campaign Schedule prevails for that campaign to the extent of the conflict.
20. **Governing Law and Jurisdiction:** This Agreement and each Campaign Schedule are governed by the laws of England and Wales. The courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction over disputes arising from them.
