# Kumar

**Role:** Project Champion & Catering Operations Lead  
**Company:** CIS (Catering & Food Service Group)  
**Context:** Leading internal evaluation of [[companies/qalzy]] smart scales for corporate catering and canteen automated food tracking.

## Facts & Notes
- On 2026-11-17, met with [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] to evaluate implementing Qalzy scales across industrial and corporate canteens.
- Strongly advocated for the technology, noting parallels with automated pay-by-weight systems in Japan and Brazil and praising Qalzy as three years ahead of the industry.
- Requested a lower monthly fee structure (~£500/month with capped support) instead of £3,000/month, as monthly SaaS costs must be absorbed by individual operating sites rather than the parent group.
- Founders decided against building custom enterprise hardware/firmware for canteens to remain fully focused on consumer DTC growth.
