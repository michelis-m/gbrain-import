# Keri Nwosu

**Role:** Director of Ecommerce & Digital Merchandising  
**Company:** [[companies/south-moon-under]]  
**Location:** Maryland, USA  

## Context
Director of Ecommerce and Digital Merchandising at [[companies/south-moon-under]], a multi-brand fashion retailer based in Maryland with an ownership group based in New York. Evaluated [[companies/intelistyle]]'s visual AI recommendation and styling technology on behalf of the brand.

## Facts & Notes
- On 2026-11-02, met with [[people/michael-michelis]] along with Jana (ownership group executive) to evaluate Intelistyle's "Complete the Look", automated matching set recognition, and deep fashion tagging.
- Manages an active online catalog of ~2,500 SKUs generating ~100,000 monthly sessions and ~$4.5 million in annual e-commerce revenue.
- Expressed dissatisfaction with current recommendation vendor Nosto due to excessive manual maintenance and inability to intelligently pair matching sets or style outfits visually.
- Evaluating a proposed $500–$900/month Intelistyle tier to replace rule-based Nosto functions and automate visual outfitting.
