## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Subscription Attach Rate Modeling
- **Historical vs. Current Attach Rates:**
  - When subscriptions were preloaded automatically into the cart/basket via Checkout Plus, attach rate reached 32%–38%.
  - Under the current clean unbundled model, the organic subscription attach rate is **18%**, exceeding initial financial model projections of 10%.
- **Future Testing:** Planned a future dedicated A/B test specifically focused on cart-level subscription presentation once baseline CRO testing concludes.

### 2. Financial & Growth Trajectory
- **ROAS Performance:** Live blended ROAS for the month is trending at **1.94**, nearly hitting the target benchmark of 2.0.
- **Sales Growth:** Verified pitch deck slide claims: highlighted 110% cumulative growth since July 2026.
- **Forward Forecast:** Modeled projected MRR/ARR trajectory under 13%–15% month-on-month growth assumptions, reaching ~76k–00k monthly run rate by June 2027. Future expansion into Amazon FBA targeted for Q1/Q2 2027.

### 3. Retention Modeling & Deck Curation
- **Cohort vs Active Retention Discrepancy:** Analyzed an AI-generated analytics interpretation regarding active user stickiness.
  - High-level platform metrics showed 95% retention among users with active sessions, which reflects overall active user stickiness rather than true new user cohort retention.
  - Real annual retention models indicate ~45% user retention, while early cohort drop-off shows a ~4-fold initial attrition (~11% stickiness), matching industry standard benchmarks for fitness apps like MyFitnessPal.
- **Slide Selection Strategy:** Agreed to hide overly granular or confusing retention breakdown slides for the introductory student sourcing call (Harvard VCG) to keep the narrative crisp, while keeping backup slides ready for deep-dive VC diligence calls.
