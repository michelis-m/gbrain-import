## Executive Summary
Kostas Koukoravas, Michael Michelis, and Syed Hussain held their weekly ad performance and creative review. ---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Ad Performance Dip & Fatigue Analysis
- **Post-Saturday Volatility:** Ad account performance deteriorated after Saturday with rising CPAs despite no structural account changes.
- **Spend Reallocation Failure:** Turning off the primary spending ad caused Meta to redirect budget to 4th and 5th-ranked ads (which were previously low spenders), resulting in higher CPAs without conversions. Syed reactivated the top ad today to restore baseline delivery.
- **Rising CPMs & Fatigue:** CPMs hovered at $60+, indicating audience fatigue on the top "yogurt" creative that had dominated spend for two weeks.

### 2. Meta Algorithmic Mechanics: "Entity IDs"
- **Backend Ad Clustering:** Syed explained that Meta groups visually and structurally similar ad creatives into a single backend "Entity ID" above a certain similarity threshold. Consequently, minor iterations of a winning ad share the same fatigue curve and budget rather than acting as independent creative tests.
- **Budget Allocation Risks:** Forcing minimum spend on untested or tired variations risks burning budget without discovery; minimum spend should be reserved strictly for high-confidence variations that show organic traction.

### 3. Holiday Roadmap & Black Friday Strategy
- **Christmas Gifting Shipping Cutoff (December 14):** December 14 is the firm logistics cutoff for guaranteed Christmas delivery. All holiday gifting angles must terminate on December 14.
- **Post-Dec 14 Pivot to New Year Resolutions:** Starting December 15, all ad copy and creative must immediately transition to New Year's resolutions, healthy habits, metabolic health, and protein tracking, eliminating all gifting hooks.
- **Black Friday Creative Playbook:** Syed highlighted that across SPG's DTC brand portfolio, simple static ads with giant, bold typography (e.g., "Black Friday X% Off" or "Biggest Sale of the Year") consistently outperform complex high-production video ads during BFCM week, often without even featuring the physical product.
