### 5. PDP Media Gallery & Amazon-Style Objection Handling
- **Critique of Current Product Gallery:**
  - Kostas and Michael evaluated the current top-of-fold image carousel.
  - Identified issues: too many AI-generated lifestyle images, lack of clear screen zoom showing live macro/calorie calculations, and missing technical specifications.
- **Amazon-Style Infocard Overhaul:**
  - Agreed to redesign the carousel inspired by high-converting Amazon product listings:
    - **Plate Compatibility & Dimensions:** Clear graphic showing exact dimensions and demonstrating that standard 10–11" dinner plates fit without blocking the camera or display.
    - **Real Complex Meals:** Visual demonstrating that the scale accurately segments multi-ingredient meals (e.g., bowl dishes, salmon, rice).
    - **Instant Calorie Visibility:** High-resolution zoom photos showing clear OLED numbers on real food plates.
    - **"No Subscription Required / Free App":** Prominent badge clarifying that core app features and AI scanning require no monthly fee, contrasting against subscription-heavy competitors.
    - **Verified Ingredient Database:** Highlighting verified nutritional database backing.
    - **"What's in the Box" Card:** Clean flat-lay showing the scale, charging accessories, and user guide.
    - **Scarcity / Production Batches:** Discussed testing an availability badge ("September Sold Out, October Sold Out, November Sold Out, Next Batch Unknown").
- **Influencer Review Content:**
  - Discussed commissioning a YouTuber/creator (e.g., Jen) for an authentic, landscape 16:9 product review in reporter style.
- **Testing Methodology:**
  - Rather than testing individual images in isolation, all new PDP carousel assets and layout improvements will be bundled into a comprehensive A/B test.
