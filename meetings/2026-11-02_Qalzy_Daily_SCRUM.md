## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Enterprise Catering & Worker Canteen Lead
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] reported on an inbound discussion with a French catering group managing employee canteens across corporate worksites and mining facilities.
- **Client Requirements:**
  - Accurate caloric and macronutrient breakdown for proprietary recipes and regional employee dishes.
  - Integration with the client's internal nutrition system, which stores ingredient recipes and nutrient breakdowns.
  - Strict data locality compliance: certain operating jurisdictions prohibit worker dietary and biometric data from leaving national borders.
- **Next Steps:** Kostas instructed the client to compile their technical requirements and recipe database specs into a structured spreadsheet before Qalzy will scope or quote custom development.

### 2. Fin AI (Intercom) Calibration & Response Tone
- Kostas raised concerns regarding Fin AI's tone in customer support conversations (specifically referencing an interaction with customer Joann Jenkinson).
- **Tone Drift:** Fin has drifted from being overly optimistic to excessively agreeable and self-critical when users question device accuracy (e.g., agreeing "you are absolutely right to question this" and emphasizing that home meals "will involve some manual setup").
- [[people/michael-michelis]] confirmed Fin operates across three levels (system tone/soul, communication style, and knowledge base).
- **Resolution:** Re-calibrate Fin's prompt instructions to maintain a confident, constructive, and balanced brand tone without validating user doubts or using discouraging negative phrasing.

### 3. Meta Ads Performance, Viral CPM Anomaly & Funnel Diagnosis
- **Viral Reach Anomaly:** Reviewed November campaign performance where a broad top-of-funnel ad went viral, accumulating **79,000 impressions** at an ultra-low CPM of **$4.00–$5.00** and a landing page view cost of **$0.65** (CTR ~0.8%), but resulted in 0 completed purchases.
- **Comment Section Engagement:** Commenters debated calorie counting and body metabolism, driving high algorithmic engagement without buying intent.
- **Funnel Bottleneck Analysis:**
  - Top of funnel (TOF) reach is effectively solved by compelling video concepts and low CPMs.
  - Bottom of funnel (BOF) retargeting and offers are in place.
  - The critical gap is **Middle of the Funnel (MOF) conversion rate optimization (CRO)**: directly addressing customer objections regarding accuracy and subscription value proposition before the holiday buying peak.

### 4. Creative Production, Video Hooks & Accessibility
- Reviewed video assets from Ronaldas vs recent authentic creator cuts.
- **Authenticity Over AI:** Confirmed authentic creator content consistently outperforms synthetic or overly glossy AI assets.
- **Legibility & Hook Optimization:** Evaluated subtitle font sizing on ad cuts. Subtitles must be significantly larger and high-contrast to cater to the core 45+ target demographic.

### 5. Hardware Display Acronym Confusion (P / C / F)
- Discussed feedback from a diabetic customer inquiring what the letters **"P"**, **"C"**, and **"F"** on the physical scale display represent.
- Founders realized that while fitness enthusiasts intuitively understand **Protein, Carbs, and Fat**, mainstream and diabetic users require clear guidance.
- Discussed updating onboarding materials, app color alignment, and documentation to clearly map display acronyms.

### 6. CES 2027 Booth Logistics & Managed Wireless Infrastructure
- **Print Deadlines:** Booth signage, flyers, and collateral must be submitted to printers before December 2026 to avoid expensive rush fees.
- **Live Demo Connectivity:** Live demo scales at CES require reliable Wi-Fi. Because enterprise venue networks typically enforce captive portals or 802.1x enterprise logins incompatible with IoT scales, Qalzy needs a dedicated 2.4GHz WPA2 password-protected access point.
- Michael sent an inquiry to Venetian Las Vegas internet support to obtain pricing and specifications for managed 2.4GHz wireless services.
