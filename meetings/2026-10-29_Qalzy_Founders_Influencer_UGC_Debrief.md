## Key Discussion Points

### 1. UGC Content Review (Leah Healey / One2One Diabetes)
- **Concept & Execution:** Leah delivered a reel demonstrating carb guessing versus precise measurement using the Qalzy scale. In her video, she estimated a meal (sourdough bread, cottage cheese, plain yogurt, honey, carbs) at 51g carbs, whereas the scale revealed the real carb count was over 20g higher—a critical variance for insulin dosing in Type 1 Diabetes.
- **Creator Concerns:** Leah had expressed concern that her newly treated eyebrows were visibly distracting on camera and offered to reshoot or replace the audio.

### 2. Instagram Distribution & Partnership Ad Boosting
- **Posting Format:** Discussed whether to have Leah tag Qalzy or invite Qalzy as an official Instagram Collaborator on the post.
- **Decision:** Confirmed Qalzy will be an Instagram Collaborator. This publishes the post simultaneously to both feeds, maximizes organic social proof, and allows Qalzy to boost the organic post directly as a partnership ad via Meta Ads Manager.
- **Technical Steps:** Michael will outline the step-by-step collaborator instructions for Leah, confirming whether Leah must publish the post first before Qalzy activates boosting, and verify technical requirements with Syed Hussain (`[[people/syed-hussain]]`).
