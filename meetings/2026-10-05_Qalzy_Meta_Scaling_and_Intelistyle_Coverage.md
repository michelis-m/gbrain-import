## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Meta Ad Scaling Skepticism & Unit Economics
- **Agency Claims vs. Reality:**
  - Agency/SPG claims there is substantial room to scale ad spend aggressively; Kostas expressed deep skepticism ("doom and gloom") based on real unit economics.
  - Current figures: **$142 CPA** on a **$202 AOV**, leaving a razor-thin net margin buffer (~$11 profit per unit, ~5.8% net margin). Any CPM increase or conversion dip pushes campaigns into negative cash flow.
- **AI Avatar Fatigue vs. Organic UGC:**
  - Kostas highlighted that AI-generated video avatars (such as Rolandas' AI-scripted ad creatives) are becoming subconsciously recognizable by consumers as synthetic, dampening conversion intent.
  - Speculated that Meta's ad delivery algorithm may also be penalizing synthetic/AI content or routing it to lower-intent inventory.
  - Organic UGC comparison: Mika's UGC asset generated an immediate early sale on limited spend; Agapi's video scaled effectively to $1,000+ spend. Authenticity clearly outperforms synthetic generation for high-consideration purchases.

### 2. Influencer & Creator Persona Matching Buyer Demographics
- **Marketing Persona Principle:**
  - Re-anchored on core direct-to-consumer marketing principle: *the creator persona must mirror the buyer persona*.
  - For a premium $199–$239 smart health hardware product, the core purchasing demographic consists of mature, affluent consumers managing health, weight, or metabolic conditions (35–55+ age bracket).
  - Evaluated Namzi's creator shortlist: rejected several very young creators who attract adolescent or low-purchasing-power audiences. Identified candidates in older age brackets as primary targets for gifted scale outreach.
- **Creator Pipeline Execution:**
  - Verified Fiverr creator [[people/sean-c]] delivered raw assets and is awaiting instructions.
  - Michael confirmed sending outreach to candidate creators matching target demographics.

  - Assessed whether a deep 40% discount or an bundled lifetime app subscription is financially viable. Kostas cautioned that aggressive price discounting alongside surging Q4 peak CPMs risks catastrophic margin erosion unless unit manufacturing costs or freight are subsidized.
