### 2. Enterprise B2B Custom App & Recognition RFP Scoping
- Evaluated feasibility and engineering estimates for a custom enterprise client inquiry:

  - **Custom Model Training:** Training custom client ingredients and portion weights (~1 week, priced at $2,500).
  - **White-Label Custom Companion App / Web Portal:** App showing custom recipes and ingredient matching (~2 weeks engineering, quoted at $5,000).
  - Discussed feasibility of using AI coding workflows (Claude Code/simulators) to accelerate prototype building.
  - Explored enterprise wellness programs (such as UK Vitality) where corporate health budgets subsidize employee fitness hardware.
