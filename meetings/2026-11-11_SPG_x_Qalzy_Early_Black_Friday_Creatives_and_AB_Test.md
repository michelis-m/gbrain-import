## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Statistical Significance Flaws in Absolutely A/B Testing
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] broke down statistical inaccuracies in Absolutely's reported 96–98% confidence score for removing the subscription table:
  - **One-tailed vs. Two-tailed test:** Absolutely calculates a 1-tailed significance test instead of a rigorous 2-tailed test, artificially inflating confidence (true confidence drops from 98% to ~92%).
  - **Multi-variant omission:** Absolutely measures control vs. winning variant in isolation, ignoring the second variant entirely. Factoring both variants drops actual statistical confidence to ~86%.
  - **Recent performance:** The variant clearly outlining the subscription breakdown actually outperformed earlier runs today.
  - **Decision:** Do not make drastic changes to the mobile app or hide subscription details prematurely. Keep monitoring data stability.

### 2. Launching Early Black Friday Promotion (Starting Friday)
- Debated launch timing: agreed not to wait for the official Black Friday week. Across eCommerce and health tech, November functions as "Black Friday month."
- Decided to launch the Black Friday promotion early (starting Friday, Nov 13/14 or Nov 15) to capture upcoming weekend shopping volume.
- Avoids multiple confusing price shifts by establishing one strong seasonal offer.

### 3. Product Page Offer Framing: Free Lifetime Premium
- Rather than eliminating subscription mentions entirely, agreed to retain the subscription comparison section on the PDP, cross out the recurring fee, and badge it:
  > *"Free Lifetime Premium — Black Friday Only"*
- Prevents post-purchase backlash by transparently showing what premium includes while delivering massive perceived promotional value.
- FAQs will be updated to align with the Black Friday lifetime promotion.

### 4. Ad Creative Deliverables & Event Angles
- [[people/syed-hussain]] noted the draft creative brief had generic "Early Bird" copy; instructed that creative assets must explicitly declare *"Black Friday Early Bird"* / *"Black Friday"* to capture seasonal intent.
- SPG creative pipeline:
  - High-performing evergreen static and video ads will be adapted with Cyber/Black Friday event hooks.
  - Review-focused testimonial ads and cyber banners will be delivered before Friday for immediate launch.
