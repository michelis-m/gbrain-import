### 3. Video Creative Pipeline & Live School Kitchen Shoot
- **Commercial Kitchen Shoot at School:** Michael proposed filming authentic cooking content at the [[people/ilda-prifti|Montessorians]] school kitchen in Anoixi. The school has an enclosed commercial kitchen with countertops on both sides and an experienced female cook preparing daily meals, offering an ideal setting to film the Qalzy scale in real culinary use matching the 45+ female demographic.
- **Creative Ad Concepts:**
  - *Pizza Comparison Ad:* Filming multiple pizza varieties on the scale (Domino's vs Dr. Oetker Ristorante vs New York-style slice) with swooping camera shots, macro tracking, and tasting.
  - *Thanksgiving Feast Plate:* Tracking turkey, stuffing, pumpkin pie, and sweet potato casserole.
  - *Mac & Cheese Tracking.*
- **Visual Styling:** Focus on bright high-contrast CapCut captions (orange/green palette) and starting videos with strong problem-framing hooks (*"Stop eyeballing your calories"*).
