# Qalzy Founders Sync: Ad Creative Formats, Hook Strategy & Feature Demos

**Date:** 2026-10-30  
**Attendees:** [[people/michael-michelis]], [[people/kostas-koukoravas]]  

---

## Summary
Internal ad creative review and hook ideation session between Michael Michelis and Kostas Koukoravas. The founders reviewed newly delivered creative cuts and draft assets, evaluated video pacing and UI feature demonstrations (tap-by-tap logging vs QuickScan), refined hooks for specific meals (Bruschetta breakfast, Big Mac on a calorie deficit), established credibility rules around calorie accuracy in video demos, and brainstormed high-converting social proof mashup concepts.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Pacing, Demonstration Formats & QuickScan Feature
- **Demonstrating the App Experience:** Kostas noted that several agency cuts gloss over or obscure the actual screen UI and tapping mechanics, which risks confusing viewers about what the product actually does.
- **Testing Demonstration Angles:** Michael emphasized testing varied video formats:
  - Tapping each individual ingredient/layer on the scale display.
  - Overlaying text breakdowns of the food layers.
  - Testing dedicated demonstrations of the **QuickScan** feature, which has not yet been showcased in live ad creatives.

### 2. Meal-Specific Creative Angles & Hook Testing
- **Bruschetta / Burrata Breakfast:**
  - Reviewed title framing and hooks around the prosciutto and burrata bruschetta recipe.
  - Shifted away from restrictive breakfast timing jokes toward a straightforward, engaging hook: *"Here's how Qalzy tracks my favorite bruschetta"*.
- **Big Mac & Calorie Deficit Angle:**
  - Discussed leveraging iconic, immediately recognizable foods (like a McDonald's Big Mac) to generate instant visual curiosity and stop the scroll.
  - Replaced generic fitness hooks (*"how do you eat that and you stay slim"*) with high-affinity calorie deficit hooks that align with social media algorithms:
    - *"Enjoying a Big Mac on a deficit"*
    - *"Icons are not off limits on a calorie deficit"*
- **Stopping the Scroll & Pattern Interrupts:**
  - Emphasized that ads looking like polished commercial sales pitches are swiped away immediately.
  - Discussed introducing visual disruption and pattern interrupts early in the hook (similar to successful fitness/course creators who create jarring visual actions).
- **Regional Dialect & Agency Copy:**
  - Evaluated British idioms provided by their UK agency (e.g., *"chicken wings and so surely not"*) versus American phrasing (*"ain't no way"*). Noted that cheekier British phrasing can stand out as long as tone and delivery remain engaging.

### 3. Calorie Accuracy & Credibility Guardrails
- **Pulled Pork Calorie Inaccuracy:** Highlighted an issue in a demo where a pulled pork sandwich was logged at only 266 kcal and 14g protein—underestimating the real count given that a standard bun alone is ~200 calories.
- **Brand Protection Policy:** Michael reaffirmed that demo videos with inaccurate calories should not be published, as nutrition-conscious audiences immediately notice errors and flood the comments with negative feedback, undermining product trust.

### 4. High-Velocity Social Proof Mashup Concept
- **Concept Ideation:** Brainstormed creating dynamic, fast-cut compilation ads displaying high-volume proof:
  - Hooks like *"50,000 meals logged, 3,000 pounds lost"* or *"More than 4,000 customers logging with Qalzy"*.
  - Rapid-fire transitions showing real meals (*bam, bam, bam*) matched with authentic 5-star customer review quotes (*"changed my life"*, *"so easy, one tap done"*).
  - Maintaining organic variation across cuts so the content appears authentic and user-generated rather than overly staged.
