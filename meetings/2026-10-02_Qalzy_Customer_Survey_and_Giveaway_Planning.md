## Summary
Strategic product and marketing sync between Michael Michelis and Kostas Koukoravas focused on designing an objection/friction survey tied to a "Win a Free Scale" raffle to understand why interested shoppers and abandoned cart visitors hesitate to complete checkout.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Survey Architecture & Giveaway Incentive
- **Incentive:** A "Win a Free Qalzy Scale" raffle to drive high completion and response rates among cart abandoners and newsletter subscribers.
- **Raffle Terms & Rules:**
  - Clear entry window: 7-day duration.
  - Transparent winner announcement date clearly stated in the invitation email and promotional posts.

### 2. Refining Survey Questions & Friction Points
- **Hesitations & Objections:**
  - *Delivery Timing:* Retained as an option (evaluating if 6–10 business days international air shipping from China creates hesitation).
  - *Return Policy:* Removed from survey; the 30-day money-back guarantee is already prominently featured and standard.
  - *App Compatibility:* Retained and rephrased ("I don't want to switch apps / I want it to work with my existing calorie tracking app like MyFitnessPal").
  - *Import Costs / Tariffs:* Dropped from the survey; US direct-to-consumer shoppers rarely consider import fees, and raising it unprompted introduces unnecessary friction.
  - *Seasonality / Timing:* Added an explicit option: "Waiting for Black Friday".
- **Current Tracking Habit:**
  - Choices include: regular kitchen scale, notes/spreadsheet/paper, dedicated apps, and ChatGPT / AI agents (explicitly adding ChatGPT/AI agent tracking).
- **Format:** Configured core questions as multiple-choice checkboxes (capped at "up to 3 selections") to generate clean histogram data on primary purchase barriers.

### 3. Distribution Channels
- Send dedicated email blast to active subscribers and cart abandoners.
- Promote across Qalzy social media accounts (Instagram, Facebook) to drive broader community participation.
