## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Creative Strategy & Holiday Gifting (Sol Campbell)
- **Early Holiday & Gifting Ad Strategy:** Sol emphasized warming up the holiday gifting audience ahead of Black Friday. Early gifting ads scheduled to launch mid-November to capture organized shoppers buying holiday presents early, gaining momentum through Black Friday and Cyber Monday.
- **Unboxing & Kitchen B-Roll Needed:** Unboxing videos perform best psychologically for gift-oriented ads. Requested three specific shot angles:
  1. *POV:* First-person unboxing opening the package on a counter.
  2. *Mid-shot / 3rd-person:* External camera filming someone (e.g., dad or family member) opening the gift.
  3. *Webcam / 2nd-person:* Creator facing camera with the box in front.
- **Persona Messaging for Gifting:** Gifting ads will focus on core personas (GLP-1 protein maintenance, gym-goers, and chronic meal trackers) using the "better than an app" hook. Every gifting ad must resolve three customer trust hurdles:
  1. *Will it arrive on time?* (Clear last order/shipping cut-off dates).
  2. *Is it the real thing / does it work?* (Demonstrating accurate real-time food recognition and weighing).
  3. *Is it the right gift for the recipient?* (Relieving tedious manual app logging).

### 2. Media Buying & Cost Cap Scaling (Syed Hussain)
- **Cost Cap Campaign Performance:** Hussain launched a dedicated Cost Cap campaign with a $90 target CPA. Over the past 7 days, delivered CPAs very close to $90, successfully buffering account performance while highest-volume campaigns experienced elevated CPAs (~$166 on Meta).
- **Budget Scaling:** Increased budget to $250/day yesterday and testing $300/day today. If the campaign continues spending near budget, will scale budget further; if spend caps out, will incrementally lift the cost cap ceiling.
- **Attribution Discrepancies:** Triple Whale (Qalzy's primary source of truth) reports lower CPAs across both campaigns than Meta's native reporting, indicating delayed or missing Meta attribution. SPG recommends maintaining highest-volume budget until cost caps scale to $700–$800/day.
- **A/B Test Campaign Structure:** Transferred top-spending ads into the cost-cap setup under a structured Meta A/B test to prevent audience overlap and attribution cannibalization.

### 3. CRO, Landing Pages & Creator Ads
- **PDP Headline Test:** Currently running a variant testing a shorter product title (removing "world's first" to eliminate early-stage brand friction).
- **Offer & Bundle Tests:** Hussain preparing offer proposals, including bundling silicone prep plates as a free gift/add-on.
- **Michael Creator Ads Live:** Michael's personal ads went live prior to the call. Team preparing to activate whitelisted partnership ads across creator accounts (including Leah from One2One Diabetes and another creator).
