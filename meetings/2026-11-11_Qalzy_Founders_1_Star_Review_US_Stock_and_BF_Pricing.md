## Key Discussion Points

### 2. US Fulfillment Urgency ($15 Cross-Border Shipping Friction)
- Noted a customer abandoned purchase citing the $15 international delivery charge.
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] urged accelerating the transition of physical stock to a US-based 3PL warehouse ASAP (*"move to US AFAP stock"*), eliminating customer friction over cross-border transit times and delivery fees.
- Analyzed US sales tax presentation: verified that US platforms (including Amazon) calculate and display sales tax distinctly at checkout rather than baking it into the list price.

### 3. Black Friday Offer Structure & Discount Messaging
- Debated how to frame the Black Friday bundle discount without causing customer confusion:
  - Option A: Highlight "43% Off".
  - Option B: Emphasize crossed-out retail price vs. promotional bundle price.
  - Option C: Frame as total dollar savings (*"$200+ / $249+ Total Savings"* factoring the $199 scale + lifetime premium subscription).
  - Option D: "Free Lifetime Premium Included with Scale".
- Michael proposed a high-converting layout: green accent banner showing total dollar savings on one side, paired with "43% Off + Lifetime Premium" messaging. Agreed to A/B test layout variants.

### 4. Video Creative Ad Enhancements
- Evaluated ad creative formats demonstrating food weighing:
  - Single-ingredient weighing can look tedious to prospective users.
  - Agreed to prioritize dynamic multi-ingredient B-roll (e.g., assembling sandwiches, bruschetta, or multi-component bowls) to highlight rapid logging and smart auto-recognition.
