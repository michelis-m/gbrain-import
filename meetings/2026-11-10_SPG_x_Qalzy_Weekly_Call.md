## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Meta Ad Performance & Creative Rotation
- [[people/syed-hussain]] reviewed Meta ad account metrics:
  - Account experienced higher CPAs and a slight performance dip yesterday, characterized as normal intra-week volatility.
  - Meta's delivery algorithm has shifted substantial spend toward an alternate product demo ad variation, demonstrating an automated cycling pattern between demo creatives.
  - Strategic response: Produce a fresh batch of product demo variations with diverse kitchen backdrops, lighting setups, and food types to prevent ad fatigue.
- **Budget Scaling:** Increased ad spend by 00 on Saturday and scaled again on Monday; holding budget steady today to evaluate conversion stabilization.

### 2. Black Friday Landing Page & Mobile CRO
- Reviewed mobile design treatments for upcoming Black Friday promotions:
  - Evaluated countdown timer placement above the fold and selected the preferred high-contrast yellow accent shade.
  - Discussed avoiding crowded mobile sliders to preserve page load speed and ensure clean, immediate visibility of the core offer.

### 3. Subscription Table A/B Test & Conversion Psychology
- [[people/syed-hussain]] noted that omitting the subscription table from the landing page drove noticeable improvements in conversion rate (CVR).
- Discussed the psychological barrier of subscription tables:
  - Explicit tables detailing AI scan tiers can trigger anxiety about hidden recurring fees, depressing checkout intent.
  - [[people/michael-michelis]] proposed positioning standard AI logging as included, reserving subscription framing exclusively for advanced nutrition coaching or unlimited heavy scanning.
  - Founders agreed to gather more data before implementing permanent changes to avoid hurting long-term subscription attach rates.

### 4. Facebook Advertorials & Conceptual Angle Testing
- [[people/syed-hussain]] prepared a Facebook advertorial script tailored to problem-aware audiences (weight management, diabetes, metabolic health).
- Recommended allocating 5–10% of overall ad spend toward testing novel narrative concepts and educational advertorials.
- Discussed audience receptivity to authentic, relatable personas (such as creators managing diabetes or midlife health).

### 5. Whitelisted Creator & Content Curation Tactics
- [[people/syed-hussain]] suggested testing short problem-focused hooks clipped from niche health creators and running them via whitelisted third-party handles.
- This approach allows rapid validation of new hook angles and consumer pain points with minimal upfront production cost before commissioning full creator partnerships.
