### 4. A/B Testing & Variant Sales Velocity Analysis
- **Price A/B Test:** Currently standing at **86% statistical confidence**. Evaluating the psychological barrier of the $199 price point. Agreed to allow the test to run for several more days before locking changes.
- **Color Inventory Disparity:**
  - **White Variant Velocity:** **5.96 units/day** (~406 days of stock remaining at current velocity).
  - **Black Variant Velocity:** **1.21 units/day**.
  - **Root Cause & Testing:** White is currently the default pre-selected SKU on the Shopify store (mirroring Kickstarter where Black was pre-selected and dominated).
  - **Planned Experimentation:** Plan an A/B test on default pre-selection (White vs Black) to measure conversion impact, with a potential transition to White-only if Black is phased out.

### 5. Dedicated Landing Pages & BFCM Strategy
- **Persona-Specific Pages:** Agreed on developing dedicated landing pages tailored to specific personas rather than directing all paid traffic to the lean product homepage.
- **Older Demographic Angle:** Prioritizing a dedicated landing page tailored for older adults/seniors focused on health tracking and ease of use.
- **Long-Form Architecture:** Inspired by brands like iM8 (David Beckham AG1 competitor), evaluate long-form advertorial/product landing pages with deep educational panels to test against the minimal homepage.
- **BFCM Preparation:** Plan a seasonal landing page strategy for Black Friday / Cyber Monday (BFCM) to validate landing page conversion mechanics before scaling Q4 ad spend.

### 6. Creator & Influencer Outreach Pipeline
- **Pipeline Need:** Scaling requires a continuous top-of-funnel UGC and influencer pipeline.
- **Automated Sourcing:** Michael proposed building an automated scraping/discovery pipeline leveraging social search and AI tools to target niche nutrition, dietetics, and calorie-counting creators with verified audience parameters.
