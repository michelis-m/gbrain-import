## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Meta Account Status & Restructure ([[people/syed-hussain]])
- **Completed Actions:**
  - Migrated campaign architecture from ABO (Ad Set Budget Optimization) to CBO (Campaign Budget Optimization).
  - Consolidated ad sets and applied strict customer exclusion segments.
  - Deactivated separate retargeting campaigns to prevent auction overlap and frequency spikes.
  - Reactivated top historical winning ads.
- **Klaviyo Integration:** Dynamic sync between Klaviyo and Meta needed for automated customer exclusions. Jess and Syed will resolve account access via SPG's centralized admin credentials (`info@socialparadigmgroup.com`).
- **Creative Fatigue & Cost Control:** High CPCs and CPMs will be addressed by increasing creative and format diversity to feed Meta Andromeda’s recognition algorithm.

### 2. Pre-Sale Advertorials & Listicles Strategy
- **Mechanism & Conversion Impact:** Listicles and advertorials (e.g. "Top 5 Tools to Support Your Weight Loss Journey") provide pre-sale education and social proof, frequently generating massive conversion rate lifts (+20%–30% CVR).
- **Execution Plan:**
  - **Phase 1:** Build a general branded advertorial on Qalzy's domain. Split-test traffic directly against the existing Product Detail Page (PDP).
  - **Phase 2:** Test direct checkout from the bottom of the advertorial vs. linking through to the PDP.
  - **Phase 3:** Create hyper-personalized landing page variants for specific personas (diabetics, PCOS, GLP-1 users) paired directly with congruent ads (no split test required across personas).
- **Recommended Tech & Tools:** Suggested using **Replo** for no-code Shopify landing page development, and researching competitor hooks using **AdSpy** and **Foreplay**.

### 3. Influencer & Partnership Ads Framework
- **Micro-Influencer Focus:** Recommended contracting micro-creators (<10k followers) rather than expensive macro-influencers. Advised negotiating UGC briefs down from initial quotes to $200–$300 per brief.
- **Testing Protocol:** Commission UGC videos with additional hook variations first. Allocate a temporary 5%–10% minimum budget within CBO for 5–7 days to evaluate algorithmic traction. Only transition proven winning creators into paid whitelisted partnership ads.
- **Audience & Accent Dynamics:** Emphasized that for US Meta ad spend, creators must have American accents, as British and European accents suffer significant conversion drop-offs with American consumers.
- **Handle vs. Audience:** Clarified that partnership ads primarily serve to purchase a distinct new ad ID/handle for Meta's auction algorithm rather than tapping the creator's organic follower base. Founders can also run partnership ads from personal handles or generic branded blog pages.

### 4. Creative Strategy, Messaging & Medical Positioning
- **Benefit-Led Messaging:** Shift all hooks from feature-led ("AI scale with a camera") to pain- and benefit-led ("effortless consistency", saving time, avoiding app friction).
- **Expansion Beyond Weight Loss:**
  - Highlighted that Qalzy tracks micronutrients, vitamins, and minerals, unlocking vast health niches beyond calorie counting.
  - **High-Converting Health Niches:** Post-menopause nutrition, PCOS, bone health, gut health, and elderly dietary tracking.
  - **GLP-1 / Ozempic Support Angle:** Highlighted the massive market of GLP-1 users needing precise protein/fat tracking to preserve lean muscle mass and prevent facial volume loss ("Ozempic face").
- **Creative Delivery & Evergreen Copy:**
  - First weekly creative batch will be delivered by SPG on **Wednesday, September 23, 2026**.
  - Qalzy will provide turnaround feedback within 1 business day.
  - All outdated "Summer Sale" mentions on active ads will be updated to evergreen "Flash Sale" copy.

### 5. Google Ads Roadmap
- **Immediate Overhaul:** Syed initiating implementation of the Google audit today.
- **Architecture:** Separating Brand vs. Non-Brand search campaigns, fixing UTM parameters, and introducing ad-level negative keywords.
- **Shopping Campaigns:** Setting up Standard/Performance Max shopping campaigns targeting intent-based nutrition and weight loss keywords.
- **Budget:** Capped at ~10% of total blended ad spend. Tracking will remain on the native Google Ads tag.
