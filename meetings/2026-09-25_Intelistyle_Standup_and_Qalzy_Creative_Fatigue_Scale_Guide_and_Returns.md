### 4. Customer Scanning Misconceptions & Support Friction
- **Bizarre User Behaviors:** Reviewing recent customer tickets, Kostas highlighted instances of extreme user confusion—such as a customer querying ChatGPT on how to operate the hardware scale instead of reviewing the provided scale guide.
- **Scanning Errors:** Users repeatedly hover food high in front of the camera instead of placing it flat on the scale surface, or scan mixed dishes (e.g. salmon with avocado, tacos) and obscure foods (unclear puddings/yogurts) expecting the AI to guess hidden ingredients.
- **Return Impact:** Noted that poor scanning habits and lack of basic comprehension directly trigger product returns and inflate customer acquisition costs.

### 5. Scale Guide Enhancements & In-App Quick Tips UX
- **Website Instructions:** Acknowledged that website text under the demo video contains outdated agency copy and lacks clear guidance for scanning beverages and sequential ingredients.
- **Three Core Golden Rules:**
  1. *Always place items directly on the scale surface* (never hover in the air).
  2. *Scan ingredients sequentially/one-by-one* for mixed dishes, drinks, or hidden ingredients.
  3. *Use common sense:* If you cannot tell what it is by looking at it, the AI cannot either—scan a barcode.
- **App UX Placement:** Debated tooltip vs permanent banner placement. Agreed to add an in-app banner ("For best results, see top tips") in the "From AI Scale" / "Track Food" section linking directly to the full Scale Guide with the 3 top tips prominent at the top. Discussed making the banner dismissible after 3–5 successful scans.
