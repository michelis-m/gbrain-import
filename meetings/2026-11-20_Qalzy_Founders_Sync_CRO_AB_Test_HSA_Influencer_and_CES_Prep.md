## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Landing Page CRO & A/B Testing Results
- [[people/michael-michelis]] reviewed the latest A/B test results (testing feature/recipe sections including "Everything you need on one device" / Gemini feature blocks):
  - The test reached **95% statistical confidence**, but demonstrated a catastrophic conversion drop (**-62% conversion rate loss**).
  - Discussed visitor behavior: additional interactive buttons/sections distract prospective buyers right before purchase. Visitors get pulled into feature details, FAQs, or exit due to incoming phone notifications.
  - Heatmap/attention analysis showed strong visual attention on the Gemini section and free feature inclusions, but direct drop-off before checkout.
  - **Decision:** Stop and kill the underperforming variant immediately.
  - Michael verified tracking tags to confirm there was no configuration error (confirmed the "no recipes" control was correctly tagged).
  - **Next Iteration:** Michael will deploy a test featuring customer reviews in that section instead.

### 2. Surge in Customer HSA/FSA Inquiries
- Michael noted receiving 4 distinct messages from different customers over a short window asking about paying via HSA (Health Savings Account) or FSA.
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] confirmed seeing the surge as well. Validates the strategic urgency of completing Truemed and Redo HSA/FSA integrations.

### 3. Accounting, VAT & Operational Tasks
- Kostas flagged that VAT compliance and accounting require dedicated focus:
  - Discussed whether to hire full-time or part-time accounting support.
- Upcoming email newsletter preparation is in progress.

### 4. Creator & Influencer Licensing Terms (3Cath GLP-1 Creator)
- Kostas shared incoming terms from influencer 3Cath (GLP-1 creator):
  - Creator requested: $600 USD fee, custom splash discount code, 6 months category exclusivity, 12 months organic usage, and 90 days paid media and partnerships.
- **Qalzy Standard Contract Terms Alignment:**
  - **Paid Media Rights:** Qalzy standard policy is strictly **60 days** (not 90 days).
  - **Organic Usage Rights:** Must be **Perpetual** (in perpetuity). Michael explained that a limited 12-month organic window is an administrative and legal nightmare to track across dozens of creators over time. Creators almost universally agree to perpetual organic usage once explained.
  - **Outreach Automation:** Automated outreach via Hermes/AI enforces these standard baseline terms and budgets.
  - Kostas will counter with Qalzy's standard terms and budget cap.

### 5. App Bug Fix: Hand-Labeled Favorites
- Michael identified and resolved an app bug where users could not save hand-labeled custom favorites properly.
- The bug fix is contained; decided it does not require an emergency hotfix release, but can ship with the next scheduled build.
