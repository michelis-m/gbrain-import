# Familink: South Korea Wholesale Distribution Strategy

**Date:** 2026-10-21  
**Attendees:** [[people/michael-michelis]], [[people/kostas-koukoravas]], [[people/mike-eom]]  
**Companies:** [[companies/qalzy]], Familink Global  

---

## Executive Summary
Michael Michelis and Kostas Koukoravas met with Mike Eom, Senior Officer at Familink Global, to evaluate a wholesale commercial distribution agreement for the Qalzy AI Nutrition Scale in South Korea. Familink operates an 8-figure cosmetics brand and extensive distribution infrastructure across TV home shopping, celebrity endorsements, live commerce, and offline retail chains. Discussions focused on Familink's 3-phase go-to-market strategy, Qalzy's core demographic data (35+ and 45+ health/metabolic segments), Chinese manufacturing lead times, app/database localization, and electrical certification.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Familink Distribution Model & Market Entry
- **Wholesale Bulk Model:** Familink buys inventory upfront in bulk (zero marketing fees charged to Qalzy) and scales through its internal channels.
- **Phase 1 (Celebrity & Influencer Seeding):** Penetration begins with non-salesy K-wave celebrity endorsements (e.g., travel videos, factory visits generating 0k–00k in 3-day bursts) followed by high-conversion product demonstrations (generating 00k+).
- **Phase 2 (Paid DTC eCommerce):** Establishing a dedicated localized Korean landing page and driving Meta/Instagram and TikTok paid ads for sustained customer acquisition and repeat purchases.
- **Phase 3 (Retail Rollout):** Placing products into nationwide offline department and electronic retail chains once brand equity is established.

### 2. Demographic Alignment & Target Audience
- **Target Audience Calibration:** Mike suggested targeting homemakers and gym fitness enthusiasts.
- **Qalzy Historical Sales Data:** Kostas and Michael clarified that Qalzy's real demographic profile is significantly older:
  - Over 35: 95% of sales.
  - Over 45: 75% of sales.
  - Primary use cases: Menopausal metabolic management, postpartum recovery and protein tracking, and senior health tracking (55+).
- Mike confirmed their celebrity partners and influencers include women in their late 30s and that marketing research will target this demographic.

### 3. Supply Chain, MOQ & Order Timelines
- **Stock Allocation:** Qalzy's current manufacturing lot is in Shenzhen, China (1 kg per packaged scale). Kostas noted that stock is slated to move to the US via sea freight within 1–2 weeks; an immediate order would allow direct shipment from China to Korea, saving double-freight costs.
- **Order Volume:** Familink typically launches with 3,000 units (minimum 1,000 units) and orders full containers once scaled.
- **Factory Lead Times:** Factory MOQ is 10,000 units with ~2.5 month lead time. Michael highlighted that the upcoming production run includes minor component refinements (buzzer removal, camera upgrade) and certificate updates.

### 4. App Localization & Regulatory Certification
- **Food Database:** Michael confirmed that Qalzy's database already contains 6,000–7,000 Korean food items. Mike noted that MyFitnessPal is widely used in Korea, so food data compatibility is high. App text translation can be handled via AI with Familink verifying terminology.
- **Hardware Certification:** Qalzy holds FCC, CE, FDA, RoHS, and IC certifications. Mike verified that having CE and FDA credentials will significantly expedite securing Korea Certification (KC mark) for electrical consumer goods.
