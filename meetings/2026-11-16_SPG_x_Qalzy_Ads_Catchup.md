## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Meta Ad Budget Scaling & Account Performance
- [[people/syed-hussain]] reported that the ad account is scaling stably ahead of Black Friday.
- Increased the primary daily ad spend by 00 on Sunday, raising the daily budget from ,400 to ,600.
- Cost Cap campaign review:
  - Spent 54 over the weekend as auction volume expanded.
  - Spend naturally throttles during weekdays (~0/day) and surges on weekends, functioning as an automated low-risk surplus budget that only delivers when CPA targets are met.

### 2. Black Friday Static Creative Approvals & Iterations
- SPG redesigned multiple Black Friday concepts entirely rather than making minor tweaks, following prior founder feedback on discount framing.
- [[people/michael-michelis]] and [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] approved launching the redesigned concepts immediately.
- Specific creative guidelines and corrections agreed upon:
  - Remove redundant discount text where percentages were repeated twice on a single graphic.
  - Fix currency errors (switching Euro symbols back to US Dollars).
  - Eliminate explicit price mentions (99) from static banners; retain "43% Off" as the primary hook. Prior A/B testing revealed that displaying the full price triggers immediate price resistance before customers understand the value.

### 3. Video Creative Holiday Overlays
- Syed will overlay simple Black Friday promotional banners across Qalzy's top-performing historical video ads, a proven DTC format that drives high conversion during holiday sale surges.
- Founders reinforced keeping overlay banners concise and un-cluttered.

### 4. Communication Cadence During Peak Holiday Period
- Daily and weekly written reports will remain concise and focused on high-level spend and CPA metrics to allow Syed maximum bandwidth for real-time bid adjustments and rapid creative iteration.
