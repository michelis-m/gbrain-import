## Summary
Founders strategy discussion between [[people/michael-michelis]] and [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] evaluating commercial sponsorship proposals for CES 2027 and performing a comprehensive financial assessment of app onboarding paywalls versus upfront hardware gross margin.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. CES 2027 Media, PR & Sponsorship Packages
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] met with representatives from the official CES media group (publishers of CES Daily, Tom's Hardware, T3):
  - **Official CES Newsletter:** ~,000 per send. Qalzy appears as an ad placement among general news items (high risk of banner blindness compared to dedicated Kickstarter blasts).
  - **CES Daily Magazine (Print):** ~,000 for a product showcase slot (3–4 products per page, published across three show days).
  - **Meta Ad Retargeting Package:** Publisher can target registered CES attendees using attendee email lists directly on Meta Ads.
  - **On-Site Creator Video:** ~,000 for an official CES influencer to visit the booth, produce video content, and distribute across media channels.
- **Strategic CES Orientation:**
  - Evaluated the core purpose of attending CES: direct consumer sales vs. enterprise B2B partnerships (distributors, gym chains, health systems, corporate wellness).
  - Recognized healthcare and digital health as high-growth, high-value segments at CES where enterprise conversations justify exhibition investment.

### 2. App Conversion Dynamics & Paywall Friction
- Analyzed mobile app conversion behavior across iOS:
  - **Day 1 Download-to-Pay:** ~3.72%.
  - **Day 7 Download-to-Pay:** ~3.72% (negligible incremental conversions after day 1), proving nearly all converting users make their subscription purchase during initial onboarding.
  - Discovered a non-platform-specific edge case where users complete trial onboarding without an associated scale ID due to back-and-forth app navigation.

### 3. Financial Trade-Off: Upfront Hardware Profit vs. SaaS MRR
- Analyzed recurring subscription revenue economics:
  - Current iOS MRR stands at ~.39K (growing ~60% over August's .13K, a ~80/month increase).
  - Meanwhile, the landing page Absolutely A/B test (removing the subscription table) has reached 98% statistical confidence, generating an immediate gross profit swing of over ,000 per inventory batch.
- **Strategic Perspectives:**
  - [[people/michael-michelis]]: Prioritize customer delight, eliminate paywall friction during initial hardware setup, and capture immediate hardware gross profit. Build product stickiness before aggressive monetization.
  - [[people/kostas-koukoravas]]: Retain focus on long-term enterprise valuation, SaaS multiples, and establishing clear recurring revenue to cover ongoing AI cloud inference costs.
- **Decision:** Allow the Absolutely A/B test to run for another 1–2 days before making a permanent deployment decision on the primary sales page.
