## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Meta Ad Account Structure & Aggressive BFCM Scaling
- **Campaign A/B Test Recreated:**
  - [[people/syed-hussain]] recreated the traffic split test between the Cost Cap (CBO) and Highest Volume campaigns following an attribution window discrepancy on a legacy ad set.
  - Overall performance has remained healthy: ad spend is sustaining upward scale with CPAs consistently holding between **$120 and $130** (well within target CPA limits).
  - Cost cap budgets increased to **$500/day** to absorb spikes when Meta's algorithm identifies high conversion probability.
  - Highest Volume campaign budget scaled from **$1,850/day to $2,000/day**.
  - Black Friday promotional campaign allocated **$300/day**.
  - Syed confirmed plans to scale budgets incrementally every single day leading into Black Friday.

### 2. Advertorial Campaign Performance & Microsoft Clarity Gap
- The newly launched advertorial campaign spent **~$158 with 0 conversions** so far despite respectable click-through rates (CTR).
- Syed identified a critical diagnostic gap: **Microsoft Clarity is missing** on the advertorial landing page, preventing session recording and drop-off analysis.
- Syed requested Kostas install Clarity immediately. If conversions do not materialize by end-of-day Tuesday, Syed will pause the advertorial campaign to focus 100% of budget and bandwidth on proven bottom-of-funnel BFCM campaigns, revisiting the advertorial post-Cyber Monday.

### 3. Google Ads & PMax Black Friday Configuration
- **Search Campaigns:** Pinned assets containing the Black Friday promotional discount offer are already active; no separate search campaign needed.
- **Shopping Ads / Banner Policy:** A test incorporating promotional banners into product feed images was rejected by Google for policy violations (Google requires clean product photography without overlay banners).
- **PMax Assets:** Creative updates for PMax are pending SPG internal delivery, though Syed views PMax creative refreshes as secondary to Meta for direct BFCM volume.

### 4. Creative Pipeline & Factory Dinner Video Footage
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] captured authentic raw video footage during dinner with manufacturing factory representatives at a Greek restaurant (featuring dishes like dolmadakia, pastitsio, and table interaction).
- Syed explained that the SPG creative editing team is entirely backlogged with BFCM creative cutdowns and cannot handle ad-hoc editing this week. However, he requested the raw files so SPG can integrate the clips as B-roll in upcoming post-holiday concepts.
- No new net-new Black Friday video concepts will be started this week; existing high-performing BFCM creative assets will be repurposed for Cyber Monday by swapping the final call-to-action offer card.

### 5. On-Site Conversions & Countdown Timer Timing
- Syed recommended deploying the site-wide Black Friday countdown timer on **Thursday** (to capture early shoppers) or first thing **Friday morning**.
- Suggested extending the Cyber Monday promotional discount through the entirety of the following week, noting that consumer electronics and health-tech hardware maintain strong purchase momentum post-Cyber Monday.

### 6. Q5 (Dec 26 – Jan 5) Growth Opportunity
- Syed highlighted the impending "Q5" window (post-Christmas through early January) as a massive scaling opportunity:
  - CPMs across Meta typically drop by **30% to 50%**, allowing double the reach at identical spend.
  - Health, wellness, and body-composition products experience massive organic tailwinds from New Year's resolutions.
- Kostas confirmed that Qalzy saw app downloads double during last year's New Year period. Both agreed to prepare dedicated "New Year's Resolution" video and static creatives in early December.

### 7. PDP Media Gallery Recommendations & Desktop Bug
- Syed strongly endorsed updating the top-of-fold product image gallery with structured infocards:
  - Product-in-use photos with real humans.
  - Comparison tables ("Us vs. Them" / Traditional manual logging vs. Qalzy AI scanning).
  - Feature callouts with pointer lines.
  - Highlighting verified 5-star customer reviews tailored to specific health niches.
- **Desktop UX Bug:** Kostas pointed out that the desktop PDP main image lacks carousel navigation arrows, making it difficult for desktop shoppers to cycle through photos without scrolling down to thumbnails.
