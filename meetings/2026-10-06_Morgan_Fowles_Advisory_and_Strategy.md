Introductory strategic advisory meeting arranged via Humphrey Maddan between Michael Michelis and Morgan Fowles (former CEO of Tala, ex-LVMH, ex-Farfetch, ex-Spoke). ## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Morgan Fowles Career & Advisory Focus
- **Career Trajectory:**
  - INSEAD MBA graduate; transitioned from traditional retail into early-stage digital startups.
  - Worked at Farfetch during the early SEO/PPC expansion; Spoke (men's chinos) during the early Meta Ads arbitrage era.
  - Served as CEO of Tala (sustainable activewear founded by Grace Beverley), scaling the brand through organic social and creator momentum.
  - Stepped down from Tala in January 2026 to cultivate a portfolio career of advisory, mentorship, and fractional CEO engagements.
- **Current Advisory Portfolio:**
  - Focused primarily on health, fitness, and longevity startups (functional athletic apparel, high-efficacy liposomal supplements, luxury high-tech gyms, and [[companies/remaker]]—her sole other hardware client, whose equipment she highly endorses).
  - Personal health regime: Trains 11 sessions/week (cardio, strength, blood flow restriction [BFR]); tracks macros via AppyDiet, Apple Watch, and ChatGPT.

### 2. Qalzy Origin, Hardware Development & Unit Economics
- **Founder Background:**
  - Michael detailed his progression from software engineering (Intelistyle fashion AI, blockchain venture sold to Indian public entity) into longevity after facing elevated cholesterol and cardiovascular risk markers at age 35.
  - Professional beach volleyball background in Greece illuminated the critical challenge of precise caloric and carbohydrate fueling for athletic explosive performance.
- **Hardware & Intellectual Property:**
  - Identified the operational friction of conventional "smart" scales requiring manual smartphone input.
  - Engineered the Qalzy AI scale with an integrated optical camera; secured a US utility patent.
  - Pre-orders on Kickstarter achieved $250,000 (~1,000+ units). Completed mass production tooling and assembly in China.
- **Commercial Performance & Metrics:**
  - E-commerce DTC sales reached 420 units since end-of-July 2026 launch (~1,420 total units distributed).
  - Physical inventory stands at 4,000–4,500 units in stock.
  - Advertising ROAS expanded from 1.3x/1.4x to 1.6x, with an active target of 2.0x ROAS by Day 90 to unlock scalable unit economics.

### 3. Software Architecture & Mobile Funnel Mechanics
- **AI Infrastructure:**
  - Engineered a modular LLM pipeline integrating multi-step validation and authoritative nutritional databases (USDA, CoFID).
  - Evaluated OpenAI ChatGPT, Anthropic Claude, and standardized on Google Gemini for superior inference latency and speed.
- **Mobile Funnel Strategy:**
  - Morgan shared that she downloaded the Qalzy iOS app and found the 50-question onboarding questionnaire exceptionally long and diet-centric without referencing the hardware scale.
  - Michael clarified the intentional design: launched the standalone software first to refine AI logging models and establish organic retention; the 50-step questionnaire serves as a deliberate commitment filter that nurtures qualified users toward purchasing the hardware scale.

### 4. Morgan's Product Concept & GTM Perspectives
- **Go-To-Market Archetypes:**
  - Contrasted performance-driven DTC (heavy promotional discounting, performance ad scaling, big-box US retail rollout like Target) with elevated aesthetic brand-building (organic social halo, community, influencer alignment).
- **Morgan's AI Training Concept:**
  - Conceptualized a smart coaching LLM designed to resolve the biological tension between cardiovascular endurance (Tour de France cyclist protocols) and hypertrophy/sculpting (bodybuilder protocols) using an 80/20 synthesis based on continuous wearable telemetry.
  - Identified her personal constraint: deep domain mastery in consumer storytelling and marketing, but lack of technical expertise in structuring AI pipelines and LLM data harnesses.

### 5. Mutual Advisory Agreement
- **Knowledge Exchange Structure:**
  - Agreed to establish a reciprocal advisory arrangement: Michael will advise Morgan on structuring her AI infrastructure, LLM data ingestion, and technical pipeline architecture; Morgan will advise Qalzy on US brand positioning, marketing channel diversification beyond Meta, and retail distribution strategies.
  - Agreed to schedule a dedicated 60-minute session (30 minutes technical / 30 minutes brand strategy) in two weeks (week of October 19, following Morgan's travel next week).
