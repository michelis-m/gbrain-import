# StoryMD Partnership Exploration: Healthcare, Clinical Trial Data & Food as Medicine

**Date:** 2026-10-29  
**Attendees:** [[people/alexander-tsiaras]], [[people/karen-jagoda]], [[people/kostas-koukoravas]], [[people/michael-michelis]]  
**Organization:** [[companies/storymd]]  

---

## Summary
Strategic partnership introductory discussion between Qalzy co-founders and Alexander Tsiaras (Founder & CEO of StoryMD), facilitated by Karen Jagoda (Empowered Patient Podcast). Alexander outlined StoryMD's patient-centered visualization and multi-source health data platform ($69M invested to date, 115k institution connections, 61+ wearables integrated) and explored high-value commercial synergies with Qalzy. Key avenues discussed include tapping into pharmaceutical-underwritten clinical trial research, positioning nutrition data for emerging US state-funded "Food as Medicine" initiatives, and repositioning Qalzy's core messaging around nutritional quality and protein retention rather than basic calorie counting.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. StoryMD Background & Healthcare Infrastructure (Alexander Tsiaras)
- **Genesis & Funding:** Built over 30 years starting from DARPA and NASA virtual surgery modeling. Raised $55M plus an additional $14M from private family offices/wealthy individuals (no institutional VC) to build high-resolution biomedical visualization and longitudinal health tracking technologies. Recently launched commercially.
- **Universal Data Ingestion:** Platform ingests clinical records across 115,000 healthcare institutions, 61+ wearable devices, document OCR, and manual inputs into a unified interactive patient health journey.
- **Business Model:** Three-tier structure:
  1. *Out-of-pocket payers:* IVF patients, pregnant mothers, postpartum mothers, caretakers (parents of T1D children, children of aging parents), elite athletes, pet owners.
  2. *Worried Well / Foundations:* Partnering with non-profit disease foundations (e.g., Hepatitis B Foundation) whose programs are underwritten by pharma. Foundations distribute StoryMD's $360 platform for free to patients, gaining high patient compliance.
  3. *Pharmaceutical Research Data:* Pharma funds trials and programs to acquire clean, dynamic, longitudinal patient data rather than episodic medical checkups.

### 2. "Food as Medicine" & Policy Reimbursement
- **State Funding Momentum:** California has allocated $8.5B and New York $2.5B toward "Food as Medicine" initiatives. Clinical evidence demonstrates that structured doctor-prescribed nutritional interventions have equal or superior efficacy to pharmaceutical intervention for conditions like hypertension.
- **Medicare/Medicaid Reimbursement:** The federal government and state programs are increasingly reimbursing nutritional tracking and therapeutic diets, opening institutional distribution channels.

### 3. Qalzy User Demographics & Retention Metrics (Kostas Koukoravas)
- **Audience Demographics:** Contrary to initial assumptions of young gym-goers, Qalzy's real DTC customer base is mature:
  - **95%+** are 35+ years old.
  - **75%** are 45+ years old.
  - **~50%** are 55+ years old.
  - The vast majority track to manage or prevent chronic conditions (Type 2 diabetes, fatty liver, hypertension) or support weight loss.
- **Hardware-Driven Retention Lift:**
  - App-only tracking month-1 retention is ~16%.
  - Qalzy smart scale user month-1 retention is **49% (3x higher)**.
  - Scale owners log on double the number of days compared to app-only users.
  - **21%** of all scale users log continuously for 30 consecutive days or more.
  - High compliance makes Qalzy hardware exceptionally suited for clinical trials and remote patient monitoring.

### 4. Strategic Repositioning & Messaging Advice
- **Beyond Calorie Counting:** Alexander advised Qalzy to deemphasize pure calorie counting in health/pharma discussions. GLP-1 medications naturally create calorie deficits; the critical medical hazard is lean muscle loss, water loss, and malnutrition from poor food quality.
- **The Value Proposition:** Qalzy should position itself as the guarantor of nutritional quality—verifying adequate protein intake, micronutrients, and dietary fiber during medical treatment or aging.
- **CES 2027 Alignment:** Kostas noted Qalzy's upcoming CES 2027 startup pitch in Eureka Park centers on helping carers and aging adults maintain independence through nutritional visibility.

### 5. Technical Integration & Security
- **API Architecture:** Kostas confirmed Qalzy operates clean RESTful APIs returning structured JSON nutrition data.
- **Compliance:** StoryMD maintains SOC 2, HIPAA, and GDPR compliance. A partnership would leverage white-label embedding (e.g. Qalzy tracking embedded into foundation apps powered by StoryMD) to ensure strict patient privacy and data isolation.
