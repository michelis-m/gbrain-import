### 3. Customer Support & Portless Return-to-Sender Policy
- **PledgeBox Address Error (PBID007797201):** A customer entered an invalid shipping address on PledgeBox/Kickstarter, causing the parcel to be marked Return to Sender (RTS).
- **Policy Decision:** Michael and Kostas aligned on customer policy: if a delivery fails due to a customer-provided invalid address, the customer must either pay the reshipment delivery fee for immediate dispatch, or wait until the original unit returns to the overseas warehouse in China before a free replacement is sent.
- **Redo Returns Warehouse SOP:** Kostas noted that Redo warehouse currently only grades returned units as "working" or "damage". Kostas is establishing an SOP with granular grading: *unsealed/in-as-new condition*, *minor blemishes*, and *damaged/warranty*, reserving clean units for influencer seeding. A follow-up call with Redo is scheduled for tomorrow.

### 4. Influencer Pipeline Progress

- **Vakia:** Scale delivered; Michael tasked with following up on content creation.

### 5. BFCM (Black Friday / Cyber Monday) Strategy
- **Urgency:** Kostas emphasized that Qalzy must immediately finalize its BFCM creative strategy and offer structure before CPMs and CPAs spike in Q4.
- **Offer Concept:** A "Lifetime App Subscription" included with the scale purchase ("Buy once, no subscription for life, this Black Friday only"). Because app software access carries negligible marginal COGS, the offer provides exceptional conversion leverage while preserving hardware margin.
- **Technical Execution:** Will leverage Shopify discount codes and order tags to grant lifetime Qalzy Premium access automatically.
