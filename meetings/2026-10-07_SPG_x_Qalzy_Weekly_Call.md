Weekly growth sync between Social Paradigm Group (SPG) and Qalzy founders. Key topics included Sol Campbell's creative matrix mapping Meta ad batches to core personas and pain points (GLP-1 protein maintenance, menopausal plateau, diabetes/cholesterol, burnout trackers, and founder VSLs), Syed Hussain's conversion rate optimization (CRO) roadmap for the Product Detail Page (PDP), performance debrief showing CPA stabilizing below $108 (leading to an agreed ad spend scale), and strategic considerations surrounding 3PL shipping speed vs. margins.

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Creative Strategy & Persona Matrix
- **[[people/sol-campbell]] Creative Plan:**
  - Designed an asset matrix aligning ad hooks to distinct audience personas:
    - **Burnout Weight Loss Trackers:** Core high-converting demographic based on historical engagement data.
    - **GLP-1 Users:** Positioning Qalzy around protein and nutrient preservation (countering muscle loss during medical weight loss) rather than generic weight loss. Framed as a transparent, one-time hardware purchase vs. recurrent, unverified supplement pills.
    - **Menopausal Weight Plateau:** Addressing hormonal weight resistance and metabolic tracking.
    - **Doctor's Orders / Metabolic Health:** Diabetes and cholesterol management (suggested by Kostas for older demographics).
    - **Gym Goers & Muscle Builders:** High-precision macro and protein tracking.
- **Creative Formats & Asset Requests:**
  - Requested Founder VSLs (Video Sales Letters) addressing specific audiences; Kostas noted past red-background founder videos underperformed. Sol offered guided scripts focused on organic founder storytelling rather than rigid scripts.
  - Developed Atria mood boards covering product demos, unboxing, warehouse packaging, and lifestyle usage.
  - Confirmed the "gift from boyfriend/husband" creative angle remains consistently stable and should be expanded for Q4/Black Friday gifting.

### 2. Product Detail Page (PDP) Conversion Optimization
- **[[people/syed-hussain]] CRO Strategy:**
  - Proposed high-trust conversion elements for Qalzy's landing page:
    - **Live Order Activity Tickers:** Dynamic social proof indicators updating recent orders.
    - **Clinical & Survey Results:** Verifiable customer survey benchmarks (e.g. control over cravings, improved energy, visual body composition).
    - **Authority Endorsements:** Micro-videos from certified dietitians or medical practitioners endorsing the scale.
    - **Timeline of Expected Results:** Month-by-month journey (month 1: craving control, month 2: visible body recomp), tailored carefully for compliance.
- **Traffic Split Discipline:**
  - Syed strongly advised against risky 50/50 A/B splits during active paid scaling; recommended an 80/20 or 90/10 split (preserving 80-90% baseline traffic on control) to safeguard revenue and cash flow while testing individual PDP modules.
- **Bundle Architecture & AOV Enhancement:**
  - Syed outlined a 3-tier bundle structure to lift AOV and gross contribution margin:
    1. *Anchor/Base:* Scale + 1 complimentary plate.
    2. *Target Margin Bundle:* Scale + 3 portion plates (discounted to maximize uptake).
    3. *Premium Bundle:* Scale + 5 portion plates + travel case.

### 3. Paid Media Performance & Budget Scaling
- **CPA Stabilization:**
  - Following the reversal of previous landing page changes, Meta CPA stabilized significantly: dropped to **$108** yesterday and **$97** the previous day, beating the $115 CPA target.
- **Budget Scaling Decision:**
  - Agreed to increase daily Meta ad budgets immediately to maximize net contribution margin while winning creatives remain fatigue-free.
- **Fixed Costs Discussion:**
  - Syed probed Qalzy's $19,000 monthly fixed operational overhead; founders noted these costs are lean core operations that cannot be cut further.

### 4. Supply Chain, Shipping Speed & 3PL Strategy
- **Fulfillment Model Constraints:**
  - Evaluated current direct-from-origin 3PL model (Portless) versus US domestic warehousing.
  - Portless is economical for lightweight apparel, but dimensional hardware shipping carries distinct cost and SLA trade-offs.
  - Syed suggested running an intentional test promising faster delivery (e.g., 3-day SLA) to measure conversion lift, noting a UK client suffered a 20% CVR drop when fast delivery was discontinued.
  - Founders highlighted hurdles: high US inventory insurance costs for a UK business entity, customer support ticket load during transit delays, and existing margin dynamics.
