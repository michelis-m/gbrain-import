## Summary
Weekly growth strategy and ad performance call between Qalzy and Social Paradigm Group (SPG) following Syed Hussain's return from leave. The primary focus was diagnosing a sharp CPA surge over the past 48 hours ($271–$276 on-platform). Syed shared screen data indicating that while CPMs have dropped considerably (a positive sign of broadening out of expensive GLP-1 weight-loss auction competition), Meta has begun shifting spend from historical hero creatives into newer variants. Analysis confirmed that the drop is isolated to checkout completion rather than add-to-cart rates. Michael and Kostas explained the removal of the conflicting $4.50 "Checkout Plus" return fee. The team debated whether to immediately revert all recent PDP changes or allow 48 hours to evaluate the isolated removal of Checkout Plus. Additional topics included concluding the pricing test at $199 base, testing compare-at price anchors ($299 vs $349), deploying founder Instagram partnership ads, and developing high-engagement "rage bait" creative angles.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. CPA Spike & Delivery Diagnosis
- **Recent Surge:** On-platform CPA escalated to $271–$276 over the previous two days, significantly above Qalzy's target CPA of $100–$120 and breakeven threshold of $150.
- **CPM Dynamics & GLP-1 Competition:** Syed clarified that lower CPMs are beneficial. Previously, Qalzy's heavy weight-loss and calorie messaging competed directly with GLP-1/Ozempic advertisers bidding at $100–$150 CPMs with $700+ CPA margins. Diversifying into nutrition and health reduces auction pressure.
- **Spend Shifting & Ad Fatigue:** Outbound CTR on top spenders softened (from ~1.64%–1.87% down to lower levels) alongside higher 3-second view costs. Meta responded to elevated CPAs by reallocating impression share across newer ads.

### 2. Funnel Analysis & PDP Troubleshooting
- **Checkout Bottleneck:** Syed confirmed that add-to-cart percentages remained healthy; the drop occurred almost entirely between add-to-cart and order confirmation.
- **Checkout Plus Friction:** Kostas and Michael noted that the $4.50 "Checkout Plus" button was charging customers for returns right above the checkout button, creating friction against the 30-day money-back guarantee. It was turned off earlier in the day.
- **Reversion Debate:** Syed recommended reverting all recent PDP changes (UGC video block, subscription FAQ trim, add-ons removal) to establish an unquestioned baseline. Michael and Kostas advocated holding the current PDP layout for 48 hours (until Friday) to see if removing Checkout Plus alone restores performance.

### 3. Pricing Strategy & Compare-At Anchor Test
- **$199 Base Price Locked:** The prior $199 vs $239 price test was finalized. While statistical confidence hovered around ~90%, $199 delivered superior revenue per visitor and breached key consumer psychological friction.
- **New Compare-At Test:** A new split test was launched today comparing a $299 anchor vs a $349 anchor against the $199 price point (evaluating whether a 42% discount creates skepticism or urgency).

### 4. Creative Pipeline & Scaling Tactics
- **Product Demo Core:** Reaffirmed that direct, clear product demonstration remains Qalzy's highest-converting core format.
- **High-Engagement / Comment Bait Ads:** Syed recommended testing polarizing foods (e.g. weighing a Nutella jar or Snickers bar on the scale). From agency experience, controversy in the comments dramatically boosts platform engagement and algorithmic distribution.
- **Partnership Ads:** Michael proposed running partnership ads directly through the founders' personal Instagram accounts. Syed agreed, recommending running ads directly under the creator/founder handle without the brand tag for higher authenticity.
- **Advertorials & UGC Creators:** Sol Campbell provided creative briefs for advertorials. The team agreed to produce the pages in parallel but hold live ad spend until core CPA stabilizes.
