## Summary
Michael Michelis and Kostas Koukoravas met with Leo Schottenstein from Truemed to kick off the integration enabling US customers to purchase the Qalzy AI scale and subscriptions using pre-tax HSA/FSA funds via automated Letters of Medical Necessity (LMNs).

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Partnership Value & Regulatory Mechanics
- **Mechanism:** US consumers have pre-tax HSA (Health Savings Account) and FSA (Flexible Spending Account) dollars through employer healthcare plans. Truemed integrates directly into the checkout funnel, connecting customers to licensed healthcare providers who evaluate their health profile and issue compliant Letters of Medical Necessity (LMN) for qualifying metabolic and nutritional needs.
- **Consumer Benefit:** Effectively saves US buyers 30%–40% on their Qalzy scale purchase using pre-tax funds.
- **Conversion Benchmarks:** Peer DTC health and fitness brands (Peloton, AG1, Garmin, Eight Sleep) experience a 20–40 bps lift in checkout conversion and ~15% GMV share moving through HSA/FSA payments.

### 2. Commercial Pricing & Structure
- **Integration Fees:** Discussed Truemed's commercial structure, including an 00 one-time setup fee, a standard transaction commission (~8% +  on one-off orders), and 5/LMN for annual subscriptions.
- **Discount Compatibility:** Confirmed that the Truemed fee applies on top of any promotional discount codes offered to customers.
- **Price Balancing:** Leo agreed to confer with Truemed's finance team to explore custom pricing terms or volume concessions tailored to Qalzy's margins.

### 3. Technical Integration & Stripe Entity Requirements
- **Shopify Setup:** Truemed provides a streamlined Shopify checkout integration that can be implemented within 1–2 weeks asynchronously.
- **Entity Requirements:** Discussed entity and banking setup. Qalzy currently operates with a US EIN and UK corporate bank account. Leo noted that Truemed requires Stripe compliance; he will confirm with his finance/legal team whether Qalzy's current UK banking setup with US Stripe meets underwriting requirements.
