# Qalzy Strategy & CRO Sync: October Board Prep, Landing Page Clean Revert, and Black Variant Inventory Test

**Date:** 2026-10-15  
**Attendees:** [[people/michael-michelis]], [[people/kostas-koukoravas]]  
**Companies:** [[companies/qalzy]]  

---

## Summary
Michael Michelis and Kostas Koukoravas conducted a detailed operational, financial, and product strategy session. They reviewed September KPI performance and burn figures in preparation for the October board presentation, analyzed the underperformance of recent landing page CRO changes, agreed to revert recent multi-variable tweaks, and prioritized an urgent 3-to-4 day A/B test setting Black as the default color variant to mitigate white scale stockout risks before Q4. They also evaluated app feature requests, competitor branding tactics, and influencer video assets.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. October Board Meeting Prep & September Financial Performance
- **Meeting Scheduling & Reporting Cadence:** With board member Jeffrey Faustin unavailable next week, Kostas prepared the deck covering September performance, maintaining the principle of full-month historical reporting rather than incomplete partial-month figures.
- **KPI & Efficiency Review:**
  - September closed with concluded pricing A/B tests establishing the 99 upfront and 49 lifetime price tiers.
  - ROAS and contribution margins dropped during the final week of September, though CPMs decreased significantly during the same period.
  - Paid conversion and customer retention rates showed slight improvements.
  - Noted technical tracking issues with Triple Whale subscriptions, requiring manual KPI calculations.
- **Burn & Liabilities:** Current monthly burn is calculated around £6,000–£6,500 when incorporating payroll taxes and employer liabilities.

### 2. Landing Page CRO Review & Clean Revert Decision
- **Multi-Variable Test Diagnostics:** Recent live test had accumulated 50–60 visitors without a conversion. Michael and Kostas audited the changes and concluded that too many elements had been modified simultaneously, muddying attribution:
  - Subtitle was inadvertently removed.
  - Headline copy changed to emphasize one-tap AI logging.
  - Dedicated subscription section removed (which previously received significant user inspection and reassurance).
  - Clinical study data and timeline re-positioned.
  - Video placed on autoplay and FAQ/review placements reorganized.
- **Consensus & Revert:** Kostas strongly recommended reverting the landing page back to the stable baseline version that historically converted, then isolating individual tests (e.g. testing title/copy independently).

### 3. Critical Black vs. White Inventory Balancing Test
- **Imminent Stockout Risk:** White scales represent the vast majority of current orders. Factory production for the next 10,000-unit batch takes approximately 2 months with May and the manufacturing team in China. If current order distribution continues, white inventory will exhaust before Black Friday.
- **A/B Test Execution:** Rather than waiting weeks, Kostas urged running an immediate 3–4 day A/B test making the Black scale the default selection on the product page. This will determine whether conversion rate holds steady when demand is directed to existing black stock, providing actionable inventory certainty by Monday.

### 4. Product Feature Feedback & Competitor Insights
- **Duplicate Recipe Feature:** Evaluated strong user feedback requesting a "duplicate recipe" capability in the app. Users can clone an existing meal recipe and modify a single ingredient (e.g., swapping milk for yogurt) without rebuilding the entire meal from scratch. Kostas noted Chronometer supported similar item-level adjustment on logged entries.
- **Bitepal Marketing Mascot:** Analyzed competitor app Bitepal's customer acquisition strategy utilizing a fairy mascot across viral organic and paid creative.
- **Influencer Video Performance & Scripting:**
  - Jonas influencer ad underperformed, attributed to target audience misalignment.
  - Emphasized that upcoming influencer scales must not arrive without predefined scripts. Michael committed to preparing script versions for pending creator deliveries.
