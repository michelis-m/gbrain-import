# Qalzy Founders Sync: SPG Creative Critique, Pricing Errors & Ad Copywriting Standards

**Date:** 2026-11-16  
**Attendees:** [[people/michael-michelis]], [[people/kostas-koukoravas]]  

---

## Summary
Impromptu afternoon review between Qalzy co-founders critiquing the latest batch of Black Friday creative assets delivered by [[companies/social-paradigm-group]]'s new designer. Identified critical errors including duplicated discount badges, incorrect regional pricing, and unnatural AI-generated copy, establishing standardized copywriting rules and tagline formulas for holiday campaigns.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Quality Control & Execution Failures in SPG Creatives
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] and [[people/michael-michelis]] reviewed newly submitted static ads and expressed frustration with the quality control of SPG's new design hire:
  - "43% off" badge was accidentally duplicated twice on several assets.
  - Price figures were completely wrong (showing 54 and 49), likely because the foreign designer accessed Qalzy's European/UK store view rather than the standard US dollar storefront.
  - Re-emphasized the strategic rule: eliminate raw dollar prices from top-of-funnel creative altogether, relying on percentage discounts ("43% Off") to drive curiosity and qualifying clicks.

### 2. Copywriting & Headline Standardization
- Strongly rejected the designer's awkward headline: *"One price every meal forever"*, identifying it as unnatural, literal AI output that damages brand credibility.
- Established clear, approved tagline and copy alternatives:
  - *"Pay once, track forever"*
  - *"Pay once, track forever — no subscriptions"*
  - *"One payment, zero renewals"*
  - *"This is your sign to cancel another subscription"*
- Emphasized that value props like *"Premium included for life"* must have prominent visual hierarchy without being obscured by background elements.
