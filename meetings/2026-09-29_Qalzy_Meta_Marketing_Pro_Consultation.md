## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Account Fragmentation & Learning Phase Bottlenecks
- **Learning Limited Status:** Multiple active ad sets (including the SPG campaign and general top-of-funnel ad sets) remain stuck in Learning Limited, failing to achieve the minimum 50 weekly conversion events required to optimize machine-learning delivery.
- **Audience Overlap & Internal Competition:** Despite utilizing Advantage+ budget and placement settings, having multiple ad sets targeting the same geographic region and broad demographic parameters forces Qalzy's own ads to bid against each other in the auction.
- **CPA Impact:** This fragmentation directly inflates customer acquisition costs, resulting in the current diagnostic CPA of $143 per purchase (above Qalzy's target range of $100–$120).

### 2. Ad Set Consolidation Strategy
- **Single Ad Set Recommendation:** The Meta representative advised keeping exactly one primary active ad set per target audience segment to pool budget and conversion signals, immediately turning off all competing or underperforming ad sets.
- **Retargeting Segmentation:** Confirmed that separate ad sets should only be created when isolating non-overlapping audiences, such as distinct Advantage+ broad targeting versus dedicated warm retargeting/custom audiences.

### 3. Creative Volume vs. Technical Limits
- **50 Ads per Ad Set Ceiling:** Michael raised that when attempting to scale creative volume, Meta enforces a hard ceiling of 50 active ads per ad set, asking how competitors run hundreds to thousands of ads for single-product DTC brands.
- **Budget Dilution Warning:** The representative clarified that uploading dozens of creatives on a $1,000 daily budget spreads spend too thinly for the algorithm to determine statistical winners, inducing premature creative fatigue and delivery stagnation.
- **Quality over Quantity:** Meta advised concentrating spend on 3 to 5 (or 3 to 10) high-converting creative variations rather than flooding the account with unproven iterations.
- **Carousel Multi-Asset Solution:** To scale creative volume without creating extra ad levels or ad sets, Meta recommended carousel ad formats combining 3 to 10 static cards and short video assets (allowing up to 100 creative assets across 10 ad slots).

### 4. Creative Performance Evaluation & Next Steps
- **Success Metric:** Meta confirmed that the algorithm evaluates creative quality strictly on conversions and purchases generated, rather than impressions or CTR alone.
- **Audit Follow-up:** The representative agreed to email formal documentation and guides on creative consolidation and scheduled a follow-up review for Tuesday, October 6, 2026, offering to include Qalzy's agency ([[companies/social-paradigm-group]]).
