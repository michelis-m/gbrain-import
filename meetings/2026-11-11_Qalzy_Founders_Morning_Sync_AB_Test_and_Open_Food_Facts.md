## Key Discussion Points

### 1. General & City Observations
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] discussed the completed Thames Water super sewer park development near his London flat, contrasting the sensationalized media opposition during construction with the high-quality final park infrastructure.

### 2. Meta Ads Scaling as Core Operational Priority
- Evaluated ad account performance: recent spend increased while CPA experienced mid-week volatility ahead of holiday shopping patterns.
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] affirmed that stabilizing and scaling the primary Meta ad account remains priority #1, taking precedence over smaller third-party creator platforms like Tribe.

### 3. Early Black Friday Timing & Backend Logic
- Agreed on pulling forward the Black Friday promotional launch to Friday to capture early November weekend shoppers.
- Backend readiness requirement: Ensure Qalzy's server/app backend automatically identifies orders placed during the Black Friday campaign window to grant lifetime premium access without requiring manual support interventions.

### 4. Subscription Transparency vs. Device Friction
- Debated customer psychological expectations around hardware subscriptions:
  - Comparisons drawn with Oura, Whoop, and Apple Health.
  - Emphasized that Qalzy scales provide complete core weighing and macronutrient logging out of the box with zero subscription required.
  - Emphasized aligning marketing copy so customers understand that premium app insights are optional value-adds rather than a forced lock-out.

### 5. Open Food Facts Inaccuracies & AI Verification Solution
- Kostas raised frustration over inaccurate crowdsourced nutritional entries in the Open Food Facts database (e.g., Oreos registered as 100 kcal per 100g instead of ~450 kcal), prompting customer support tickets.
- [[people/michael-michelis]] proposed implementing an intelligent AI verification layer:
  - Badge entries as *"Unverified Community Data — Verify with AI"*.
  - When selected, an AI call cross-references USDA/branded databases to correct calorie and macro figures automatically and save the verified record.

### 6. Wellness & Winter Productivity
- Kostas reported purchasing a 10,000-lux SAD daylight therapy lamp to combat dark London mornings and mid-afternoon energy dips, noting an immediate positive effect on focus.
