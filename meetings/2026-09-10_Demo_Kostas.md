- **Active Testing:** Qalzy is running multi-tier pricing and offer A/B tests on Shopify (testing lower price points against the original higher anchor).
- **Primary Bottleneck:** The critical constraint limiting aggressive scaling is creative asset volume and creative quality, alongside media buying execution.

### 2. Agency Scope & Service Mismatch
- **Ben Haxhiu's Value Proposition:** Ben operates a high-level consultative agency focused on:
  - Landing page A/B testing and conversion rate optimization (CRO).
  - Post-purchase workflow optimization and retention flows.
  - Customer research SOPs and creative flywheel consulting (guiding clients on how to create angles, but not filming or editing assets).
- **Core Disconnect:** Ben explicitly clarified that his team does **not** manage ad accounts, run Meta ads, or produce creative assets. 
- **Decision:** Because Qalzy specifically requires hands-on creative generation and media buying rather than on-site CRO advisory, Kostas determined the agency is not a fit.
