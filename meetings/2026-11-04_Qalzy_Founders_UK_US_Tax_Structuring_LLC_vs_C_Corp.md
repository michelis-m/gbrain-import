## Key Discussion Points

### 1. The UK-US LLC Hybrid Mismatch Trap
- **Asymmetric Tax Classifications:**
  - The US Internal Revenue Service (IRS) treats single-member or multi-member LLCs as "transparent" flow-through (or disregarded) entities, taxing members directly on company earnings as they arise.
  - The UK HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) strictly classifies US LLCs as "opaque" corporate bodies by default, treating LLC profits as belonging to the LLC itself and only taxing UK members upon distribution of dividends.
- **Punitive Double Taxation:**
  - Because of this timing and structural mismatch (US taxes income immediately; UK taxes upon distribution), Double Taxation Relief (DTR) often cannot be claimed straightforwardly.
  - This can trigger cumulative tax liabilities reaching up to **70%**.
  - While HMRC recently opened a consultation on LLC tax reform, it only applies to UK resident individuals, not UK corporate members. Case law (e.g., Anson v HMRC) demonstrated that establishing flow-through transparency is an evidentiary nightmare that HMRC fiercely contests.

### 2. Delaware C-Corp Subsidiary as the Clean Solution
- **Structural Legality:** Incorporating a Delaware C-Corp as a wholly owned subsidiary of the UK holding company (`Qalzy Ltd`) creates an unambiguous, standard corporate structure recognized identically by both US and UK authorities.
- **Intercompany Agreements & Transfer Pricing:**
  - Profits and expenses are balanced legally via intercompany management service agreements and IP licensing.
  - The UK parent company will retain ownership of core assets (such as the pending Qalzy patents and brand IP) and charge the US C-Corp licensing royalties and management service fees.
  - US operational revenues pay for marketing, salaries, and management expenses invoiced by the UK entity, legitimately offsetting taxable US profits.
- **Preservation of UK SEIS/EIS:**
  - Maintaining the UK entity as the primary operating company paying salaries ensures compliance with SEIS/EIS investment deed covenants and prevents Qalzy Ltd from becoming an ineligible shell company.
  - Establishes a standard architecture for a future "Delaware Flip" should US venture capital firms lead a future Series Seed/A round.

### 3. Expansion to the UK Domestic Market
- Discussed launching sales in the United Kingdom alongside the US:
  - Leverages identical creative assets, English-language ad copy, and the existing Portless supply chain.
  - Generates direct revenue in the UK parent entity without cross-border tax complexity.
  - Return units can be managed locally in the UK at low cost.

### 4. US Physical Address Requirements for Banking & Compliance
- **P.O. Box Prohibition:** TrueMed, Stripe Atlas, and commercial merchant banking underwriters explicitly reject P.O. boxes and virtual forwarders.
- **Commercial Street Address:** Kostas identified services providing true physical commercial office street addresses (such as `PhysicalAddress.com`) that provide lease/utility documentation sufficient to satisfy Stripe and US banking Know Your Business (KYB) checks.
- Confirmed the necessity of obtaining a verified US telephone number for banking records, TrueMed onboarding, and CES booth inquiries.
