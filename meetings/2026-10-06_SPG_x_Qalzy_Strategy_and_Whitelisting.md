## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Meta Partnership Ads & Whitelisting Setup
- **Facebook Business Page vs. Personal Profile:**
  - Kostas converted his Instagram account to a business profile and was setting up a linked Facebook creator/business page.
  - Syed clarified that Meta strictly prohibits running ads from personal user profiles; an ad must be connected to an official Facebook Page even if it launches with 0 followers.
- **Verification Bottlenecks & Profile Age:**
  - Michael encountered an issue where Meta failed to send the email verification code required to connect his newly created Facebook Page to Instagram.
  - Kostas and Syed suggested alternative verification channels: checking mobile SMS or in-app Facebook notifications.
  - Michael noted an ineligibility warning indicating his newly created page does not meet partner monetization policies for partnership ads.
  - Syed explained that brand-new Facebook profiles and pages often trigger automated restrictions; recommended building a page off an established personal Facebook account with multiple years of active history, followed by posting 4–5 sample posts to establish platform legitimacy.

### 2. Video Production Status & Creator Timelines
- **SPG Editing Pipeline:**
  - Sol Campbell is finalizing edits for the KFC food comparison ads and the gym influencer creative; unhealthy snacks comparison edit will follow today.
  - Diabetes video ads are fully edited and approved, waiting solely on resolution of Michael's Meta partnership ads account linking.
- **Influencer Turnaround Realities:**

### 3. Non-Creator & AI Creative Formats (Miro Board Review)
- Syed presented a Miro board framework of evergreen top-of-funnel ad archetypes that do not require external influencer filming:
  - **AI Cartoon Emotional Story Ads:** Trending 3D/Pixar-style animations depicting relatable wellness/body struggles.
  - **Problem-Solution Listicles:** Hooking viewers on common physical symptoms (e.g. stubborn belly pooch, energy crashes) before introducing the nutrition tracking mechanism.
  - **Third-Person Clinical Case Studies:** B-roll filmed in clinics or kitchens featuring founders/actors with an authoritative American voiceover narrating a patient/client breakthrough.
  - **Founder Stories:** Concise founder hook transitioning into dynamic usage B-roll.
  - **Product Demo Ads:** Pacing food preparation, scale camera recognition, and macro readouts set to music/captions without spoken narration (currently Qalzy's highest-converting format).
- **Roadmap Commitment:** Syed and Sol will compile a comprehensive 4–8 week creative roadmap outlining specific angles, formats, and assets required from Qalzy.

### 4. Landing Page CRO & Advertorials
- Syed commended Qalzy's aggressive CRO testing cadence, noting that offer testing and PDP structural enhancements represent the fastest path to scaling.
- Re-emphasized implementing dedicated pre-sale advertorials; Syed committed to checking with Will at SPG for recommendations on vetted, conversion-specialized copywriters.

### 5. Creative Tailoring for Older Demographics (50+)
- **Audience Metrics:** Kostas highlighted analytics showing peak conversion rates concentrated in 35–45 and 54+ demographics, suggesting custom assets for 54+ (warmer visual tones, family/health focus, slower pacing, eliminating aggressive hip-hop/rap audio).
- **Syed's Strategic Guidance:**
  - Conversion in health/wellness is driven primarily by the acute problem (e.g., diabetes, liver health, nutrient deficiencies) rather than age bracket alone.
  - Edit pacing: Older audiences convert significantly better with fewer fast cuts and calmer cadence.
  - Female messaging nuance: Direct blunt callouts ("lose your gut") work for men but backfire with women. For women, messaging must emphasize emotional well-being, longevity, self-worth, and relationship to family, using relatable internalized language (*"I don't feel like myself in my body"*).
