## Summary
Discovery call between Qalzy founders ([[people/michael-michelis]], [[people/kostas-koukoravas]]) and [[people/christopher-day]], founder of [[companies/media-stack]] (a performance marketing agency specializing in health & wellness brands). The discussion reviewed Qalzy's launch performance, ad creative fatigue challenges, demographic insights, and Media Stack's operational model and pricing structure for Q4 scaling.

## Qalzy Business & Performance Context
- **Product & USP:** Smart nutrition scale with built-in camera and AI that identifies, weighs, and automatically logs food and calories in one tap without manual smartphone entry.
- **Launch Traction:**
  - Kickstarter campaign raised ~$250,000 (~1,200 scales sold in 30 days).
  - Order fulfillment began late July 2026 alongside DTC e-commerce launch.
  - **Month 1 Metrics:** Spent ~£25k–£26k on Meta Ads, generated ~£30,000 revenue (~1.47 blended ROAS, approximately breakeven).
  - Minimal Google Ads spend (brand protection only).
- **Core Challenge:** Creative fatigue. Qalzy was reliant on a single winning creative asset that saw performance decay after four weeks.
- **Customer Demographics:**
  - Converts predominantly 35+; highest converting segments are 45–55 and 65+ years old.
  - Core converting ICP: Women navigating menopause, perimenopause, and postpartum weight management.
  - Under-35 age bracket currently does not convert profitably.
- **Channel Strategy:** Prioritizing mastering Meta DTC on the direct website before expanding to Amazon or TikTok. Goal is to achieve steady profitability and reach £1M in Year 1 revenue by July 2027.

## Media Stack Overview & Proposed Framework
- **Leadership & Track Record:** Christopher Day brings 15 years in digital (ex-Myprotein/The Hut Group US lead, ex-Unilever Beauty, co-founder of watch brand Nordgreen scaled to £15M). Founded Media Stack ~3 years ago.
- **Team Structure:**
  - Media buyers and creative strategists based in London.
  - Creative production team based in Spain (Barcelona & Seville).
  - Staffing model: Senior talent only (minimum 7 years experience). Accounts typically assigned 4–7 specialists.
- **Creative Strategy & Testing Methodology:**
  - Creative-first acquisition strategy pairing paid social with tailored landing page CRO.
  - Benchmark testing velocity: ~£1,000 spend per ad creative (~20–30 creative concepts per month at a £25k–£30k spend tier).
  - Ad account cut-off rule: Recommends testing to 2x–3x target CPA before cutting ads (noting Qalzy's current 1x target CPA shutoff rule may prematurely kill high-value upper-funnel assets).
  - Hook counting: Variations in opening hooks for the same core body are counted as 1 creative asset rather than separate deliverables.

## Pricing & Commercial Terms
- **Core Paid Social & Creative Retainer:** **£3,000 / month**
  - Includes paid social management + 20 creative assets.
  - Additional assets: **£900** per additional batch of 10 creatives.
- **UGC Creator Management:** **£1,000** for 3 creators
  - Media Stack scripts, directs shot lists (10–20 shots), and repurposes raw B-roll into multiple ad variants (creator fees/gifting handled separately).
- **Analytics & Reporting:** Includes custom performance dashboard tracking blended ROAS, MER, LTV, retention rates, and contribution margin/P&L.
