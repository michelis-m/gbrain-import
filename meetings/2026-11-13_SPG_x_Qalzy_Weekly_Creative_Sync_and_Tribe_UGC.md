## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Creative Deliverables Reconciliation & Catch-Up Schedule
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] and [[people/syed-hussain]] audited SPG's production tracking board against shared Google Drive folders:
  - Identified a one-week deficit caused by folder naming mismatches during team onboarding (Week 44 omitted between Weeks 43 and 45). While SPG assumed they were ahead, they were in fact one week behind their contractual 2 briefs / 20 assets weekly SLA.
  - Syed took ownership of the oversight and committed to an accelerated delivery plan: SPG will deliver 3 briefs next week and 3 briefs the following week (totaling 6 briefs / ~60 assets across the two-week sprint) to fully catch up on owed creative volume.
  - Syed noted that a new video editor had initially favored rapid turnaround over polish; SPG is actively refocusing on higher-quality edits.

### 2. Streamlining Revisions: Direct Figma & Canva Collaboration
- [[people/michael-michelis]] requested that SPG provide direct Figma/Canva source links for all static ad designs:
  - Allows the Qalzy team to immediately tweak copy, swap headline variants, or run Figma AI adjustments without waiting on agency feedback loops.
  - Syed agreed to supply editable Figma links alongside all future static deliverables.
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] requested and received a centralized master Google Drive folder link housing all past and upcoming campaign assets to eliminate fragmented link hunting across Slack channels.

### 3. Long-Form Storytelling & Narrative Ad Formats
- Analyzed competitor ad styles utilizing lengthy narrative primary text and soap-opera style drama hooks (citing BB Code's long-form copy format):
  - Syed noted that high-drama, story-driven formats perform exceptionally well across US Meta auctions when grounded in strong, relatable emotional hooks.

### 4. High-Profile YouTube Review Analysis & PR Implications
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] and [[people/michael-michelis]] debriefed an unsolicited YouTube review by an influencer with nearly 1,000,000 subscribers:
  - **Review Dynamics:** The creator took an aggressively critical approach, labeling the hardware "overkill", tossing the instruction manual aside, failing to recognize tare weights on canned goods, and attempting to scan the interior of a closed Taco Bell burrito.
  - **Public Reaction:** Generated ~200 hostile comments alleging kitchen surveillance, data harvesting, and microphone spying.
  - **Silver Lining:** The video unintentionally showcased strong technical performance, successfully recognizing complex salad components and accurately calculating macronutrients when used normally.
  - **Takeaway:** Independent third-party reviews on YouTube are critical for conversion; prospective buyers routinely search YouTube for validation before ordering. Qalzy must proactively seed hardware to credible reviewers.

### 5. Performance UGC via Tribe Creator Platform
- [[people/syed-hussain]] recommended onboarding to **Tribe**, a performance creator platform adding ~35,000 creators daily with heavy US market concentration:
  - **Incentive Alignment:** Unlike traditional flat-fee UGC creators who get paid regardless of results, Tribe creators earn performance-based commissions on conversions, motivating them to produce scroll-stopping hooks and compelling demos.
  - **Execution:** Syed will draft a comprehensive guidelines brief (framing guardrails, must-include features like demonstrating the device within the first 3 seconds, and compliance boundaries) and submit to Qalzy via Slack for review.
