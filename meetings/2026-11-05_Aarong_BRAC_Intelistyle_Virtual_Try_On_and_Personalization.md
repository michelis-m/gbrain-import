## Summary
Exploratory introductory meeting between [[people/michael-michelis]] and [[people/atanu-bhakta]] discussing potential implementation of Intelistyle's AI styling, Virtual Try-On, Complete The Look, and personalized product recommendations across Aarong's flagship digital platform (`aarong.com`).

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Client Background & Technical Architecture
- **Company:** [[companies/aarong]] (social enterprise retail arm of BRAC, the largest fashion and lifestyle retailer in Bangladesh).
- **Current Tech Stack:**
  - Headless frontend built on **Next.js**.
  - Backend powered by **Magento** (Adobe Commerce Cloud) in production since 2012.
  - API communication layer handled via **GraphQL**.
- Aarong has initiated a 2-year e-commerce innovation roadmap with Virtual Try-On and AI recommendation engines identified as central milestones.

### 2. Intelistyle Solution Overview
- Michael presented Intelistyle’s core personalization and recommendation suite:
  - **1-to-1 Personalization:** Builds a dynamic taste profile for each individual visitor based on real-time browsing behaviors, category interactions, and basket events rather than static demographic cohorts.
  - **Performance Benchmarks:** Intelistyle consistently drives a **+16% revenue uplift** in head-to-head A/B tests against legacy recommendation providers such as Nosto and RichRelevance.
  - **Omnichannel Extensions:** Capabilities include interactive styling quizzes, conversational styling chatbots, WhatsApp/WeChat messaging campaigns, and automated personalized email recommendations integrating with Klaviyo or Brevo.
- **Integration Feasibility:** Confirmed full compatibility with Aarong’s Next.js and Magento architecture via GraphQL APIs and Google Shopping product feeds.

### 3. Enterprise Sales Gate & Commercial Requirements
- Atanu outlined the internal decision-making process at Aarong:
  - Technical feasibility is secondary to hard business metrics. His executive team, led by **Tamjid Biranis** (Head of E-commerce), requires verified revenue uplift projections (e.g. 10–16% uplift) and client case studies from globally recognized fashion brands before engaging in product demonstrations.
  - Team Lead **Jamil** supports technical evaluation alongside Atanu.
- **Urgent Scheduling Window:** Atanu requested an executive summary email by next week to present to Tamjid Biranis; Atanu will be traveling outside Bangladesh for approximately one month starting mid-November.
