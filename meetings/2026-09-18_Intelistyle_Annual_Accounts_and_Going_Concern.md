## Key Discussion Points

- **Accountant Requirement:** The company accountant requires a formal going concern declaration confirming the business will continue trading without liquidation over the next 12-month accounting cycle (FY ending January 2027).
- **Balance Sheet & Asset Revaluation:**
  - Cash reserves remain strong and liquid.
  - No capitalized software development or intellectual property prepayments on the books.
  - Minor depreciation on historical computer equipment.
- **Bad Debt Write-Off:**
  - Agreed to write off aged trade receivables from client **AFG**, who have failed to settle outstanding invoices for over a year and are non-responsive to outreach.

### 2. Cash Reserves, Burn Rate & Runway Analysis
- **Cash Position:** Total liquid bank balances stand at **~£200,000** (£68,000 in primary operational account + £134,000 in secondary reserve account).
- **Recurring Revenue:** Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) stands at **~£9,000**.
- **Operating Outflows:** Gross monthly operating expenditure is **~£15,000** (factoring director remuneration and periodic batch transfers to Intelistyle's Greek subsidiary branch).
- **Net Burn & Runway:** Net burn rate is approximately **£6,000 per month**. At this pace, Intelistyle possesses **over 2 years of solid cash runway** even without new contract closures.

### 3. Commercial Pipeline & Client Concentration
- **Guess Dependency:** The Guess contract represents approximately **40% of Intelistyle's recurring revenue**. Kostas noted that given custom workflow integrations, Guess is unlikely to churn or replace Intelistyle with an alternate vendor in the near term.
- **New Enterprise Contracts:** Directors confirmed they can credibly represent to the accountant that the business is actively pursuing major enterprise contract opportunities (e.g. ongoing discussions with Frasers Group).
