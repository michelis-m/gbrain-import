## Summary
Founders' sync between Michael Michelis and Kostas Koukoravas covering booth logistics, staffing, and demo strategy for CES 2027 (Eureka Park), an update on stabilizing Meta ad metrics, and a new creative testing roadmap focused on macronutrient angles (protein, fiber) and podcast hooks.

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. CES 2027 Eureka Park Planning & Booth Setup
- **Booth Economics & Buildout:** Big health-section booths cost $30k–$100k+. Qalzy is exhibiting in Eureka Park with the subsidized upgraded booth package ($3,300 including power and internet). Discussed booth setup options: spending ~$1,000–$2,000 on turn-key furniture, signage, and backdrops vs. self-hauling pop-up displays to save ~$1,000. Kostas decided to finalize the booking and pay immediately.
- **Booth Staffing & Runway Preservation:** Debated whether to bring an external US-based brand ambassador / booth host or Michael's wife [[people/ilda-prifti]] to drive foot traffic. The founders concluded that hiring US talent would require flights, 3 days of day-rate pay, and an additional hotel room—unnecessary burn on runway. Outside staff would also lack deep product knowledge. Michael and Kostas will staff the booth directly.
- **Demo Strategy:** Decided against self-serve iPad stands, which can cause unattended crashes or long queues. Instead, both founders will conduct active, guided demos directly on mobile phones to maintain control of the user flow and qualify conversations.
- **Food Demo & Compliance:** Noted that live demos using real foods (fruit, pre-packaged meals) need clarification with CTA regarding permissions and health/safety rules, alongside early verification of event insurance requirements.

### 2. Meta Ads Performance Recovery
- **Algorithm & Metric Turnaround:** Meta's ad manager flagged an automated "high performing campaign" / trending status.
- **Efficiency Gains:** CPAs dropped noticeably and CPMs normalized down to ~$62 following recent PDP checkout fixes and campaign stabilization.

### 3. Creative Roadmapping: Protein, Fiber & Podcast Hooks
- **Broadening Beyond Calorie Counting:** The founders agreed to expand creative hooks beyond weight loss / GLP-1 angles into specific high-intent nutritional needs: High Protein and Fiber.
- **Slab Video Variations:**
  - *Protein Hook:* "Are you eating enough protein?" featuring salmon slab weighing and macro breakdown.
  - *Fiber Hook:* "Are you eating enough fiber?" featuring chickpeas/lentils and app notification prompts.
  - *Saturated Fat & Micronutrients:* Highlighting in-app warnings when meals exceed healthy saturated fat limits.
- **Huberman Podcast Hook Strategy:** Kostas proposed leveraging Andrew Huberman's discussions on the gut microbiome—specifically how gut bacteria starve without fiber and degrade mucosal linings, triggering systemic inflammation—as a top-of-funnel talking head hook, immediately cutting to Qalzy scale demo footage.
- **Creative Production Alignment:** Propose a 50/50 creative split with partner agencies (SPG / Blazer), requiring them to test Qalzy's founder-developed angles alongside their own concepts using existing footage.
