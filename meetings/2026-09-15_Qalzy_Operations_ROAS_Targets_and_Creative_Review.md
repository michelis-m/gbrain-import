## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Scaling Framework & Target ROAS/CPA Guardrails
- **Target ROAS:** Target blended ROAS is anchored at **~2.0**.
- **Target CPA & Spend Thresholds:**
  - Current blended CPA is running efficiently at **$112–$115** over recent days (well under the breakeven CPA of $150 at the $199 price point).
  - If daily ad spend exceeds **$1,300/day**, the team is willing to accept a higher blended CPA up to **$120** (ROAS ~1.85) to achieve aggressive scaling.
  - Break-even CPA on the $199 retail price is **$150** (break-even ROAS of 1.48).
- **Revenue Targets:**
  - Ramp monthly revenue to **$120,000** next month (October).
  - Target **$160,000** in revenue for December.
  - The critical high-volume window spans November, December, January, and February, where Qalzy must capture disproportionate market share.

### 2. Burn Rate & Overheads
- Fixed monthly overheads (including salaries and software/infrastructure) stand at approximately **$20,000/month**.
- At current volume, net operating burn is approximately $10,000/month.
- Eliminating burn and fully funding co-founder salaries requires reaching sustained monthly revenue of $120k–$150k at a minimum blended ROAS of 2.0.

### 3. COGS & Landed Unit Economics Reconciliation
- Base scale hardware COGS in Shopify is confirmed at **$28.24**.
- Factoring in accessories (protective cases, chargers) and multi-item orders (average order contains ~1.8 items), landed cost per order adjusts to approximately **$31.00–$33.46** once US customs duties and weight-based shipping are included.
- At $199 AOV, contribution margin is approximately 66%, generating ~$13–$18 net contribution per unit at a $115 CPA.

### 4. Triple Whale Tracking Verification
- Michael identified that Triple Whale's auto-installed checkout script and online store pixel displayed an "enable tracking" alert.
- Kostas had configured primary pixel connections on Friday, but the auto-install snippet requires verification to prevent tracking attribution dropouts across Shopify and Meta.

### 5. Creative Analysis & Competitor Ad Teardowns
- **Oura Ring Comparison Hook:**
  - Competitor hook analyzed: *"I have a watch that costs more than the ring, but this gives me a lot more."*
  - **Qalzy Adaptation:** Position against calorie tracking apps:
    - *"I use a calorie tracking app that costs a lot less, but I wasn't losing weight until I tried this AI scale."*
    - Alternative: *"I've tried everything to lose weight... One button calorie tracking changed everything."*
- **Cal AI Format Analysis:**
  - Analyzed Cal AI's high-converting 17-second video ads.
  - Core message: *"Great, you need to be in a calorie deficit. One-button calorie counting, that's it."*
  - The ultra-short format drives high watch time and immediate value clarity. Qalzy can replicate this format using stock scale b-roll, high-energy editing, and punchy captions.
- **Creative Production Directives:**
  - Scripts provided to creators must be highly prescriptive (exact hook, exact demonstration of the camera recognizing food, macro readout, and CTA) rather than open-ended briefs.
