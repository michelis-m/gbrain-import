## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Critique of Meta Pro Consultation & Agency Landscape
- **Meta Rep Incompetence:** Both founders agreed the Meta Pro provided generic, script-driven advice ("glorified VA") that overlooked real-world scaling dynamics. The recommendation to restrict the account to 10 creatives directly contradicts modern DTC scaling playbooks based on volume, creative diversity, and rapid testing.
- **Conflicting Agency Methodologies:** Discussed how every agency pitches mutually contradictory playbooks:
  - Meta/Meta blogs preach strict learning phase adherence and small creative sets.
  - Performance agencies preach creative volume ("quantity creates quality") and Advantage+ broad automation.
  - Considered hiring a specialized Meta machine learning engineer via Intro.co for an objective 15-minute technical account audit.
- **SPG Agency Dynamics:**
  - Zero confidence in Grace's guidance, feeling she simply parrots Meta blog posts.
  - Retain moderate confidence in Syed Hussain's strategic capability; agreed to hold off on structural ad changes until Wednesday's scheduled sync with Hussain.

### 2. Funnel Diagnostics & ROAS Reality
- **Top-of-Funnel vs. Conversion Disconnect:**
  - CPMs have decreased and link click-through rates (CTR) have improved, but this may be driving unqualified curiosity traffic rather than high-intent buyers.
  - Add-to-cart (ATC) rates have remained steady, but final checkout conversion has deteriorated.
- **Financial Performance:**
  - 7-day blended ROAS is sitting at 1.48–1.5 (below target profitability).
  - Monday recorded an $800 daily loss.
  - Historical comparison: The best recent performance was September 13–19 (achieving ~2.0 ROAS), which occurred after the team consolidated account changes in a single batch on Friday and left the algorithm uninterrupted to stabilize.

### 3. Immediate CRO Decisions & Funnel De-risking
Rather than destabilizing the ad account with frequent mid-week creative uploads, the founders decided to capture incremental conversion gains (5% compounding improvements) through three direct funnel fixes:
1. **Kill Price A/B Test ($199 vs. $219):**
   - The test demonstrated high volatility in Revenue Per Visitor (RPV fluctuating between $4.78 and $6.12) with conversion baseline dropping to ~2.8% (below the 3.4% baseline threshold).
   - Dynamic price splits confuse incoming visitors and may trigger algorithmic pricing friction in Meta's pixel tracking.
   - Decided to invoke the kill rule and standardize on the proven $199 price point.
2. **Remove Redo "Checkout+" ($4 Return Fee):**
   - The $4 return coverage option was originally intended to boost buyer confidence, but it lowers the barrier to returning products carelessly.
   - Given ongoing operational breakdowns at [[companies/redo]] (where returned units are not binned and cannot be refulfilled or resold), elevating return rates poses severe financial risk.
3. **Remove Subscription Option from Checkout:**
   - Offering recurring subscription tiers on a hardware purchase creates cognitive friction and cart abandonment.
   - Streamlining checkout to a clean one-time hardware purchase will reduce friction and lift conversion rate.
