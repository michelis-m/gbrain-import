## Summary
Immediate internal debrief between Michael Michelis and Kostas Koukoravas following their partnership call with Andrei Zimiles. The founders discussed expanding Qalzy's long-term product vision into an integrated metabolic health platform (capturing nutrition, wearable sync, and AI-extracted blood panels), reviewed October's financial results where Qalzy achieved near break-even performance on ~$9.8k gross revenue with ~1.75 ROAS, debated fundraising timing vs peak-season sales execution, and addressed personal financial constraints regarding founder salaries and mortgage approvals.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Strategic Platform Positioning: Hard Biometrics & Blood Data
- **Nutrition as the Moat:** Discussed how passive biometric data (sleep, HRV, heart rate via Apple Watch, Whoop, Oura) is commoditized, whereas meal and nutritional data remains the hardest, highest-friction dataset to collect. Qalzy's computer-vision hardware is the foundational bridge.
- **Biomarker Ingestion Feature:** [[people/michael-michelis]] proposed allowing users to upload existing blood panel PDFs, using multimodal AI to parse lab values, track longitudinal biomarker trends, and correlate them directly with macronutrient intake.
- **Wearable & GLP-1 Context:** Positioning Qalzy alongside GLP-1 weight-loss regimes (focusing on adequate daily protein compliance to prevent sarcopenia) and continuous health monitoring.

### 2. October 2026 Financial Review & Near Break-Even Milestone
- **Revenue Performance:** October gross revenue reached approximately **$9,800** with an blended **ROAS of ~1.75**.
- **Break-Even Status:** Operational revenue came very close to covering the company's baseline fixed monthly overhead and founder salaries (~$10k/month).
- **Subscription ARR:** App reporting indicates **~$30k ARR** (over $1,000/month MRR), providing a growing baseline of high-margin recurring software revenue.

### 3. Fundraising Strategy vs Holiday Sales Execution
- [[people/michael-michelis]] suggested that reaching near break-even provides a strong narrative to begin speaking with venture investors immediately.
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] strongly argued against diverting focus to fundraising before January 2027:
  - **Peak Holiday Window:** November and December are critical retail periods (Black Friday, Cyber Monday, holiday gifting) to maximize cash generation and scale volume.
  - **Focus & Bandwidth:** Distracting the core team with cold investor pitching risks stalling marketing stabilization, CRO testing, and CES preparation.
  - **Upcoming Schedule:** Kostas is traveling to India from December 3 to December 16, followed by the holiday shutdown.
  - **Fundraising Leverage:** Raising from a position of verified profitable unit economics and strong Q4 growth metrics allows the founders to dictate terms rather than raising out of distress.
- **Consensus:** Focus 100% on marketing stabilization, conversion rate optimization, inventory management, and CES preparation through year-end. Re-evaluate formal fundraising outreach post-CES in early 2027.

### 4. Founder Personal Finance & Corporate Director Constraints
- Kostas shared that UK mortgage advisors rejected an application to convert a personal residential mortgage to an interest-only structure.
- Lenders treat directors owning >25% of a business as self-employed and assess the statutory company accounts. Because Qalzy has historically operated at an accounting loss while bootstrapping and scaling R&D, lenders refuse mortgage underwriting.
- Reinforces the imperative to achieve sustainable, profitable unit economics so the company can report healthy financials and support increased founder payroll.
