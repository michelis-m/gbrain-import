# Qalzy Founders Sync: CIS Catering B2B Evaluation, Influencer Vetting & In-App Nutrition Insights

**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Attendees:** [[people/kostas-koukoravas]], [[people/michael-michelis]]  

---

## Summary
Midday strategy and operational sync between Qalzy co-founders. Evaluated an inbound enterprise proposal from catering group CIS, decided against pursuing custom B2B canteen hardware/firmware to preserve DTC focus, reviewed influencer turnaround times via US Redo stock, vetted prospective creator profiles, and reviewed upcoming in-app Nutrition Insights and "Healthy Swaps" features.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. CIS Catering Enterprise Opportunity & Strategic Evaluation
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] reported on his meeting with Kumar, project champion at CIS (corporate catering and food service operator).
- Kumar praised Qalzy's vision, highlighting automated pay-by-weight concepts in Japan and Brazil and asserting Qalzy is "three years ahead of the market."
- Commercial and operational friction:
  - While CIS corporate could absorb setup fees (~£10,000–£17,000), ongoing monthly fees must be billed to individual operating sites. CIS strongly resisted the proposed £3,000/month fee, counter-proposing ~£500/month with capped support (e.g., 8 hours).
  - Technical requirements: Deploying in industrial canteens would necessitate custom firmware with auto-snapshot weighing (detecting scale weight stabilizing from zero to snap an automated picture without button presses) and disabling buttons to avoid worker confusion.
- Strategic decision: [[people/michael-michelis]] and Kostas concluded that building custom hardware and supporting enterprise canteen deployments is an extreme defocus from consumer DTC scale growth. Agreed to hold firm at the $3,000/month price point and allow CIS to decline.

### 2. Influencer Scale Delivery via Redo Inventory
- Scales dispatched to US creators directly from Redo returns stock arrived in just 5 days—substantially faster and cheaper than cross-border fulfillment from China.

### 3. Influencer Prospect Vetting & Creative Angles
- **Kelly / Morgan Official:** Creator dropped fee drastically from $1,750 to $250. Noted engagement is inconsistent across videos (mostly near zero except for high-protein/fiber bean recipes). Agreed to pass or strictly limit scope to fiber content.
- **The Glucose Warrior:** Reviewed content; determined video editing was too rapid and text-heavy with poor on-camera delivery. Passed.
- **Danielle Fitness (Bodybuilder):** Evaluated fitness/bodybuilding niche. Noted historical data shows bodybuilding content converts poorly with Qalzy's core demographic (perimenopausal women and older men). Passed.
- **Half-Marathon Runner (Video 6 Creator):** Received scale via Redo. Agreed top performing angles are "How I track nutrition while training for a half-marathon / run fuel" or "Healthy food swaps."
- **Calm and Strong Mama:** Creator departing for a 2.5-week trip to Japan; agreed not to rush content delivery.
- **Holiday Gifting Angles:** Brainstormed Christmas and New Year's resolution positioning: "The best gift you can give yourself this Christmas to stay lean and hit your New Year's resolutions."

### 4. App Development: Nutrition Insights & Healthy Swaps
- Michael shared progress on testing an in-app "Insights & Healthy Swaps" engine.
- Feature analyzes logged nutritional history, alerts users if they are off-target, suggests automated healthy ingredient swaps, and routes them directly into the AI Nutrition Advisory chat.
- Feature will be gated exclusively for premium subscription users.
