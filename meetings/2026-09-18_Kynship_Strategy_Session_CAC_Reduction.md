## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Account Economics & Agency Fit
- **Current Performance Baseline:** Qalzy spent ~$28,000 over the past month across Meta and Google, generating ~$47,000 in top-line revenue. High customer acquisition costs (CAC) are eating operating margins.
- **Kynship Fee Structure:** Kynship charges a monthly retainer of $20k–$25k. At Qalzy's current volume, agency fees would consume the majority of working capital without guaranteed 3x efficiency lift.
- **Scale Threshold:** Tom recommended re-engaging Kynship once Qalzy's monthly ad spend reaches $60k–$70k, at which point an incremental 10%–20% efficiency easily covers their retainer.

### 2. Meta Media Buying & Cost Control Architecture
- **Cost Controls (Cost Per Result Goal):**
  - Kynship runs ad accounts **exclusively on cost caps** rather than lowest-cost / daily spend budgets.
  - Caps are reverse-engineered directly from Shopify contribution margins and business P&L metrics rather than relying solely on in-platform ROAS.
- **Elimination of Auction Cannibalization:**
  - Emphasized that brands must **never run cost cap campaigns simultaneously alongside lowest-cost (highest volume) campaigns**.
  - Lowest-cost campaigns commit guaranteed daily spend and win priority in Meta's auction, relegating variable cost-cap bids to second tier and causing budget self-competition.
- **Ad Inventory Scale:** Typical Kynship client accounts maintain between **2,000 and 2,500 active ads** running concurrently under strict bid caps.

### 3. Creative Production & Organic Seeding Playbook
- **The 85/3 Rule of Creative Yield:** In high-scale DTC accounts, approximately **85% of total ad spend concentrates into just 3% of winning creative assets**. Scaling therefore requires high asset volume at low marginal cost.
- **Organic Product Seeding Funnel:**
  - **Outreach Volume:** 500 targeted influencer outreaches per month offering free product with no posting obligation ("We love what you do, here is a gift to help your routine").
  - **Acceptance Rate:** ~100 creators accept free product (cost is limited to COGS and shipping).
  - **Organic Posting Rate:** 35 to 40 creators share unprompted, organic reviews/unboxings.
  - **Usage Rights Conversion:** 80% of creators who post organically will grant full ad usage rights for free when approached post-factum. Conversely, asking creators for ad rights upfront triggers paid demand quotes from 80% of influencers.
- **Micro-Influencers vs. Celebrities:**
  - Micro-creators (<10k followers) drive far superior conversion rates because ad viewers respond to relatable authenticity.
  - Case Study: For a high-profile, Kardashian-owned brand managed by Kynship, an unpolished organic seeding video from an ordinary customer outperformed highly produced studio creative featuring Kris Jenner by **6x**.
