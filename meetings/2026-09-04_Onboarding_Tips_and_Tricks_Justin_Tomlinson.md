# Onboarding Tips & Tricks with Justin Tomlinson (Lucky Orange)
**Date:** 2026-09-04
**Attendees:** [[people/michael-michelis]], [[people/justin-tomlinson]]

## Summary
Michael attended an onboarding and walkthrough session with Justin Tomlinson from Lucky Orange following Qalzy's installation of the tool on their Shopify store (`nutrioscale.myshopify.com`). Justin demonstrated key platform features including visitor session recordings, heatmaps, scroll maps, Shopify conversion funnels, custom events, customer feedback surveys, and announcements.

## Key Topics & Platform Features
- **Visitor Session Recordings:** Automatic tracking of user visits, duration, location, and page navigation with playback to identify UX friction points.
- **Heatmaps & Scroll Maps:** Visualizes click density by device (desktop vs. mobile) and drop-off percentages as users scroll down pages.
- **Conversion Funnels:** Automated 3-step Shopify funnel (View Product → Add to Cart → Order) with drop-off recording filtering to diagnose checkout abandonment.
- **Custom Events:** In addition to standard Shopify events, custom tracking can be created for specific element interactions (button clicks) and URLs.
- **Surveys & Triggers:** Pop-up surveys (multiple choice, ratings, open text). Supports exit-intent triggers on desktop, or delayed triggers post "Add to Cart" to capture abandonment reasons.
- **API Availability:** Justin confirmed that Lucky Orange does not currently have a public API (aligns with viaSocket's integration status).
