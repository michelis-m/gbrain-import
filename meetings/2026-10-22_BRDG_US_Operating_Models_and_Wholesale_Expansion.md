---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Impending US Customs Regulatory Shift (November 30, 2026)
- **Executive Order Mandate:** By November 30, 2026, foreign importers of record will no longer be permitted to use informal entries or continuous customs bonds (requiring single-entry bonds or CTPAT-certified brokers).
- **US Shell Entity Crackdown:** Crucially, CBP rules specifically target newly formed US shell entities established solely to act as domestic importers of record without physical substance or staff. Consequently, forming a paper US LLC may not resolve customs clearance on its own.
- **Customs Broker Recommendation:** Sonia urged Qalzy to urgently speak with a second customs broker to clarify how foreign importers will be handled post-December 1, 2026 and examine single-entry bond costs.

### 2. Comparative Evaluation of US Operating Models
- **Model 1: Direct UK Ltd Sales (Foreign Importer):**
  - UK company bills US customers directly.
  - Files annual Form 1120-F protective return with Form 8833 claiming UK-US tax treaty protection (exempting US federal income tax provided no US permanent establishment exists).
  - Still triggers California state filing due to 3PL inventory.
- **Model 2: UK Ltd with US LLC (Disregarded Entity):**
  - US LLC owned 100% by UK parent; disregarded for federal income tax.
  - Activities reported on UK parent's Form 1120-F; LLC files pro forma Form 1120 + Form 5472.
  - Provides US commercial bank account, EIN, and domestic presence with minimal administrative friction; convertible to C-Corp later.
- **Model 3: UK Ltd with US C-Corporation Subsidiary:**
  - US C-Corp invoices US DTC customers and files federal + California corporation tax returns.
  - Requires transfer pricing: UK parent sells hardware to US sub at cost plus a 20–25% markup and recharges UK management/advertising overhead.
  - Delaware incorporation preferred for future US venture capital investment.

### 3. State Nexus & Banking Requirements
- **Physical Nexus in California:** Storing inventory at ShipBots in Gardena, CA creates immediate physical presence, mandating California corporate tax and sales tax compliance regardless of revenue volume.
- **US Address & Banking Prerequisites:** Recommended Opus Virtual Offices for a dedicated suite/lease, noting that US banks (Mercury, Brex) and payment processors (Stripe, Wise) reject generic virtual maildrops.
- **Service Partners:** Recommended Stripe Atlas for entity setup/EIN, Abacai or E-commerce Accountants for dual-jurisdiction bookkeeping, and automated sales tax software (~$250/mo).
