# Qalzy Ad Creative Review & Video QA

**Date:** 2026-10-16  
**Attendees:** [[people/michael-michelis]], [[people/kostas-koukoravas]]  
**Companies:** [[companies/qalzy]]  

---

## Summary
Michael Michelis and Kostas Koukoravas conducted an internal creative QA session reviewing newly assembled fitness and gym-oriented video ad cuts, focusing on visual narrative consistency, graphic overlay legibility, and product visibility rules for retargeting ads.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Visual-Narrative Mismatch (Protein Hook vs. Footage)
- **The Issue:** [[people/michael-michelis]] identified a critical continuity error in the current cut: while the voiceover emphasizes hitting high daily protein targets, the video B-roll cuts to a sweet dessert crepe and a muffin.
- **Required Edit:** The edit must incorporate actual high-protein meals (e.g. eggs, steak, Greek yogurt, protein bowls) to visually ground the nutrition claim.
- **Execution:** Michael noted that since the project is structured in CapCut, swapping the B-roll shots can be done quickly without re-rendering the entire timeline from scratch.

### 2. On-Screen Callout Overlays & Palette
- **Graphic Callouts:** [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] noted that some of the text callouts felt slightly distracting and disconnected from the hands/plate action.
- **Color Consistency:** Michael observed that the callout color palette matches the scale screen interface well, keeping brand cohesion even if layout adjustments are needed.

### 3. Bottom-of-Funnel Product Visibility Principles
- **Scale Framing:** Kostas raised concern that the physical scale is barely visible in several frames of the edit.
- **Retargeting Strategy:** Michael clarified that for bottom-of-the-funnel (retargeting) audiences who already know the product, full-frame scale visibility is not mandatory. Benchmark top performers frequently show only a corner of the scale while prioritizing the food logging workflow and core benefit.

---

## Action Items & Commitments
- **Michael Michelis:** Swap dessert/crepe B-roll with high-protein food footage in CapCut; adjust callout timing to align with plate action.

---

## Key Decisions
- Enforce strict alignment between voiceover claims and B-roll visuals (protein claims require protein visuals).
- Retain flexible product framing for bottom-of-funnel retargeting ads where benefit demonstration supersedes hardware close-ups.
