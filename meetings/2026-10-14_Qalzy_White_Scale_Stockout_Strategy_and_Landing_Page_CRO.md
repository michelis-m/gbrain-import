# Qalzy Strategy: White Scale Stockout Risk, Color Defaults, and Landing Page Redesign

**Date:** 2026-10-14  
**Attendees:** [[people/michael-michelis]], [[people/kostas-koukoravas]]  
**Companies:** [[companies/qalzy]]  

---

## Summary
Michael Michelis and Kostas Koukoravas conducted an in-depth operational and CRO review addressing impending inventory depletion of white scales ahead of Q4/Black Friday, evaluated A/B testing color defaults to balance SKU velocity, and finalized Figma design adjustments for nutrition callouts on the landing page.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Inventory Balancing & Stockout Prevention (Black vs. White)
- **Impending White Scale Stockout:** With Q4 sales velocity rising toward Black Friday and Christmas, current inventory models indicate white scales will stock out weeks before the next production run lands (a new 10,000-unit batch takes ~2 months to manufacture).
- **Color Preference Dynamics:** Discussed why white scales currently represent the vast majority of orders. On Kickstarter, black originally outsold white because it was the default hero image; currently, all ads and product page defaults feature white.
- **Default Switch Experimentation:**
  - Evaluated switching the PDP default color from white to black to steer demand toward black stock without hurting overall conversion.
  - Formulated two hypotheses to test: (1) Does defaulting to black preserve conversion while balancing inventory drawdown? (2) If white goes completely out of stock, how much would conversion drop?
  - Long-term manufacturing implications: In future large runs (e.g. 10,000 units), transitioning to a single color (white) would simplify inventory management, lower supplier unit costs, and eliminate historical surface finish defects associated with black units.

### 2. Ending Current A/B Test & Launching New Page Variant
- **Concluding Existing Test:** Reviewed the active PDP test, which proved marginally positive to indifferent. Agreed to conclude the test formally to avoid lingering split-traffic artifacts.
- **Gradual Rollout:** Decided to launch the new comprehensive CRO redesign at a **20% traffic allocation** initially (escalating to 50% or 100% once stability is confirmed), rather than risking a full 100% switch immediately before feedback arrives.

### 3. Landing Page Nutrition & Macro Visuals
- Reviewed Figma mockups for visual nutrient callouts.
- Agreed to incorporate circular macro/micronutrient breakdown graphics (rings/bars representing vitamins and minerals) inspired directly by the Qalzy mobile app UI, giving visitors an immediate visual grasp of the app's scanning depth.
