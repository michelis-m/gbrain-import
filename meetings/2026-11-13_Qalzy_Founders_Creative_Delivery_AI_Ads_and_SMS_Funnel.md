## Key Discussion Points

### 1. US Inventory Liquidation & Print Collateral
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] emphasized that liquidating US inventory remains the single highest operational priority to ensure cash-flow sustainability and reach profitability.
- Follow-ups pending with Indian distribution partners regarding the US sales strategy.
- Kostas confirmed intent to order printed promotional flyers to bundle into scale shipments.

### 2. SMS Capture Funnel Strategy
- Evaluated implementing SMS capture to build an owned remarketing list:
  - Agreed to postpone active testing until after Black Friday to avoid introducing friction to the primary holiday checkout flow.
  - [[people/michael-michelis]] recommended a two-step capture mechanic: *"Enter your email to unlock your reward; enter phone number optionally for instant VIP tracking/bonuses"* to maximize email opt-ins without tanking lead conversion.

### 3. SPG Creative Delivery Backlog & Fee Renegotiation
- Addressed severe delays in creative asset deliverables from [[companies/social-paradigm-group]]:
  - SPG is contracted to deliver 2 briefs (approx. 20 assets) per week, but has accumulated a multi-week backlog.
  - [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] proposed a direct commercial ultimatum: if SPG cannot fulfill their agreed creative output volume, Qalzy will reduce their monthly retainer fee proportionally and reallocate that budget to independent UGC creators who deliver on schedule.
  - Discussed building pre-sale advertorial landing pages and dedicating targeted ad spend to test this unproven acquisition angle.

### 4. Meta Ad Performance Paradox: Faces vs. Faceless Demos
- Analyzed surprising creative performance trends in the Meta Ads Manager:
  - Creator videos featuring human talking heads are failing to generate purchases.
  - Top-converting ad creatives are consistently faceless videos showcasing close-up food, plate measurements, and the physical scale camera in action.
  - Hypothesized that unfamiliar hardware scanning food acts as a disruptive, curiosity-driven scroll-stopper ("What is that device?"), whereas talking heads immediately trigger ad-blindness.
  - Decided to reinvest in variations of the proven *"I eat a little vs. I eat a lot"* concept featuring nutritionist comparisons.

### 5. Generative AI Video Ad Workflows & Drama Script Archetypes
- Evaluated AI video production tooling:
  - Assessed subscriptions and credit usage across Higgsfield AI ($50 tier), Google Gemini, Seedance (V031 model), and Claude.
  - [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] shared experiences using Claude to iterate visual prompts autonomously for image/video engines.
  - Deconstructed high-performing mobile app drama hooks (Muscle Booster, Tai Chi programs, weight loss soap-opera scripts: *"Lady, you almost crushed me"*, *"Unrecognizable by Christmas"*, *"Impressing the ex"*).
  - Discussed translating core hooks into localized languages for broad international distribution.

### 6. Order Fulfillment & Support: Orders #2770 & #2771
- Reviewed support scenario where a customer placed two consecutive orders 15 minutes apart (Order #2770 for a scale, #2771 for a travel case):
  - Portless proposed cancelling #2771 and appending the item to #2770 to ship in a single parcel.
  - Founders decided against manual cancellation to prevent confusing the customer with automated Shopify cancellation notifications; orders will ship separately with independent tracking.

### 7. Landing Page Development & Tooling
- [[people/michael-michelis]] noted that editing landing page components via Cursor and Claude Opus depleted 50% of monthly token allowances due to large codebase context ingestion.
