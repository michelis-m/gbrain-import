# Qalzy Founders Sync: Video Ad Hook Pacing, Static Creative Priority & Black Friday Scaling

**Date:** 2026-11-26  
**Attendees:** [[people/kostas-koukoravas]], [[people/michael-michelis]]  

---

## Summary
Midday sync between [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] and [[people/michael-michelis]] reviewing creative video ad iterations, marketing materials, and ad budget pacing ahead of Black Friday. The founders critiqued a slow video creative that delayed product introduction, prioritized static ad revisions over video edits, discussed delayed print collateral, and debated risk tolerance regarding Meta ad budget increases.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Video Ad Critique & Pacing Flaws
- Reviewed a newly submitted UGC/concept video ad:
  - **Pacing & Hook Failure:** The video opens with slow personal storytelling (*"Dinner for the people I love"*, *"cooking in everyday life"*) and does not show or explain the Qalzy smart scale until 23–28 seconds into the video.
  - Both founders agreed that social feed users will scroll away long before the product is introduced; ads must hook viewers and feature the physical product and AI quick-scan demonstration within the first 3 seconds.
  - Video text headings and title cards appeared awkward and artificial, resembling unedited AI-generated copy.

### 2. Static Ads Prioritized Over Video
- Decided to instruct the creative team/editors to address comments on static image ads first, where modifications are faster and have historically driven predictable Black Friday conversion.
- Paused further iteration on the slow video creative until the core script is restructured around immediate product demonstrations.

### 3. Print Collateral Delays
- Noted that promotional flyers and printed materials (intended for CES and marketing distribution) are delayed from the printer despite paying expedited rush delivery fees.

### 4. Black Friday Ad Spend & Risk Tolerance
- Discussed Meta ad budget scaling: [[people/michael-michelis]] increased spend yesterday by $600 to capitalize on rising pre-Black Friday demand (*"high risk, high reward"*).
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] expressed caution about scaling too aggressively without guaranteed conversion efficiency, but acknowledged strong overall top-line revenue momentum.
- Evaluated customer response to the Black Friday promotional bundle including Lifetime App Premium access.

---

## Commitments & Action Items
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]]: Instruct creative team to implement revisions on static ads and pause revisions on the delayed-hook video.
- [[people/michael-michelis]]: Monitor ad spend velocity and hourly conversion metrics following recent budget lifts.
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]]: Follow up on delayed print flyer shipments.

---

## Key Decisions
- Reject/pause video creatives that do not display the scale and core AI scanning feature within the opening 3 seconds.
- Focus creative team effort on high-converting static image ads for the immediate Black Friday push.
