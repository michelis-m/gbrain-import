## Executive Summary
Michael Michelis and Kostas Koukoravas held a short sync to review video ad script refinements, hook wording, nutrient callout timing, and B-roll shot selection for top-of-funnel DTC video ads.

---

## Key Discussion Points
- **Tactile Hook Selection:** Michael recommended testing the hook line: *"No more sauce-covered fingers smudging your screen,"* emphasizing that it addresses a visceral, relatable friction point for active home cooks using smartphone calorie apps.
- **Nutrient Callout Pacing:** Rather than displaying nutrient callouts over a talking-head shot, the team agreed to overlay graphical callouts (sodium, fiber, saturated fat, vitamin D) directly onto cooking B-roll footage.
- **Demographic Representation:** Decided to incorporate B-roll of an elderly woman cooking to directly appeal to Qalzy's primary 45+ demographic segment.
- **Editing Tools:** Discussed using CapCut MCP versus Open Montage for script rendering and auto-captions.
