### 2. Trustpilot 1-Star Review Dispute Management
- **Context & Incident:**
  - A German backer (Michael J. Fuchs) posted a 1-star review on Trustpilot 14 hours prior (~1 hour after sending an initial email to the founder), claiming product inaccuracies (gingerbread scanned as a burger/beef patty) and lack of instant response.
  - Order verification confirmed the backer purchased through Kickstarter / Pledgebox and received delivery.
- **Evaluation of Response Options:**
  - **Option 1 (Trustpilot Platform Report):** Report the review as fake or "not based on a genuine experience" or containing personal information.
    - *Risk Assessment:* Kostas noted that reporting reviews on Trustpilot often fails unless clear doxing or violation occurs. Furthermore, failing an official dispute can flag the business account, making subsequent review removals significantly more difficult.
  - **Option 2 (Direct Customer Negotiation via Email):** Send a firm yet professional email acknowledging their frustration, presenting AWS/scan proof, and offering return label issuance and a full refund strictly conditioned on the customer withdrawing the defamatory review.
  - **Option 3 (Public Rebuttal):** Reply publicly on Trustpilot stating that the backer is an early Kickstarter backer who did not pay retail software fees and misstated support response timelines.
- **Decision:**
  - Avoid risking an official Trustpilot dispute flag.
  - Draft a carefully worded private email offering to facilitate the return and refund provided the review is taken down first.
