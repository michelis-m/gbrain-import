## Executive Summary
Michael Michelis and Kostas Koukoravas met to address customer return requests driven by lack of user understanding, strategizing a mandatory onboarding accuracy guide/tutorial. ---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Product Usability vs. User Education & Onboarding Flow
- **Return Pressure & User Sentiment:** Kostas raised that several return requests stem from users struggling to operate the scale, not hardware or software deficiencies. Both agreed that family members (e.g., parents) use it successfully once they put in minimal effort.
- **Mandatory Education Gate:** Discussed implementing a mandatory onboarding tutorial or interactive step-by-step test in the app before the scale is enabled (e.g., requiring users to understand tare, multi-ingredient vs. whole-meal tracking, and zeroing). Kostas subsequently deployed an "Accuracy Guide Onboarding Preview" in Klaviyo outlining the three tracking modes (whole-meal scan, ingredient-by-ingredient, and saved recipes).

### 2. High-Volume Ad Creatives & Visual Styling
- **Volume Strategy:** Reviewed the upcoming launch of ~30 new ad variations, aiming to identify top-performing hooks and visual formats.
- **Aesthetic Refinement:** Noted that the new creative assets have a cleaner, high-contrast look reminiscent of TCF (clean direct-to-consumer aesthetic).
- **Previous Iteration Fixes:** Confirmed that prior feedback—where ad visuals featured meals missing protein or relied heavily on generic stock elements—has been resolved in the latest cuts.

### 3. Customer Reviews Integration & Value Proposition Framing
- **Social Proof Assets:** Analyzed customer reviews from the App Store and Google Play, including an enthusiastic 5-star review from user Luke.
- **Portion Control vs. Effortless Tracking:** Evaluated a customer testimonial highlighting live macro updates (*"I can adjust my portion before I eat it. Watching the macros update live as I add food is what sold me"*).
- **Messaging Debates:** Michael and Kostas debated where this review fits best—whether under "effortless tracking" or "efficiency and convenience." Michael advocated focusing on efficiency and portion control clarity rather than conflating distinct value propositions.

### 4. Video Editing & Pacing QA
- **Cut Frequency & Camera Motion:** Kostas shared a work-in-progress video ad draft. Michael pointed out that the opening sequence had excessive, rapid cuts and felt disjointed.
- **Stabilization:** Recommended stabilizing shaky footage and smoothing out camera transitions to ensure the viewer can follow the tracking interface clearly.
