## Key Audit Findings & Strategy Recommendations

### 1. Meta Ads Strategy & Account Structure
- **Campaign Optimization:** Qalzy currently runs ABO (Ad Set Budget Optimization), which SPG considers outdated and restrictive. SPG strongly advises shifting 80–90% of budget into CBO (Advantage Campaign Budget) and ASC, letting the Meta algorithm optimize budget distribution.
- **Ad Cut-off / Kill Rules:** Charlie strongly advised against Qalzy's current practice of manually killing ads at 1x target CPA or based on early lack of purchases:
  - Meta optimizes for overall conversion volume across the whole funnel, not isolated ad-level CPA.
  - Upper-funnel volume ads drive clicks, impressions, and awareness; lower-funnel ads capture the conversion. Killing top-spending ads forces budget to progressively worse creative assets.
  - Recommended approach: Align with the algorithm using Meta Cost Caps ($250 cap) if strict CPA control is required, rather than manually switching off volume-driving ads.
  - Attribution window caution: Qalzy has a 2–3 week customer decision journey; evaluating ad performance on a <7-day click basis yields inaccurate conclusions.
- **Placement Distribution (Reels):** Reels placement currently accounts for only 25% of spend. SPG noted Reels delivers the lowest CPMs and CPCs, but requires fast-paced, TikTok-native UGC content rather than feed-style ads.
- **Retargeting & Exclusions:**
  - Recommended eliminating separate retargeting campaigns for early-stage brands; the pixel already retargets high-intent users naturally within broad campaigns.
  - Essential to set up dynamic Klaviyo exclusion lists to prevent wasted ad spend on existing customers.
  - Recommended configuring Meta Audience Segments (Net New, Engaged, Existing Customers) in ad account settings.
- **Partnership Ads / Whitelisting:** Highlighted as the single biggest lever to lower CPMs ($166) and CPCs ($7). SPG observes direct correlation across 72 client accounts between higher partnership ad spend and lower acquisition costs.
- **Funnel & Offer Diversification:**
  - Recommended testing advertorial / listicle pre-sale pages (e.g. informative articles highlighting weight loss/cortisol pain points) before directing traffic to the $199–$254 PDP.
  - Advised bundling free gifts / starter packs to boost perceived value without heavily discounting product margin.

### 2. Creative Pipeline
- Need to expand creative persona coverage beyond narrow fitness segments to include busy parents, general health, menopause/weight management, and nutrition professionals.
- SPG delivers 80 creative assets per month (20 new assets/week), repurposing raw UGC B-roll into varied angles, hooks, and Reels-native cuts.

### 3. Google Ads & Klaviyo Email Audit
- **Google Ads:** Tracking cleanup required immediately (duplicate conversion tags, WeTrack.io overspill). Urged separating Brand and Non-Brand search campaigns (brand spend should only be 3–5% of Google budget).
- **Email (Klaviyo):** Add exit conditions to welcome series once a purchase occurs to prevent post-purchase discounting (e.g. Email 4 discounts). Build a site abandonment flow and tighten unengaged customer segmentation.
