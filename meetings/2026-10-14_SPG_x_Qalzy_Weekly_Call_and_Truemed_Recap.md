## Summary
Michael Michelis and Kostas Koukoravas held their weekly strategic sync with Syed Hussain of Social Paradigm Group (SPG), briefing him on the Truemed HSA/FSA partnership, reviewing ad performance and checkout funnel conversion drops, and debating CRO testing methodology and traffic allocation.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Truemed HSA/FSA Integration Briefing
- Michael and Kostas shared key takeaways from their Truemed kickoff call.
- Syed expressed strong enthusiasm for the partnership, noting that enabling HSA/FSA pre-tax purchasing will serve as a potent conversion lever and marketing hook for US ad campaigns (effectively offering a 30%–40% tax discount backed by medical necessity).

### 2. Funnel Conversion Drop Analysis
- The team analyzed the recent dip in checkout conversion rate.
- Discussed whether the removal of Checkout Plus (which unbundled subscriptions from default carts) triggered the conversion drop or if broader ad fatigue played a role. Michael emphasized that removing multiple variables simultaneously makes root cause attribution challenging.

### 3. CRO Testing Methodology & Black vs. White Inventory Urgency
- **Methodology Debate:** Syed reiterated his preference for isolated, single-variable tests to isolate statistical winners, cautioning that large multi-change tests historically have high failure rates. Michael countered with a portfolio approach: implementing high-conviction aggregate UX improvements quickly, as sequential 1-by-1 tests take too long heading into Q4.
- **Traffic Allocation Agreement:** Agreed to roll out the new CRO page with an initial **20% traffic allocation**, monitoring user session recordings and heatmaps before scaling to 40%–50%.
- **Inventory Balancing Test:** Kostas emphasized the urgency of running the Black vs. White PDP default test to curb white scale runout before holiday peak season. Agreed to prioritize CRO stability first and initiate inventory balancing tests immediately thereafter.
