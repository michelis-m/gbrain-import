### 3. Website CRO & Customer Objections Analysis
- **Customer Survey Findings (Reasons for Not Purchasing):**
  - **54%:** Worried about recurring subscriptions (dominant #1 concern).
  - **44%:** Lack of confidence in AI accuracy (#2 concern).
  - **18%:** Shipping costs.
  - **18%:** Existing kitchen scale is sufficient.
  - **18%:** Seeking more customer reviews / proof.
  - **9%:** Delivery transit time (6–10 days).
- **Addressing Subscription Fears:**
  - Evaluated whether to prominently state *"No Subscription Required / Free App Included"* or if over-emphasizing subscriptions inadvertently triggers user worry.
  - Agreed that keeping the *"Do I need a subscription?"* FAQ open by default is essential since it is the top documented objection.
  - Considered hiding the *"How fast will I get my order (6–10 days)"* FAQ item to reduce pre-purchase hesitation.
- **Addressing Accuracy Concerns:**
  - Agreed to design a dedicated section on *"How accurate is Qalzy"* explaining ingredient detection, mixed meal handling, and USDA nutritional database matching.
