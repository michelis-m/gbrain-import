# Qalzy Founders Sync: Mobile App UI Dark Mode, A/B Testing Pause, Creator Scraping & Black Friday Prep

**Date:** 2026-11-26  
**Attendees:** [[people/michael-michelis]], [[people/kostas-koukoravas]]  

---

## Summary
Morning founders sync between [[people/michael-michelis]] and [[people/kostas-koukoravas]]. The founders reviewed a new mobile app build featuring dark mode, evaluated current landing page CRO experiments, discussed YouTube creator outreach automation and Shopify Collabs setup, analyzed Black Friday promotional readiness and creative ad hooks, and addressed an Android subscription customer support issue.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Mobile App UI Redesign & Dark Mode
- Reviewed the latest mobile app redesign build:
  - **Dark Mode:** Received enthusiastically by both founders; described as sleek, modern, and visually compelling.
  - **Light / Non-Dark Mode:** Colors appear somewhat washed out with a greenish tint; lacks the visual contrast and impact of dark mode. Icons for *Diary*, *Insights*, and *Recipes* were updated.
  - **Action:** Agreed to create a separate branch, commit dark mode, and conduct further design iterations on light mode before general release.

### 2. US Thanksgiving Holiday Slowdown & Legal/Patent Filings
- Noted that US business operations and partners are largely shut down for the Thanksgiving holiday (Thursday/Friday).
- Discussed feedback from patent attorney regarding USPTO filing requirements: to use USPTO as a receiving office, the applicant must have a US citizen/permanent resident or local office presence, otherwise applications risk being rerouted to WIPO with forwarding fees.

### 3. A/B Testing & Analytics Dashboards
- **Landing Page A/B Test:** Current experiment showed high variance in the control group while add-to-cart rates remained unchanged, rendering results inconclusive. [[people/michael-michelis]] decided to pause the current test and launch a new test focusing on image carousels and visual assets.
- **Analytics Anomaly:** Noted that the Absolutely dashboard is reporting inflated figures (~$200k monthly revenue) due to attribution/extrapolation quirks.

### 4. Creator Outreach Automation & Shopify Collabs
- [[people/michael-michelis]] built an automated YouTube discovery script that searches YouTube, identifies relevant creator channels, extracts business contact emails, and drafts outreach pitches.
- Set up the internal **Shopify Collabs** program to issue discount codes and provide creators with a dedicated dashboard to track referral orders and commissions, eliminating recurring third-party platform software fees (only a 2.9% payment processing fee on creator payouts).

### 5. Black Friday Page Elements & Creative Ad Strategy
- **On-Page Assets:** Adding navigational arrows to the product image carousel; mounting a countdown timer on the PDP highlighting the Black Friday sale deadline (ending tomorrow night). Discussed replacing text-heavy "How It Works" sections with visual graphics.
- **Top-Performing Ad Hooks:** Identified the 3 strongest hooks favored by Meta algorithms:
  1. *"Losing weight has never been easier"*
  2. *"Calorie counting is hard"*
  3. *"This is how much calories..."*
- Highlighted the urgent need for authentic holiday unboxing videos before Christmas shipping deadlines.
- **Month-to-Date Revenue:** Sales reached ~$97,000 month-to-date, recovering ground from slower previous months.

### 6. App Store Approval & Android Play Store Support
- iOS app update was reviewed and approved promptly by Apple; Kostas preparing deployment.
- Reviewed a support inquiry from customer David Hepker regarding an Android Google Play subscription where Google refunded his purchase because premium features failed to activate; Michael checked Play Console transaction logs and confirmed backend verification failed to unlock the entitlement.
