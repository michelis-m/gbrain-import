### 2. Media Buying Strategy & Account Restructure ([[people/syed-hussain]])
- **CBO Migration:** Strongly recommended transitioning the existing account from ABO to CBO immediately to eliminate fragmentation and prevent auction overlap.
- **Reviving Winning Personas:** Syed reviewed historical ads and identified strong performers that had been turned off prematurely by Blazer despite significant spend (notably ads addressing insulin management and GLP-1 weight loss). These will be reactivated.
- **Demographic Focus (50+):** Highlighted that Qalzy's strongest converting demographic is aged 50+. Recommended addressing their specific daily friction points.
- **Lowering CPMs & CPCs:** Plan to deploy whitelisted partnership ads and introduce pre-sale advertorials and landing page CRO to boost conversion rates (CVR).
- **Consolidation:** Ad sets will be consolidated to maintain clean delivery while respecting Meta's 50-ad limit per ad set.

### 3. Creative Flywheel & Andromeda Diversity (Sol)
- **Algorithmic Alignment:** Meta's Andromeda algorithm requires constant surplus creative diversity (both messaging and visual) to deliver personalized ads to different micro-segments.
- **Funnel Stages & Hooks:**
  - *Problem-Unaware:* Focus on "invisible calories" (e.g. visualizing how 1 chocolate bar equals 7 apples in calories), triggering realization and impulse interest.
  - *Problem-Aware:* Highlight scale features vs phone app tracking limitations.
  - *Personas:* Weight loss / GLP-1 patients, gym goers / protein trackers, and busy parents meal prepping.
- **Creator Pipeline:** Emphasized building a B-roll content bank via low-cost UGC creators (Fiverr, Billow) focusing on daily morning/kitchen routines, paired with micro-influencers for partnership ads. Turnaround for initial batch is 4–5 days with same-day amends from SPG's in-house creative team.

### 4. Email Lifecycle Strategy (Leanne)
- Klaviyo audit of core flows (welcome series, abandoned cart, post-purchase review collection).
- Monthly campaign calendar outlining themes, target segments, and Figma designs for founder approval.

### 5. Commercials, Transition & Next Steps
- **Unit Economics:** Michael shared that Qalzy COGS is ~$45 (including delivery and fees). App subscription LTV is currently estimated at ~$16/user, which Qalzy keeps as an internal safety margin buffer rather than factoring into breakeven CPA calculations.
- **Blazer Cutover Plan:** To eliminate "too many chefs in the kitchen," Blazer will be halted from all media buying immediately and directed to utilize remaining contracted credits solely on creative video production through September. SPG will assume full media buying control immediately once onboarded.
- **Onboarding Logistics:** Jess Brown will send over the PandaDoc agreement, Stripe payment link, and onboarding questionnaire today.
