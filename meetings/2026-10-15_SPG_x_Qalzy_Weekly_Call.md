## Key Discussion Points

### 1. US 3PL Transition & Unit Economics
- **Margin Impact:** Moving inventory to a domestic US 3PL is modeled to deliver at least a **7% boost to gross margins** purely through lower domestic shipping rates compared to international air transit from China, alongside higher conversion rates from faster delivery times.
- **Current Blocker:** Awaiting palletizing and freight-prep cost quotes from current warehouse partner Portless (CSM [[people/ryan-torreta]]).
- **Timeline:** The next new manufacturing run is confirmed to ship directly to the US. Transitioning existing inventory will take approximately two months from transit to full warehouse availability.

### 2. Meta Ads Pricing Copy Alignment & Spend Throttling
- **Bottom-of-Funnel Discount Consistency:** Syed identified that several active retargeting ads and headlines still referenced outdated promotional discounts (33% and 43% off, or old flash sale copy). Current store promotional pricing is set at **42% off**. Syed will gradually update top-of-funnel headlines and systematically replace bottom-of-funnel creatives.
- **Budget Reduction:** Agreed to scale daily Meta ad spend back down to **,250/day** (from higher tests) while landing page optimization and variant default A/B tests are underway.
- **CPA Realities:** Triple Whale reported Meta CPA around **37**, higher than target scaling levels, though blended CPA remains lower due to organic and app re-engagement.

### 3. Product Accuracy Positioning & Managing Customer Expectations
- **The Core Problem:** Return rates have edged up to 4% (against the 3% benchmark for 30-day trial models). Root cause analysis reveals new users (who have never tracked macros) expect "magic" one-click tracking on complex, hidden, or messy dishes (e.g. sandwiches with hidden spreads or mixed stews).
- **Technical Architecture Clarification:**
  - **Vision Model (Google Gemini):** Identifies visible foods and ingredients on the plate.
  - **Nutritional Database (USDA / Official Food Databases):** Nutritional macros are pulled strictly from verified databases, avoiding LLM calorie hallucinations.
  - **Weight vs Volume:** Traditional phone camera apps estimate food volume from pixels (highly inaccurate). Qalzy measures exact total physical plate weight, drastically reducing estimation error.
  - **One-Tap vs. Ingredient-by-Ingredient (Auto-Tare):** The scale's proprietary feature allows building a meal on a single plate where the scale auto-tares and measures each consecutive item incrementally (100% precision).
- **Creative Strategy Shift:**
  - Syed proposed leaning directly into consumer skepticism with provocative, honest hooks: *"We proved this AI scale is a scam"* or *"Can an AI scale see Nutella inside a sandwich?"*.
  - Showcases real-world usage: highlighting how simple it is to log 90% of a meal instantly and tap once on the phone to correct any misidentified ingredient.
  - Syed will brief SPG creative lead Saul upon his return next week to design accuracy-focused video assets.
