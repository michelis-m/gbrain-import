## Key Discussion Points

### 1. EU Battery Directive & DTC Geographic Focus
- **Regulatory Headwinds:** In-depth review of incoming EU battery regulations confirmed severe compliance hurdles for sealed consumer electronics. Redesigning hardware to incorporate removable batteries is technically unviable at Qalzy's current stage.
- **Strategic Direction:** Rather than attempting piecemeal European shipping via DHL from personal residences or small EU hubs, Qalzy will formally prioritize the massive US market (00M+ opportunity).

### 2. Inventory Allocation Formula & Absolutely A/B Test
- **A/B Test Early Trend:** In the ongoing Absolutely A/B test (evaluating Black vs. White scale variants), the Black scale leads 3-to-1 (+200% conversion advantage on a preliminary sample of 8 orders across 400 visitors).
- **Paralysis vs. Shipping Savings:** Michael emphasized that pausing ocean freight to wait for absolute statistical significance is counterproductive: shipping direct via Portless air burns an extra ~0 per unit in shipping costs (costing ~,000 every 10 days of delay).
- **Agreed Allocation Formula:** Rather than guessing future sales ratios, Qalzy will divide current remaining inventory proportionally (e.g. 40–50% across both Black and White SKUs) and ship that balanced ratio to ShipBots US. If White units sell out faster domestically, Portless China will serve as an immediate air backfill buffer.

### 3. Factory Production & Quality Assurance (Mei)
- **Production Lead Times:** Order must be placed with manufacturer Mei immediately to ensure units arrive before February stockout risk.
- **Fast-Tracking Certification:** Agreed to skip redundant product re-certifications since hardware changes are low risk (buzzer removal and camera upgrade), saving 2–3 weeks of lead time.
- **Quality Control Safeguards:** Golden samples functioned properly, but past mass batches exhibited loose/floppy camera alignment and barcode focus issues. Qalzy will require high-resolution photographic proof of barcode and food recognition tests before dispatch, supplemented by a paid third-party on-site inspection of the finished lot.

### 4. Portless Shenzhen Warehouse Palletization
