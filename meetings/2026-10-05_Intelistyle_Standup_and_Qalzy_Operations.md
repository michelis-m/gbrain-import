### 2. Qalzy Unit Economics & Returns Strategy
- **Return Volumes & Impact:**
  - Total sales stand at 413 units to date.
  - Current return pipeline: 8 completed returns and 10 units in transit (~4.3% overall return rate).
  - Discussed recent cancellation and return case from Latvia where customer requested cancellation immediately after international dispatch ("impulse purchase").
- **Free Returns Policy Debate:**
  - European/Netherlands fulfillment logistics mean international returns eat significantly into product margins (especially without a dedicated local returns warehouse).
  - Evaluated whether to remove free returns or introduce return shipping fees to deter impulse purchase returns, while balancing conversion friction.
  - Kostas emphasized monitoring return metrics closely as a key profitability lever.

### 3. Financial & Advertising Performance Trends
- **August vs. September Comparison:**

  - September blended ROAS improved to **1.56x – 1.6x** following an additional ~100k impressions spend.
  - Net margin closed at approximately **5.84%** (leaving limited margin buffer at a $142–$143 CPA).
  - Discussed feasibility of retail vs. direct-to-consumer: retail distribution requires complex integrations and scale that current volume does not yet justify.

### 5. Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) & A/B Testing
- **Social Proof Popups ("Someone just purchased"):**
  - Michael proposed testing real-time social proof notifications ("someone just purchased", "someone just left a 5-star review") to boost conversion confidence.
  - Kostas noted he had previously authored custom code for this prior to the Kickstarter campaign, and suggested reviewing native Shopify apps vs. custom scripts.
- **Scientific A/B Testing Discipline:**
  - Agreed strictly to isolate and test site optimizations one by one rather than bundling multiple changes simultaneously, allowing at least 7 days to achieve statistical maturity. Bundling risks confounding positive and negative effects.
  - Mobile layout evaluation: mobile view requires excessive vertical scrolling, which may increase drop-off before visitors reach value-proposition copy.
