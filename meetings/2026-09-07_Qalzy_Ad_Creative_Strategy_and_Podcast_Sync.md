## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Ad Account Fatigue & Creative Scaling
- **Creative Volume Deficit:** Noted that direct competitors run hundreds of active ad creatives (e.g., 300+) whereas Qalzy currently has around 20. Rapid creative production and iteration is essential.
- **Hook Iteration on Proven Assets:** The primary top-performing video (scale identification / "This is crunchy" hardware demo) saturated at ~16k audience reach due to algorithmic capping tied to that specific hook.
  - Priority is to retain the converting core body of the video while testing at least 3 brand new 3-second opening hooks to force the algorithm to serve the asset into different audience clusters.
- **Advantage+ Shopping Campaigns (Shopping Plus):** Evaluated launching Meta Advantage+ Shopping campaigns loaded with proven creatives to target bottom-of-funnel buyers without audience retargeting constraints.

### 2. Angles, Hooks & Formats
- **Health & Condition-Specific Angles:** Testing hooks specifically addressing chronic health goals:
  - *"If you have high cholesterol, this is for you."*
  - *"And if you have diabetes, this is for you."* (Matches recent influencer outreach to [[people/vanessa-haydock]]).
  - Longevity / Mediterranean angle (e.g., Ikaria Blue Zone longevity secrets).
- **Addressing Objections & Comments:** Producing video responses directly addressing common objections from ads/support (e.g., why physical buttons matter, why manual app logging fails, accuracy, ease of use).
- **Reaction-Style Hooks & Authentic Settings:** Testing reaction openers ("What is this?") and shooting in varied non-polished environments (e.g., beaches, outdoors, raw lifestyle settings) rather than purely staged studio setups.
