- **Timeline Copy Optimization:** Evaluated the phrasing for outcome guarantees (*"See results in 90 days"* vs. *"3 months"*). Adopted "90 days" in accordance with CRO guidance, as days feel more immediate and actionable than months.

  - Reviewed copy around observational study results. Flagged and stripped AI-generated draft disclaimers mentioning a lack of statistical significance.
  - Emphasized strict advertising compliance (FTC / consumer protection standards prohibiting misleading "clinical" claims). Restricted clickable external links to the specific study anchor text rather than large interactive blocks.
