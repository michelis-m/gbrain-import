## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Wi-Fi 2.4GHz Router Troubleshooting & Support Policy
- **Customer Return Friction:** A customer requested to return their Qalzy scale after becoming frustrated trying to configure their Wi-Fi router for 2.4GHz compatibility. The user had difficulty navigating a router admin portal.
- **Support Boundary Definition:** Kostas pointed out that modern routers (including Amazon Eero, Verizon, etc.) almost universally support 2.4GHz and are managed via mobile apps rather than complex IP gateway settings. Qalzy support cannot act as a generic ISP helpdesk for every router make and model.
- **Fin AI Prompt Update:** Agreed to refine the automated [[companies/fin-intercom]] response template:
  - Advise customers that all modern Wi-Fi routers support 2.4GHz.
  - Instruct customers unsure about their dual-band configuration to contact their Internet Service Provider (ISP) directly to enable 2.4GHz, preventing user fatigue and premature returns.

### 2. Ad Creative Experimentation: Narrative Drama & AI Stories
- **Emerging Ad Formats:** Michael noted high engagement on Twitter/Meta with serialized AI drama ads featuring episodic narrative scripts (e.g., a dialogue-driven "7-Minute Military Calisthenics" routine between neighbors).
- **Application to Qalzy:** Discussed whether similar episodic storytelling, conversational dialogues, or informational video formats could effectively communicate Qalzy's value proposition without feeling generic.

### 3. Native App Onboarding & Connection Guidance
- **In-App Troubleshooting:** Kostas is reviewing the scale pairing experience in the Qalzy mobile app to implement built-in troubleshooting steps, giving users direct self-serve solutions during setup before they encounter connectivity dead-ends.
