---

## Summary
Brief midday creative and technical check-in between Qalzy co-founders. Michael shared a high-energy music/pop ad creative concept ("banger") for the mobile app, and founders discussed creative testing angles. Kostas and Michael also evaluated testing hardware needs for the latest iOS builds, agreeing to source a refurbished/used iPad rather than purchasing an expensive brand-new unit from Apple.

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. High-Energy Music Ad Creative
- Michael shared a dynamic, pop-music ad video concept with Kostas ("Sound on, full on").
- Kostas praised the energy ("It's full pop, love it").
- Founders agreed that with a strong creative strategy, polished B-rolls, and clear subtitle overlays, this style can perform very well as a top-of-funnel creative variation.
- Michael noted the potential to iterate automated/AI creative generation into compelling ad hooks.

### 2. Testing Hardware: iPad for iOS Compatibility
- Discussed the need for an iPad running the latest version of iOS to test upcoming app layouts, UI responsive scaling, and new features.
- Brand-new iPads from Apple are cost-prohibitive for dedicated device testing (£300-£400+).
- Agreed to source a used or refurbished modern iPad (e.g. from eBay) with backward compatibility that supports recent iOS versions.
- Discussed dual utility for home/family use (allowing kids to use iPads without monopolizing work phones or computers for gaming like Roblox).
