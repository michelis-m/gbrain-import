# Qalzy Ad Creative Review: Subtitle Legibility, Editing Standards & Video Feedback

**Date:** 2026-11-03  
**Attendees:** [[people/michael-michelis]], [[people/kostas-koukoravas]]  

---

## Summary
Ad-hoc afternoon creative review between Michael Michelis and Kostas Koukoravas evaluating the latest batch of DTC Meta ad video iterations submitted by the editing agency. The founders identified persistent production defects—specifically illegible subtitle typography, rapid cuts that prevent viewers from reading macros, obscured product framing, and poor visual contrast. They formulated strict visual standards for video captions and established an annotated visual feedback process.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Ad Creative Batch Critique & Defect Analysis
- Reviewed the latest round of video deliverables prepared for Qalzy's paid Meta campaigns.
- **Defects Identified:**
  - **Subtitle Typography:** Captions are rendered in an extremely thin, small default font size with zero background contrast, rendering them illegible on mobile feeds.
  - **Pacing & Readability:** Text cards and subtitles flash across the screen far too quickly. Viewers cannot read food descriptions (e.g., "Homemade mac with cheese") or absorb the caloric readout before the cut.
  - **Framing & UI Occlusion:** Subtitles and chat bubbles frequently overlap and obscure the physical scale screen and mobile app interface.
  - **Asset Recycling:** The editor reused the identical b-roll scene across multiple ad variations without proper contextual framing.
  - **Target Audience Considerations:** The core buyer demographic skews 45+; tiny captions alienate older users and cause immediate scroll-past.

### 2. Video Captioning & Creative Design Standards
- **Caption Formatting Requirements:**
  - Enforce larger font sizes with heavier font-weight.
  - Implement high-contrast background banners or word-by-word karaoke-style highlight animations to ensure effortless readability on sound-off mobile scrolling.
  - Avoid clutter: Strip unnecessary text descriptors when the voiceover already delivers the information.
- **Product & Macro Display Rules:**
  - Ensure the scale display showing calibrated nutrition numbers (calories, protein, carbs, fat) is held steady and centered for at least 2 full seconds.
  - Incorporate clean macro icons or distinct accent colors (e.g., high-contrast badges) to emphasize nutritional data rather than relying on low-contrast green/white text.

### 3. Production Feedback & Agency Accountability
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] noted this represents the 7th iteration where basic typography and pacing feedback was ignored, suggesting work was handed off to an inexperienced junior editor.
- **Feedback Protocol:** Rather than providing written notes that get misinterpreted, Kostas will produce annotated side-by-side screenshots with clear, direct markup ("Do it like this", displaying proper contrast and scale visibility) to eliminate ambiguity.

### 4. Technical Environment & Platform Quirks
- Discussed workstation operating system bugs observed following recent software updates (including local application crashes).
- Noted functional limitations in Meta's web interfaces, specifically the inability to search messages within Instagram desktop's Creator Partnership inbox.
