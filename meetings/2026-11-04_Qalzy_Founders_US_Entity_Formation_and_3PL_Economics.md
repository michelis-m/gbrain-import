## Summary
Brief strategic alignment between Michael Michelis and Kostas Koukoravas evaluating US entity state selection (Delaware vs Wyoming), state sales tax nexus obligations driven by physical inventory placement, economic modeling comparing Portless cross-border shipping against domestic US 3PL fulfillment, and selecting a corporate formation provider (Doola vs Commenda).

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. State Selection & Sales Tax Nexus
- **Delaware vs Wyoming:** Delaware remains preferred (0% state sales tax and established corporate legal precedent).
- **Physical Nexus Realities:** Clarified that sales tax registration obligations in the US depend on inventory warehousing location (physical presence/nexus) rather than where the corporate entity is incorporated.
- **Conversion Impact:** US consumers universally expect sales tax calculations at checkout. Emphasized that showing a dynamic, guaranteed 2-day delivery window at checkout significantly lifts conversion rates compared to overseas shipping estimates.

### 2. Portless vs Domestic US 3PL Fulfillment Economics
- **Cost Comparison:**
  - Portless cross-border shipping currently costs ~**$79,000 annually** at baseline volume.
  - Domestic US 3PL fulfillment modeled at ~**$40,000–$46,000 annually** based on ~250 orders/month.
  - Transitioning to a US 3PL generates estimated operational savings of **$33,000–$39,000 per year** while improving shipping reliability.
- **Conversion & CX Advantages:** Domestic delivery eliminates customs clearance snags, cuts transit times from 10–14 days to 2–3 days, and supports native return logistics.

### 3. Corporate Formation Provider Selection
- Evaluated proposals from [[companies/doola]] and [[companies/commenda]]:
  - [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] noted that Doola's team was switched-on, experienced with cross-border e-commerce startups, and offered transparent pricing.
  - Commenda was perceived as operating like a traditional corporate consultancy with high hourly rates and unnecessary administrative overhead.
