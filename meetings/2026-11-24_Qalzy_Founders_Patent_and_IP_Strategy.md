# Qalzy Founders Sync: Competitor IP Order Investigation, Patent Analysis, Prior Art Defense & Legal Game Theory

**Date:** 2026-11-24  
**Attendees:** [[people/kostas-koukoravas]], [[people/michael-michelis]]  

---

## Summary
Urgent founders meeting between Kostas Koukoravas and Michael Michelis following the discovery of a suspicious order for a Qalzy scale placed through an Italian university requesting an invoice. Investigation revealed that the purchasers are patent attorney [[people/alessandro-romeo]] and technology transfer research fellow [[people/gian-maria]], who filed an international patent application for an AI food placemat. The founders conducted a line-by-line comparison of the patent claims against Qalzy's hardware architecture, mapped out prior art timelines dating back to July 2023, evaluated litigation exposure, and defined a game-theoretic response strategy.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Discovery of University Order & Background Investigation
- An order for a Qalzy AI kitchen scale was placed through an Italian university entity with an explicit request for an official commercial invoice.
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] vetted the individuals behind the order:
  - **[[people/alessandro-romeo]]:** Italian industrial property attorney and mediator specializing in trademarks, design rights, copyright, and IP litigation/settlements.
  - **[[people/gian-maria]]:** Research fellow in IT management and knowledge transfer supporting academic spin-offs and patent commercialization.
- Both individuals are named inventors on a pending European / International PCT patent application covering automated calorie estimation technology.

### 2. Patent Claim Analysis: Placemat vs. Kitchen Scale
- The founders reviewed the claims, abstract, and drawings of the Romeo/Gian Maria patent application:
  - **Claim Scope:** Claims *"equipment comprising an integrated camera, a platform, and a weighing platform designed to estimate meal composition in terms of calories, macros, and micronutrients using AI vision algorithms"* where *"the system detects weight reduction as food is consumed and captures images in real-time."*
  - **Structural Distinctions:**
    - The patent application describes a round, flexible **"food placemat / weighing mat"** (table mat) with a camera oriented parallel to the eating surface and without an integrated digital display (data sent to external software or a small side indicator).
    - It is conceived for passive consumption monitoring during eating rather than active culinary prep/weighing.
    - In contrast, Qalzy is a rigid standalone electronic kitchen scale built with dual high-precision load cells, an angled elevated camera gantry, an integrated color OLED display, physical navigation buttons, and an active portioning workflow.
  - [[people/michael-michelis]] noted that a commercial flexible weighing placemat faces near-insurmountable manufacturing and calibration hurdles compared to a rigid kitchen scale.

### 3. Priority Timeline & Prior Art Robustness
- **Filing Dates:**
  - The Italian patent's International filing date is **September 6, 2024** (with an earliest priority claim in early 2024).
- **Qalzy Development Timeline:**
  - Nutrioscale Ltd. was incorporated prior to their priority date.
  - Qalzy's core physical and functional designs were finalized on **July 10, 2023** (over 8 months before their priority date).
  - Qalzy has its own US utility patent application (`19/297,002`) actively handled by USPTO patent agent [[people/rory-flanagan]], with an international PCT filing pending publication.
- **Industry Prior Art:**
  - Computer-vision food weighing and automated portion estimation systems have extensive documented prior art in academic papers and commercial patent databases from 2016, 2019, 2021, 2022, and 2023 (including Red Dot design award winners and early portable scale prototypes).
  - Any attempt to aggressively assert broad claims on "camera + scale + AI" would face immediate invalidation based on pre-existing prior art.

### 4. Legal Exposure & Strategic Game Theory
- Kostas expressed severe concern regarding potential preliminary injunctions or patent assertion shakedowns that could freeze Qalzy's commercial sales.
- Michael outlined the rational game theory for managing IP threats:
  - **Do NOT reach out or ask questions:** Never send emails asking why they are buying the product; unrepresented communications can be misconstrued, documented as willful notice, or used against the company.
  - **Do NOT cancel the order:** Canceling a valid commercial transaction would signal fear, consciousness of infringement, or bad faith.
  - **Provide the Standard Invoice:** Issue the normal corporate sales receipt requested by the university without additional commentary.
  - **The "Do Nothing Preemptively" Stance:** Patent litigation requires significant capital ($2M–$5M+). Competitors and patent holders frequently purchase market devices solely for competitive intelligence, benchmarking, or academic study with no follow-on action.
  - If any formal cease-and-desist or legal letter is ever served, engage specialized patent defense counsel immediately.
