## Summary
Video negotiation call between Qalzy co-founders ([[people/kostas-koukoravas]], [[people/michael-michelis]]) and [[people/mahdi-aldashti]] to negotiate commercial terms, pricing strategy, and initial order logistics for the retail distribution of Qalzy in Kuwait. Mahdi proposed an aggressive low-price penetration model (retail at $120–$150 USD, max $169) through physical retail stores (Trolley convenience store chain with 80+ locations, gyms like Oxygen Gym, and sports supplement stores), requesting wholesale pricing of $85–$90 per unit for an initial 250-unit batch, accompanied by a 6-month performance-based exclusivity agreement. Kostas and Michael strongly pushed back against sub-$169 pricing, emphasizing that global DTC pricing is $199–$219 (with an RRP of $299), price erosion harms global brand perception, and empirical price testing proved $199 delivers higher total revenue and profit than $169. Next steps focus on evaluating wholesale margin viability closer to $100 and testing sample shipping routes into Kuwait.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Retail Footprint & Channels in Kuwait
- **Physical Retail vs. Online:** Mahdi will distribute strictly via physical retail stores in Kuwait rather than e-commerce, noting Kuwaiti consumers want to see and touch high-tech physical hardware prior to purchase.
- **Target Outlets:**
  - **Trolley:** Leading convenience/retail chain with over 80 stores across Kuwait.
  - **Gyms & Fitness Centers:** Major interest driven by fitness clubs lowering minimum age requirements from 18 to 16 (e.g. Oxygen Gym).
  - **Health & Nutrition Supplement Stores:** Leveraged via family and business relationships.
  - **Consumer Electronics Stores:** High-traffic electronics retailers selling smart lifestyle gadgets.
- **In-Store Education:** Packaging will feature QR codes leading to video demos, complemented by in-store staff demonstrations in select shops.

### 2. Pricing Strategy & Global Brand Integrity
- **Mahdi's Proposal:**
  - Target retail price: $120–$150 USD (maximum ceiling of $169 USD).
  - Retailer margin: 10%–20% cut for stores (~$30 on a $150 unit).
  - Proposed wholesale unit price: $85–$90 USD for an initial order of 250 units.
  - Exclusivity: 6-month exclusive Kuwait distribution rights tied to achieving agreed sales targets.
- **Founders' Pushback & Counter-Model:**
  - **Price Integrity:** Selling Qalzy at $120–$150 when the global online store charges $199–$219 (RRP $299) severely damages brand perception and creates arbitrage pressure.
  - **Empirical Price Testing:** Kostas and Michael explained that Qalzy's live price testing between $169 and $199 demonstrated that $199 generates significantly higher overall revenue; lower pricing did not generate sufficient volume lift to compensate for lost margin.
  - **Compromise Framework:** Kostas proposed a retail price floor of **$169 USD** (accounting for Kuwait's lack of VAT/sales tax) and a wholesale price closer to **$100 USD**. Under this model, at $169 retail minus 20% store margin ($34), Mahdi retains $35 margin per unit ($8,750 on 250 units), preserving his net earnings while protecting Qalzy's brand positioning.

### 3. Sample Logistics & Freight Routing
- **Sample Requirement:** Mahdi requested 1 sample unit immediately to evaluate hardware quality and finalize distribution pitch decks.
- **Direct Shipping Feasibility:** Kostas is checking Qalzy's logistics system for battery-line shipping eligibility directly to Kuwait (past test orders exist in system).
- **Alternative Freight-Forwarding:** If direct courier to Kuwait is unavailable or costly, Mahdi maintains commercial mailboxes in Canada, the US, and Australia; Hong Kong or Australia were identified as the fastest, most cost-effective transit hubs.
