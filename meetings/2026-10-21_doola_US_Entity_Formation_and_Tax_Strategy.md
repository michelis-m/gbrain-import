## Executive Summary
Kostas Koukoravas and Michael Michelis consulted with Parshwa Mehta from doola to evaluate US corporate structure options (Delaware LLC vs. C-Corporation) for establishing a US subsidiary. Setting up a US entity is now urgently required because Trans-Am logistics warned that upcoming November US customs regulations will block customs bonds for foreign entity EINs, preventing Qalzy from importing sea freight into ShipBots. doola recommended a Delaware LLC under their Business-in-a-Box plan, providing pass-through taxation to the UK parent company, a US bank account via Mercury, and full bookkeeping and tax compliance.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Driver for US Entity Formation & Customs Regulatory Urgency
- **Customs Bond Barrier:** Trans-Am freight forwarder Singer Nip informed Qalzy that US Customs is introducing new rules in late November 2026. Surety bond underwriters are refusing to issue customs bonds to foreign entities using foreign-registered EINs.
- **Ocean Freight Dependency:** Qalzy cannot act as importer of record for ocean freight to ShipBots in Gardena, CA without an active US legal entity and domestic EIN.
- **Existing EIN Issues:** Qalzy previously obtained an IRS EIN for its UK entity, but the IRS mistakenly classified it for Form 1065 (partnership return) with an impossible retroactive filing date. A fresh US subsidiary eliminates this administrative confusion.

### 2. Corporate Structure: Delaware LLC vs. C-Corporation
- **LLC Pass-Through Simplicity:**
  - LLC profits pass through directly to the parent UK company, avoiding double corporate taxation without triggering complex US dividend withholding taxes or double taxation treaty filings.
  - Significantly lower administrative burden (no mandatory board meetings, minutes, or annual shareholder resolutions).
- **C-Corporation Considerations:**
  - Subject to a 21% US corporate income tax on profits kept in the US, plus additional dividend withholding tax upon repatriating profits to the UK.
  - While US venture capital investors typically demand a Delaware C-Corp, doola clarified that Qalzy can easily convert an LLC to a C-Corp later in 1–1.5 months via legal partners without dissolving the company.
  - Alternatively, the LLC can file a tax election to be taxed as a C-Corp while retaining LLC operational flexibility.

### 3. Setup Timeline & Banking Infrastructure
- **Formation Timeline:** Company registered in Delaware within 1 week.
- **Banking:** Mercury business bank account opened within 2 weeks, enabling immediate commercial contracts, wires, and ACH transactions.
- **EIN Issuance:** IRS EIN issuance takes 3–4 weeks on the Business-in-a-Box plan.
- **Payroll & Cross-Border Hiring:** Mercury supports US contractor and employee payroll. doola recommended keeping UK team members on UK payroll to avoid UK cross-border employer compliance liabilities.

### 4. doola Service Scope & Sales Tax Boundary
- **Business-in-a-Box Plan:** Includes Delaware state registration, registered agent service, US business address, dedicated bookkeeping (reconciling Stripe/Shopify revenue, returns, disputes, and credit cards via doola Books), and annual federal and state corporate compliance filings.
- **Sales Tax Exclusion:** doola handles corporate income tax in-house but refers state sales tax to an external partner. Kostas confirmed Qalzy has not yet crossed state economic nexus thresholds, so sales tax registration is not immediately required.
