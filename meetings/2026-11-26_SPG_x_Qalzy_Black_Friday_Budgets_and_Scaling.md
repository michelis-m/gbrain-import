# SPG x Qalzy: Black Friday Ad Budget Strategy & Aggressive $8.5K Daily Scaling

---

## Summary
High-level strategic advertising alignment call between [[people/syed-hussain]] (Social Paradigm Group) and Qalzy co-founders [[people/michael-michelis]] and [[people/kostas-koukoravas]]. Syed presented month-to-date scaling metrics (~$25k contribution margin, ~$97k gross sales) and established an aggressive Black Friday ad scaling roadmap exceeding $20,000 in total ad spend across the weekend. The team agreed on specific intraday budget pacing, campaign allocations, and payment threshold safeguards.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Month-to-Date Performance & Scaling Viability
- [[people/syed-hussain]] reported that Qalzy's Meta ad account has maintained remarkable CPA stability and strong contribution margin (~$25,000 MTD) despite progressive budget increases over recent weeks.
- Month-to-date gross sales are hovering near $97,000.
- SPG recommended leveraging this strong baseline to scale aggressively during Black Friday and Cyber Week to maximize revenue and capture valuable benchmark data for future years.

### 2. Black Friday Weekend Budget Roadmap
Syed outlined the specific scaling schedule across the primary Meta campaigns:
- **Thursday, Nov 26 (Today):** Baseline increased from $2.8k -> $3.8k earlier, with an additional +$700 scheduled on call to finish at **$4,500 daily budget** on the main campaign (~$4.2k–$4.5k expected actual spend).
- **Friday, Nov 27 (Black Friday):** Aggressive scale to **$8,500 daily budget** (+4,000 increase) on the main campaign, front-loaded to match consumer buying rhythms across US time zones:
  - *Midnight to 1:00 PM PST (2:00 PM Mountain):* +$2,500 budget injection.
  - *1:00 PM PST to 2:00 AM PST Saturday:* +$1,500 budget injection (covering West Coast evening shopping through midnight).
- **Saturday & Sunday, Nov 28–29:** Scaled down to **$6,300 daily budget** (+1,800 over baseline).
- **Weekend Aggregate Spend:** Total ad spend across the 4-day peak will exceed **$20,000 USD**.
- Performance will be reviewed on Saturday morning (EET/GMT) to determine whether further scaling is justified ahead of Cyber Monday.

### 3. Supporting Campaigns & Google Ads
- **Black Friday CBO Campaign:** Kept steady at ~$300/day; functions primarily as bottom-of-funnel retargeting/support and does not require incremental budget.
- **Cost Caps Campaign:** Maintained without budget increase because current spend has not exhausted existing caps.
- **Google Ads:** Minor budget adjustment planned, but Syed advised against aggressive Google Ads scaling; Black Friday consumer behavior on social media is driven by immediate impulse purchases rather than extensive search evaluation.

### 4. Cash Flow & Ad Account Funding Protection
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] raised a critical operational concern: during a previous spend spike, the ad account card balance depleted after unexpected early merchant debits, triggering automated Meta payment failure notices and threatening campaign delivery pauses.
- Kostas and Michael confirmed Qalzy has ample cash reserves, but agreed to verify that the designated primary ad payment card is heavily funded and that valid secondary/backup payment cards are linked in Meta Ads Manager to prevent ad account shutdowns during peak Black Friday traffic.
