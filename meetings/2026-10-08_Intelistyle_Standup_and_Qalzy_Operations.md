### 6. US Warehouse Transition & Portless Inventory Modeling
- **Strategic Motivation:**
  - Order volume in continental Europe remains very low (<5%), making EU warehouse retention inefficient.
  - Transitioning primary inventory to a US 3PL would enable 3-day domestic delivery, significantly lifting conversion rates and eliminating cross-border transit delays.
- **Transition Economics & Risks:**
  - Estimated one-off transition expenses at ~$10,000 (freight, US company/accounting requirements, palletization fees).
  - Risk of overlapping storage fees between Portless and the new 3PL during the 30–45 day freight transit window.
  - Buffer Strategy: Maintain ~400 scale units at Portless to fulfill baseline demand while palletizing and freight-shipping ~3,500 units and accessories to the US.
  - Accessory inventory ratios (chargers, portion plates, travel cases) must be tightly calibrated to avoid stranded components.
