## Summary
Monthly investor and board update call between Qalzy co-founders and lead investor [[people/jeffrey-faustin]] (Partner & CIO, [[companies/jenson-ventures]]). Covered recent scaling wins on Meta, agency transition, unit economic expansion, US 3PL logistics transition, EU regulatory market exit, inventory runway, Batch 2 production financing, patent prosecution, and CES 2027 Eureka Park exhibition plans.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Marketing Performance & Agency Transition
- **Meta Ad Velocity:** Over 500 active ad creatives running on Meta (pruned from ~900 to eliminate redundant duplication).
- **Agency Transition:** Transitioned performance marketing to a Manchester agency ([[companies/spg]]), with very positive results:
  - October ROAS reached **1.8x** (up from 1.5–1.6x in prior months).
  - November month-to-date ROAS expanded to **2.0x**.
- **Contribution Margin:** Expanded from **15% in October** to **24% in November**.

### 2. Supply Chain, US 3PL Transition & US Entity Strategy
- **Margin Drag from China Direct Shipping:** Shipping DTC orders directly out of Shenzhen via Portless is currently the main factor suppressing contribution margin. Transitioning fulfillment to a US-based 3PL is modeled to add an immediate **~7% to contribution margin**.
- **US Entity & HSA/FSA Access:**
  - Explored establishing a US subsidiary/entity to unlock employer HSA/FSA health funds (Truemed/Shopify) and import stock directly under a domestic entity.
  - Advised that mid-market dual UK/US tax structuring and transfer pricing compliance are excessively complex and cost-prohibitive for the current stage.
  - **Decision:** Pausing US entity formation for now; will continue operating directly through the UK parent entity.
- **Customs & Freight Forwarding Obstacle:** US freight forwarder declined an ocean freight import under a foreign EIN citing late-November changes to customs bond regulations. Team is actively resolving logistics paths to position stock domestically.

### 3. EU Regulatory Phase-Out & US Market Concentration
- All marketing budget and operational focus are now concentrated 100% on the US market.
- **EU Regulatory Sunset:** Effective February 2027, new EU environmental regulations mandate user-replaceable batteries in consumer electronics. Redesigning the current Qalzy scale hardware is not a priority; consequently, European direct sales will cease in February 2027 to focus entirely on the US.

### 4. Inventory Runway & Batch 2 Financing Requirements
- **Stock Runway:** Approximately **3,500 units** remaining in available inventory.
- **Stockout Projection:** Based on accelerating Q4/Q1 sales velocity, stockout is projected around **late June or July 2027**.
- **Batch 2 Timeline:**
  - Reorder must be placed in **January 2027** for delivery by end of March 2027.
  - 1 month ocean transit positions stock in warehouse by end of April 2027, preserving a 2-month buffer before stockout.
- **Capital Requirement:** Qalzy requires **$150,000 to $250,000** to fund the new production batch. Exploring non-dilutive debt financing vs factory terms (Chinese manufacturing representatives are visiting London this week). Jeffrey confirmed current cash flow and runway sound encouraging.

### 5. Intellectual Property & AI Operations Automation
- **USPTO Patent Status:** USPTO confirmed the core utility patent is approved and on track for grant issuance within ~2 months. A US-registered patent attorney ([[people/rory-flanagan]]) has been engaged to file Form `PTO/AIA/82` to take over representation under new foreign applicant rules.
- **AI Automation:** Kostas highlighted Michael's extensive automation work across customer support, influencer marketing, and operations, noting that operations are largely run autonomously via AI agents.

### 6. Hardware QA & Product Line Simplification
- **Camera Sensor QA:** Kostas received and is testing a new camera sensor hardware prototype. Benchmarks show minimal latency difference and 90%–95% image fidelity; conducting real-world environmental testing over coming days.
- **Color Variant Demand:** White scales outsell Black by 3:1 to 4:1. Founders are considering phasing out Black entirely in Batch 2 to streamline production and supply chain SKU complexity.

### 7. CES 2027 (Las Vegas) Strategy
- **Eureka Park Exhibition:** Secured a booth in the Innovation Eureka Park section for **~$6k total cost** ($3k booth registration + $2k graphics/setup) compared to standard commercial rates of $30k+.
- **Pitching Competition:** Applied for the dedicated CES pitch competition focused on elderly care, carer independence, and chronic disease management.
- **Core CES Objective:** Focus is exclusively on **US press traction and US venture investors**, intentionally deferring retail/wholesale distribution partnerships until the direct-to-consumer brand foundation and education mature.
