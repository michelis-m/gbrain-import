# Qalzy Founders Sync: Ad Creatives Legibility, Messaging Copy, Conversational Formats & Black Friday A/B Testing Dynamics

**Date:** 2026-11-23  
**Attendees:** [[people/kostas-koukoravas]], [[people/michael-michelis]]  

---

## Summary
Founders sync between Michael Michelis and Kostas Koukoravas reviewing Meta ad creatives, visual copy legibility, messaging copy ("No subscription forever" vs "Lifetime Premium"), conversational text-thread ad concepts, 5-star customer review insights regarding free vs paid tier perception, video creative performance (Fathom / AI-generated vs real food demo cuts), and Black Friday promotional behavior impact on landing page A/B tests.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Ad Creative Copy & Mobile Legibility
- **Value Proposition Messaging:**
  - [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] noted that previous ads emphasizing "no subscription forever" captured good ad spend and delivered positive ROAS.
  - Discussed proposed copy variations: "Lifetime Premium App Membership Included for Free" vs "Quick scan recognition / AI identifies ingredients".
  - [[people/michael-michelis]] advocated for concise, clear terminology: use direct terms like "AI camera" rather than wordy alternatives like "AI-powered food detection".
- **Visual Sizing & Demographics:**
  - Kostas highlighted that text overlays and card callouts in the latest 9:16 vertical and 4:3 formats are far too small to be legible on mobile feeds.
  - Michael emphasized that Qalzy's core target audience includes an older demographic (50+), making readable text and high-contrast sizing mandatory.
  - Both agreed that the physical scale must remain clearly visible in the product composition rather than obscured by graphics.

### 2. Conversational & Text-Thread Ad Concept
- Discussed testing conversational / WhatsApp-style ad creatives featuring authentic dialogue:
  - Example hooks: *"Hit my goal for this year, lost 20 kg. How? Qalzy scale, man."* or holiday gifting angles (*"What should I get my mom this Christmas? Check the scale that I got for my girlfriend."*).
  - Authentic, conversational recommendations bypass ad blindness compared to stiff marketing claims.

### 3. Customer Perceptions & Freemium Engagement
- Qalzy received an unsolicited 5-star customer review celebrating the app as the "best free app" due to the generous free tier (free AI scan quota, free barcode scanning, clean UX).
- Michael noted user consumption patterns: users typically rely on barcode scanning for everyday pantry foods, reserving AI photo scanning for complex meals or weekend dining out (1–2 times weekly), creating natural upgrade pathways once usage scales.

### 4. Video Performance & Creative Authenticity
- Reviewed creative video iterations:
  - Discussed agency/AI video cuts (Fathom): while AI-assisted visual production looks polished, authentic hands-on product demos (e.g., preparing real foods like moussaka or spanakopita, taring the scale, live logging) feel significantly more credible.
  - An earlier video test stalled after ~$20 in spend; Michael noted weak initial 2–3 second hook retention (starting with talking heads rather than a show-stopping visual hook).

### 5. Black Friday A/B Testing Considerations
- Michael shared feedback from Hussain:
  - Conversion behavior during Black Friday is heavily promotional and transactional. Shoppers are primed to purchase based on offer and urgency rather than extended education.
  - Educational or extra descriptive sections tested during peak BFCM may not reflect typical baseline consumer behavior.
