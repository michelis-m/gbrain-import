## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Operational Rationale for US Expansion
- **Logistics & Customs Hurdles:** Operating as a UK company creates severe friction with US freight imports, customs surety bond underwriting, and commercial insurance coverage.
- **Physical Fulfillment Setup:** Qalzy manufactures scales in Shenzhen, China and will ship containerized inventory to a US 3PL warehouse in Gardena, California (ShipBots) for domestic DTC fulfillment (~300 transactions/month).
- **Banking & Insurance:** A domestic US presence provides access to US commercial banking (e.g. Mercury/Brex) and enables compliant product liability insurance.

### 2. Entity Selection & Tax Classification
- **Delaware LLC (Disregarded Entity):** If wholly owned by Qalzy Ltd (UK), the LLC is treated as a disregarded entity for US federal tax purposes. Income is reported via a Form 1120-F protective return, with intercompany transactions declared on Form 1120 pro forma and Form 5472.
- **Delaware C-Corporation:** Invoices US customers directly and files standard federal and state corporate income tax returns. Required if raising venture capital from US institutional investors.
- **Transfer Pricing:** Manya emphasized that cross-border inventory and service billing between the UK parent and US subsidiary requires formal transfer pricing benchmarking and documentation to withstand IRS and HMRC audits.

### 3. California State Nexus & Indirect Taxes
- **Physical Nexus:** Storing physical inventory at ShipBots in California creates immediate physical nexus, triggering mandatory California franchise tax board registration and state sales tax reporting, superseding the $100k/$500k economic nexus thresholds.
- **Commenda Compliance Engine:** Commenda automates state-by-state exposure monitoring, local registrations, and indirect tax filings.

### 4. Commenda Cost Structure
- **Entity Incorporation:** ~$1,500 one-time fee for Delaware formation.
- **Registered Agent & Address:** ~$150 annually.
- **Accounting & Tax Filing:** ~$6,000 annually (covering dedicated bookkeeping for ~300 monthly transactions, corporate federal/state filings, and Form 1120-F / 5472 pro forma preparation). Total estimated year 1 commitment: ~$7,650.
