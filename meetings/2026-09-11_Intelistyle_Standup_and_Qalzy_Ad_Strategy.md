### 3. Blazer / Ronaldas Media Buying Deficiencies
- **ABO vs CBO:** The account is currently structured under Ad Set Budget Optimization (ABO) with multiple fragmented ad sets competing against each other.
- **Premature Ad Pausing:** Ronaldas is disabling ads within 24–48 hours at the first sign of a CPA spike. This practice chokes the top of the funnel and completely ignores Meta's 7-day click / 1-day view attribution lag (where conversions register days after ad exposure).
- **Audience Cannibalization:** The live ad sets target identical or overlapping broad audiences, driving up auction costs and causing self-cannibalization.

### 4. Preparation for SPG Team Call
- Formulate clear questions for Charlie Williams and Syed Hussain at SPG:
  - How to transition from the existing ABO setup to a consolidated CBO campaign without resetting learning phases destructively.
  - Audience consolidation strategy and exclusion setup.
  - Video creative optimization: ensuring hooks land in the first 3–6 seconds of mobile feed video and captions are positioned properly without being cropped by Meta's UI.
