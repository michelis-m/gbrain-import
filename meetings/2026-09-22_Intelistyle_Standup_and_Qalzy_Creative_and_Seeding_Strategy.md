- **Social Media Comment Moderation:**

### 4. International Gifting & Seeding Generated Content (SGC) Pipeline
- **Sample Scale Shipments:**
  - Arranged unit shipment for podcast host [[people/jared-johnson]] ahead of upcoming recording.
  - Discussed shipping mechanics for international contacts (including Hong Kong / Kuwait via Portless).
- **SPG (Social Paradigm Group) Content Methodology:**
  - **Influencer + UGC Hybrid Model:** Gifting scales to influencers, then hiring UGC creators to film complementary scenes and B-roll to splice with the influencer footage, generating up to 36 distinct ad iterations per creator.
  - **LTV Incentive Program:** Recommending offering $100 cash incentives to top LTV customers for authentic video reviews.
  - **Landing Page Alignment (iM8 Benchmark):** Noted iM8 runs 2,500 ads and 120 dedicated landing pages, ensuring 1:1 message match where the ad offer, angle, and featured creator are immediately mirrored above the fold on the destination page.
- **Creator Discovery via Modash:**
  - Michael evaluated influencer discovery tools; while enterprise suites cost $3,500/quarter, Modash provides an API and creator database for ~$300/month.
  - Currently on a 14-day Modash trial, testing automated queries for micro-influencers (700 to 1,000+ followers) posting about calorie counting and calorie deficit.
  - **Usage Rights Strategy:** Plan to gift scales to ~10 micro-creators; once organic posts go live, request ad usage rights (industry data shows ~80% grant rights without additional spend).
  - Plan to consult SPG on the balance between seeding, paid influencers, and direct UGC creators.

### 5. Creative Iteration, B-Roll & Ad Formats
- **Editing Bottlenecks:** Kostas noted that manually finding and synchronizing matching B-roll footage with on-screen comments and headlines is very labor-intensive in CapCut.
- **MCP & Tooling Exploration:** Michael suggested evaluating Premiere Pro workflows or open-source MCP integrations for automated B-roll assembly.
- **Nutritionist Ad Hooks:**
  - Michael proposed launching new hook variations on winning video concepts to unlock new audience segments:
    - *"Your Dietitian"*
    - *"Most nutritionists won't tell you this, but you can literally eat everything and still..."*
    - *"Weight Loss"*
- **Format Delivery:** Rather than displaying large, static blocks of text on screen, agreed to test AI-generated voiceovers paired with animated, dynamic subtitles to improve hook retention and readability.
