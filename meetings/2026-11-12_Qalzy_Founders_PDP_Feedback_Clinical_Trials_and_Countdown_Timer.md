# Qalzy Founders Sync: PDP CRO Feedback, Clinical Research Feasibility & BF Countdown Timer

**Date:** 2026-11-12  
**Attendees:** [[people/kostas-koukoravas]], [[people/michael-michelis]]  

---

## Summary
Founders strategy and CRO sync reviewing qualitative user feedback on the product page, establishing UX fixes for unstated core features (folding camera, plate sizing, dimensions, vitamin tracking), analyzing the conversion impact of long countdown timers, and evaluating/deprioritizing the B2B clinical trial research market.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. PDP Friction & Qualitative User Feedback
[[people/kostas-koukoravas]] walked through actionable user feedback gathered from external testing:
- **Plate Sizing Hesitation ("Will it fit my plates?"):** Prospective buyers questioned whether standard dinner plates fit on the scale because plates in promotional images appear compact. Agreed to add a dedicated FAQ and visual comparison demonstrating compatibility with standard/large dinner plates.
- **Missing Physical Dimensions:** Discovered that counter dimensions were removed during recent PDP cleanup. Agreed that listing dimensions at the bottom of the page causes no friction and answers critical buyer questions ("Will it fit on my counter?").
- **Unstated Core Feature — Folding Camera:** Testers assumed the camera collapsed only because Qalzy offers a travel case. The folding camera arm is a core product differentiator but is never explicitly highlighted in website copy. It must be prominently presented as a key selling point.
- **Vitamin & Micronutrient Tracking:** The site touts vitamin and micronutrient tracking, but details (B12, Vitamin C, saturated fat) are buried in an obscure homepage hover with an uninformative image. Decided to showcase real in-app screenshots of comprehensive micronutrient breakdowns on the PDP and landing pages.
- **Dim Light Messaging:** Reviewed customer reaction to "works in dim light" messaging, noting that while technically useful (built-in sensor LED), it can read oddly to users who assume kitchens are well-lit.

### 2. A/B Testing Discipline
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] and [[people/michael-michelis]] reaffirmed the foundational rule: execute all landing page and offer modifications through structured A/B tests rather than making unverified subjective assumptions.

### 3. Black Friday Countdown Timer Urgency
- Evaluated buyer psychology regarding the Black Friday countdown timer displaying "15 days away":
  - Seeing a 15-day runway diminishes purchase urgency by signaling that shoppers have plenty of time.
  - Considered removing the countdown timer or activating it only during the final closing window of the sale.

### 4. B2B Clinical Research Market Assessment
- [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] debriefed his discussion with a clinical trials industry professional regarding using Qalzy scales in nutrition studies:
  - Clinical trials typically manage dietary variables by providing pre-packaged meal plans directly (e.g. HelloFresh/hospital rations) or tracking biomarkers via blood draws rather than relying on participant meal logging.
  - Trials require real-time compliance pinging; retrospectively logged data is treated with skepticism in clinical environments.
  - **Conclusion:** Pursuing clinical trial researchers would be an uphill, low-ROI distraction at this stage. Qalzy should remain focused on consumer DTC growth and proven customer segments.

### 5. Market & Industry Observations
- Brief discussion regarding a wearable assistant startup raising $10M pre-market, contrasting hype-driven hardware fundraising with delivering functional shipping hardware.
