## Executive Summary
Michael Michelis and Kostas Koukoravas held their morning standup to align on direct-to-consumer website optimization, creative production accountability, mobile app release status, and upcoming trade show milestones. The founders decided to conclude the active PDP A/B test and launch a new test isolating subscription clarity and messaging to resolve the brand's primary consumer conversion objection. They also evaluated agency creative deliverables—raising concerns regarding Sol Campbell's meeting attendance and overly simplistic educational video assets—reviewed a new iOS app build for Facebook App Review, arranged to replace synthetic AI video B-roll with authentic footage of Michael's father using the scale, and flagged the upcoming CES 2027 pitch deadline on Friday.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Sol Campbell Creative Assets & Meeting Attendance
- **Agency Accountability:** Kostas noted that Sol Campbell (`[[people/sol-campbell]]`) from SPG has repeatedly failed to attend scheduled meetings, leaving the team without clear visibility into upcoming creative deliverables.
- **Inadequate "How It Works" Asset:** Evaluated Sol's latest creative delivery explaining the calorie tracking and food scanning mechanism. Both founders agreed the concept was severely inadequate, oversimplifying the product experience to pressing a button accompanied by three brief lines of text.
- **Addressing Skepticism:** The founders emphasized that skeptical prospects frequently ask *"How is it possible?"* or claim *"This is not accurate."* To convert higher-intent buyers and reduce downstream churn, Qalzy requires substantive educational video assets demonstrating the actual food recognition, portioning, and multi-ingredient tracking technology rather than superficial high-level claims.

### 2. Website CRO & Subscription A/B Test Prioritization
- **Concluding Current Test:** Agreed to halt the current product detail page (PDP) test to reset baseline metrics.
- **Subscription Objection as Priority #1:** Both founders identified consumer confusion surrounding recurring subscriptions as the primary friction point on the website.
- **Test Variable Isolation:** While Sol suggested prioritizing benefit-driven headline tests (projected to yield up to a 40% lift), Kostas advocated isolating subscription messaging first. The proposed variant will clearly state *"No subscription required"* directly beneath the primary Add to Cart button, refine subscription FAQs, and eliminate confusing premium app sections.
- **Agency Coordination:** Michael will send Sol a clean Figma export displaying solely the subscription-related modifications to confirm alignment before implementation.

### 3. Mobile App Release & Facebook App Review
- **Consecutive Bug Fixes:** Michael implemented two back-to-back bug fixes on the mobile application codebase, confirming the fixes are stable and live.
- **Platform Verification:** The new build was deployed specifically to facilitate screen recording and test session generation required for Facebook App Review and partner verification.

### 4. Authentic Creative B-Roll vs. Synthetic AI Renders
- **Authentic Usage Assets:** Michael captured original video footage of his father operating the physical Qalzy scale in a domestic kitchen setting.
- **Replacing AI Visuals:** Agreed that future ad cuts and edits produced by Ronaldas (`[[people/ronaldas-blazer]]`) at Blazer Agency should replace synthetic AI-generated avatars and scale renders with authentic lifestyle B-roll to increase ad credibility and retention.

### 5. CES 2027 Pitch Submission Deadline
- **Upcoming Deadline:** Kostas highlighted that the submission deadline for the CES 2027 startup pitch competition is this Friday, October 30, 2026.
- **Pitch Deck Status:** Kostas noted that work on the pitch deck has not yet started, requiring dedicated focus this week to finalize the narrative and submission deck before the deadline.
