## Key Discussion Points

### 1. CES Networking, Badge Scanners & Business Card Strategy
- **Conference Lead Retrieval vs Traditional Cards:**
  - Discussed CES conference lead scanners vs business cards. Official badge scanners often carry high rental costs and deliver delayed batch CSV exports days later.
  - While digital sharing (NFC/LinkedIn) is useful, physical business cards remain an essential expectation for top-tier investors, corporate partners, and press. Handing over a well-crafted physical card reinforces brand credibility and seriousness.
- **Card Specifications & Quantity:**
  - [[people/michael-michelis]] cautioned against bulk low-quality printing, recalling previous Intelistyle cards that easily scuffed and warped in luggage.
  - **Decision:** Print a smaller, high-end run of **250 cards each** (500 total) on premium cardstock rather than high-volume cheap stock.
  - Incorporate a modern QR code on the card for instant contact saving and website access.
- **Card Design Aesthetics:**
  - Explored custom design concepts, such as a die-cut shape mirroring the 2D silhouette of the Qalzy scale with the front camera notch, backed with contact details.
  - [[people/kostas-koukoravas]] reviewed stock templates on Vistaprint and found them generic. Kostas will use Claude / AI design tools to develop a bespoke, clean, modern layout before submitting for print.
