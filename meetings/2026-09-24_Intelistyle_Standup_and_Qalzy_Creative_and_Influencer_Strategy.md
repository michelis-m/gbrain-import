### 6. Micro-Influencer Outreach & Pricing Framework
- **Strategy Shift:** Moving away from free gifting toward structured paid partnerships with micro-influencers (<10k followers) who demonstrate high engagement rates.
- **Offer Structure:** Proposed offering $150–$250 flat fee + complimentary scale ($199 value) + 60–90 day paid Meta ad usage rights + $20 flat affiliate commission per sale.
- **Negotiation Rule:** Explicitly avoid asking creators for rate cards (which inflate quotes 3x–4x); lead directly with fixed offers based on engagement metrics.
- **Sourcing Tool:** Utilizing the Modash API trial to filter creator profiles by followers, engagement rate, and niche relevance (e.g., T1D, fitness, macro tracking).

- **Pricing Errors:** Pointed out incorrect discount figures in creatives ($80 off vs 20% vs 33% off).
- **Creative Policy:** Mandated that all video scripts must receive explicit co-founder approval before being sent to creators for filming.
