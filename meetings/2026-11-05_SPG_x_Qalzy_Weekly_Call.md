## Summary
Weekly growth sync between Qalzy and Social Paradigm Group (SPG) covering Meta ad performance under aggressive budget scaling, launching an Early Bird Black Friday promotional campaign starting November 15 with a bundled lifetime subscription offer, creative ideation around "scroll-back" hooks and authority creator endorsements, and landing page copywriting workflows.

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Paid Media Performance & Scaling
- [[people/syed-hussain]] reviewed Meta ad spend and efficiency:
  - Increased daily ad spend aggressively across primary campaigns in anticipation of Q4 holiday volume.
  - CPA rose slightly yesterday due to the sudden budget expansion, but the account maintained a positive net contribution margin.
  - Core product demo videos continue to capture the vast majority of delivery and maintain strong conversion efficiency.

### 2. Black Friday / Cyber Monday (BFCM) Strategy: Early Launch (Nov 15)
- **Early Bird Launch Timing:** SPG strongly recommended launching Qalzy's Black Friday Early Bird promotion on **November 15**, ahead of standard Black Friday week.
  - *Rationale:* Consumer discretionary budgets deplete rapidly in late November; capturing demand early locks in high-intent buyers before ad auctions become excessively expensive.
- **The Core Offer ("Pay once, track forever"):**
  - Bundle the Qalzy smart scale with a free Lifetime Subscription (or 1-year premium coaching tier) during the promotional period.
  - Emphasizes value and eliminates customer hesitation regarding hidden recurring subscription fees.
- **PDP Site Adjustments:**
  - Add an Early Bird announcement banner across the top navigation.
  - Implement a countdown timer creating urgency for the early promotional window.
  - Highlight the bundled gift in the Buy Box next to the media gallery (e.g., gift icon callout: "Early Bird Black Friday Special: Free Lifetime Subscription Included").

### 3. Creative Ideation & "Scroll-Back" Hooks
- [[people/sol-campbell]] shared creative concepts for new video briefs:
  - **"Scroll-Back" Hooks:** Moving beyond initial 3-second thumb-stops to create visual curiosity triggers that cause users scrolling past to reverse course (e.g., rapidly bringing contrasting food items directly into the camera lens; extreme or unconventional demonstrations such as spraying mustard/ketchup directly onto the scale).
  - **Authority Creator Formats:** High priority on partnering with certified nutritionists, dietitians, and medical doctors (MDs). Having authoritative practitioners test food recognition and validate accuracy directly counters consumer skepticism.
  - Sol and Kostas agreed that UGC creator briefs must combine precise visual requirements (exact B-roll shots and camera angles) with authentic creator tone.

### 4. Landing Page Copywriting & AI Training
- Syed is building an AI copywriting workflow to generate high-converting DTC sales copy for Qalzy landing pages, evaluating models and feeding comparative datasets of top-performing vs underperforming DTC pages.
- Committed to delivering curated reference landing pages and copy templates to Kostas.
