# Intelistyle x South Moon Under: AI Recommendations, Complete the Look & Automated Outfit Styling

**Date:** 2026-11-02  
**Attendees:** [[people/michael-michelis]], [[people/keri-nwosu]], Jana  
**Companies:** [[companies/intelistyle]], [[companies/south-moon-under]]  

---

## Summary
Commercial discovery and capabilities demonstration between Michael Michelis (Founder & CEO of Intelistyle) and the digital merchandising leadership for retail apparel brand South Moon Under (Keri Nwosu, Director of Ecommerce, and Jana, executive at South Moon Under's parent ownership group). South Moon Under is seeking to transition away from labor-intensive rule-based recommendation software (Nosto) toward visual AI styling, automated "Complete the Look" / "Shop the Look" generation from on-figure model photography, and automated matching set detection.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. South Moon Under Business Profile & Technical Challenges
- **Scale & Performance:**
  - Active Catalog: **~2,500 active SKUs** online at any given time.
  - Web Traffic: **~100,000 monthly user sessions** (October 2026).
  - Annual Digital Turnover: Approximately **$4.5 million in revenue**.
- **Current Limitations with Nosto:**
  - Currently relies on Nosto for on-site product recommendations.
  - Team is frustrated by extensive manual rule maintenance and lack of visual intelligence.
  - Basic category rules (e.g. "if dress, show handbag") produce generic, disconnected outfits.
  - Matching sets (e.g. separate swim tops and bottoms with different product titles) currently require manual entry of backend Shopify metafields to link items together.

### 2. Intelistyle Core Capabilities Demonstrated
- **Visual AI Outfit Generation ("Complete the Look"):**
  - Uses computer vision and image recognition to identify individual garments and accessories worn by models in on-figure product photos.
  - Automatically matches model-worn items to existing catalog inventory without requiring manual SKU tagging or detailed written descriptions.
- **Automated Matching Set Recognition:**
  - AI visually recognizes matching patterns, prints, fabric textures, and colors across disjoint product titles (e.g., tropical floral print top and bottom), eliminating manual metafield linking.
- **Hybrid AI + Human-in-the-Loop Styling:**
  - While AI models generate baseline outfit recommendations, Intelistyle employs professional fashion stylists who audit and curate edge-case recommendations.
  - Ensures substitutions (e.g., swapping out-of-stock items) preserve aesthetic integrity, brand alignment, and avoid jarring mismatched outfits.
- **Deep Fashion Tagging & Metadata Enrichment:**
  - Analyzes catalog imagery to generate 834+ fashion-specific taxonomy attributes per product (e.g., collar neckline, pencil silhouette, midi length, fabric weight, body-type affinity), overcoming South Moon Under's sparse product copy.
- **Google Analytics Integration & Attribution:**
  - Intelistyle pushes all impression and conversion events directly into the client's Google Analytics, evaluating ROI through Google's data-driven attribution modeling rather than opaque proprietary dashboards. Typically demonstrates 10x–20x ROI across A/B tests.

### 3. Commercial Scope & Budget Fit
- Michael provided an indicative monthly pricing range of **$500 to $900 per month** based on South Moon Under's 2,500 SKU catalog size and 100k session volume.
- Keri noted this pricing appears viable depending on how many Nosto modules Intelistyle replaces and how the software budget is reallocated.
