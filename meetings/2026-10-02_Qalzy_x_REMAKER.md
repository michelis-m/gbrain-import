## Key Discussion Points

### 1. REMAKER Product & Company Overview
- **Product Vision:** Humphrey founded REMAKER (based in Victoria, London) during COVID lockdown to solve strength training tracking. Unlike running with clear GPS/pace data, strength training lacks automated feedback.
- **Hardware Device ("REMAKER Move"):** An attachable tension/force sensor that clips onto workout cables, barbells, or weights. It measures movement quality, prevents injury from excessive loading, and feeds an AI coach that autoregulates training loads.
- **Stage & Traction:** Started in elite pro sport (working with professional teams, including outreach from Turkish national sports) and is expanding into the prosumer fitness market. Raised 3 small angel rounds to date, with capital invested almost entirely into R&D. Currently fundraising to power their consumer marketing push.
- **Manufacturing & Logistics:** Electronics sourced from Taiwan; assembly and enclosures done in the UK. Global shipping managed through a UK-based fulfillment partner, with plans to establish US logistics hubs following the current funding round.

### 2. D2C Scaling & Meta Acquisition Dynamics
- **Qalzy Metrics & Experience:**
  - Michael shared Qalzy's progress: ~$1,000/day Meta spend (~$30k/month) at ~1.5 ROAS, targeting 2.0 ROAS for solid profitability (up from 1.3 ROAS in month one).
  - Focused purely on the US market with direct air freight from China to manage inventory risk without bulk freight commitments.
- **Algorithmic Meta Buying Strategy:**
  - Humphrey shared guidance from a seasoned hardware founder: commit 3–5x daily AOV consistently on Meta for 3 months as a sunk cost without making erratic adjustments based on early 4-week data, allowing Meta's algorithms to fully optimize audience matching.
- **Influencer Marketing & Ambassador Seeding:**
  - Humphrey shared that pure cold product gifting has low conversion. Recommended hosting localized in-person gifting and educational events (e.g., London, New York, Texas) where creators receive hands-on training, build brand rapport, generate organic content, and receive affiliate codes.
  - Humphrey mentioned a high-profile health/fitness influencer contact in London and offered to make an introduction to Michael.

### 3. Investor Intro to Jenson Ventures
- Michael recommended [[people/jeffrey-faustin]] at Jenson Ventures (London-based fund specializing in SEIS and EIS investments with a strong track record backing early-stage hardware founders, such as electric toothbrushes and water filtration systems).
- Michael offered to introduce Humphrey to Jenson Ventures upon receiving a short blurb.
