# Stockyfy / Leverify: Amazon US Distribution, Wholesale Pricing & US 3PL Services Sync

**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Attendees:** [[people/michael-michelis]], [[people/ahmed-hassan]]  

---

## Summary
Introductory commercial partnership discussion between [[people/michael-michelis]] (co-founder, Qalzy) and [[people/ahmed-hassan]] (Brand Manager & Sourcing Manager, [[companies/stockyfy]] / Leverify LLC). Covered Leverify’s Amazon US authorized reseller model, Amazon PPC management, brick-and-mortar showroom operations, US warehouse distribution/3PL services, and alignment on volume wholesale pricing tiers ahead of CES 2027.

---

## Key Discussion Points

### 1. Leverify / Stockyfy Operations & Channel Footprint
- Ahmed outlined Leverify’s background: founded in 2015, originated in consumer electronics, and has diversified into fragrances (e.g., Armaf, Rasasi), beauty (One Beauty), and toys.
- Electronics continues to represent ~70% of total revenue.
- Channel revenue breakdown:
  - 90% online (predominantly Amazon US via FBA and FBM, alongside direct brand web sales).
  - 10% offline via their physical retail showroom (average basket size ~$900 across electronics/beauty, functioning at breakeven as an experiential demo center that channels customers into repeat online orders).
- Corporate leadership: Leverify’s CEO is a retired former Microsoft executive (matching [[people/kostas-koukoravas]]'s background) who will lead Leverify’s delegation at CES 2027 and visit Qalzy’s booth.

### 2. Amazon Reseller Partnership & Open-Book Modeling
- Stockyfy acts as an authorized Amazon reseller managing brand onboarding, listings, fulfillment, and direct Amazon PPC campaigns.
- Open-book approach: Once Qalzy provides tiered wholesale pricing, Stockyfy’s Amazon team will model sales velocity, Amazon fees, and PPC ad costs to determine target margin feasibility before finalizing terms.
- Ahmed offered to connect his Amazon accounts team to provide detailed PPC ad spend and ROAS projections.

### 3. US Master Distribution & 3PL Warehousing Capabilities
- In addition to Amazon reselling, Leverify operates its own warehouse facility in the United States.
- Offers master distribution and 3PL services for international brands expanding into North America:
  - Inventory warehousing and storage.
  - Pick, pack, and direct customer/retailer fulfillment.
  - US domestic returns processing and inspection.
  - Michael noted Qalzy is actively evaluating US warehouse solutions to optimize domestic fulfillment and avoid international shipping delays.

### 4. Next Steps & Commercial Alignment
- Wholesale pricing structure: Michael confirmed Qalzy works on tiered wholesale pricing starting around 500 units (with additional tiers at 1,000 and 2,000+ units), which Ahmed confirmed aligns with their standard onboarding parameters.
- Both parties agreed to continue modeling terms remotely and meet in person at CES 2027.
