---

## Editorial Summary (100–120 Words)
For decades, diet and health apps have forced users to manually search and log every meal into a database—a tedious routine that leads to high burnout and abandonment. Qalzy is solving this friction with the world’s first AI-powered kitchen scale featuring an integrated camera and precision weighing sensors. 

With patent-pending computer vision technology, Qalzy identifies, weighs, and logs food nutritional data (calories, protein, carbs, fats, fiber, and micronutrients) in a single tap. Designed for health-conscious consumers, diabetics, fitness enthusiasts, and weight management, Qalzy delivers real-time dietary insights directly to its companion iOS and Android app without manual data entry.

---

## Key Innovation & Product Highlights

- **One-Tap AI Food Recognition:** Built-in optical camera and proprietary computer vision models automatically identify whole foods, plated meals, and multi-ingredient dishes.
- **Simultaneous Weight & Nutrient Calculation:** Instantly links precise gram-weight measurements with verified nutritional databases (USDA, CoFID) to calculate accurate macro- and micronutrient values.
- **Friction-Free Dietary Tracking:** Replaces barcode scanning and manual search with a single tap, dramatically increasing long-term habit adherence.
- **Companion Mobile Experience:** Seamless Bluetooth and Wi-Fi sync to iOS and Android companion apps with real-time goal tracking, macro balance alerts, and Apple Health integration.
- **Consumer Hardware Design:** Compact, minimalist footprint designed for modern kitchen countertops. Available in White and Black finishes.
